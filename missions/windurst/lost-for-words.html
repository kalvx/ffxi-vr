Windurst mission walkthrough

Lost for Words

There are reports that the Cat Burglar has her paws on a "Book of the Gods," a restricted book that was lost twenty years ago. Assist the Optistery in investigating the reports, and attempt to retrieve the priceless, but dangerous, tome.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC (one crystal is enough) to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
    • (Optional): As usual for Windurst Missions, the Gate Guards and their adjacent NPCs will all have optional dialogue after accepting and at end of the Mission.
  2. Head for the Optistery on the northern map of Windurst Waters (near Home Point #1). Speak to Tosuka-Porika at (G-8) (East door).
    • (Optional): The other NPCs and Tosuka-Porika inside the same room have additional dialogue.
  3. Head to (J-3) in Windurst Woods and speak to Nanaa Mihgo. She will lend you her [Key Item] Lapis monocle.
    • You may need to talk to her twice or more if you have a [Key Item] Windurst Trust permit and Aht Urhgan Lure of the Wildcat Quests started.
    • (Optional): Nanaa's cronies nearby have additional dialogue.
  4. Maze of Shakrami:
  5. Now make your way to the Maze of Shakhrami, which is in at (K-5) in the northeast corner of Tahrongi Canyon.
    • The Survival Guide places you at the entrance of Maze of Shakhrami so long as you have it unlocked.
    • The Unity Warp to Tahrongi Canyon, Kulshushu Outpost, or Mea crag warps can otherwise save some time.
  6. Inside, head for (G-6) on Map 1, where you can transition to Map 2 (the lower map). Then, head to (H-5) on Map 2.
  7. Check each Fossil Rock until you receive the [Key Item] Lapis coral.
    • There are about 12 Fossil Rocks on Map 2.
    • There are Fossil Rocks at (H-5),(I-7),(H-7),(G-7),(G-8),(I-8).
    • Many mobs aggro sound, while s will aggro to sight, so keep Sneak (Status) and Invisible (Status) up if you are a lower-level player. You can use Circumspection from the Grounds Tome if necessary.
  8. Make your way back to Nanaa Mihgo, who will give you the [Key Item] Hideout key.
    • (Optional): Nanaa's cronies have new additional dialogue.
  9. Inner Horutoto Ruins:
  10. Head to East Sarutabaruta, and enter the Lily Tower at (J-7) to reach Inner Horutoto Ruins.
    • The Inner Horutoto Ruins Survival Guide warp places you in the right tower if you have it unlocked already.
  11. Head to the Southwest room containing an Ancient Magical Gizmo. At (G-9), there is a Cracked Wall. Open it to proceed to Inner Horutoto Ruins Map 2. Now head for (G-8). There are a few ways to get there, but ultimately that is your destination.
    • You will need Sneak and Invisible due to high level (90+) monsters in the area.
    • Silent Oil and Prism Powder are alternatives to Sneak and Invisible.
    • This also includes potential blood aggro, so be sure you are above 75% HP.
    • Without Sneak and Invisible: Beetles and Bats do not aggro here. There is a path to the door without Goblins and Skeletons, allowing you to pass safely.
  12. Check the Mahogany Door for a cutscene.
    • (Optional): Nanaa and her cronies back in Windurst Woods have new additional dialogue after this cutscene.
  13. Windurst:
  14. Head to the House of the Hero at Windurst Walls (G-3). Check the door for a cutscene.
  15. Make your way back to Windurst Waters Home Point #1 and speak to Tosuka-Porika to complete the Mission.
    • (Optional): Some of the NPCs in the same room have new additional dialogue.
