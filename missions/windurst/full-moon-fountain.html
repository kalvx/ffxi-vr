Windurst mission walkthrough

Full Moon Fountain

At the request of the Orastery, you are to go to the southwestern magic tower of the Horutoto Ruins. Deep within you will find a laboratory, where you are ordered to switch on the magical gizmo.

Start NPCAny Windurst Gate Guard

RepeatableNo
PreviousThe Shadow Awaits

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade Crystals to a Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the mission. Accept it from any Gate Guard.
  2. Speak with Hakkuru-Rinkuru at the Orastery in Port Windurst to receive the Key Item "Southwestern star charm".
  3. Travel to West Sarutabaruta and enter the Dahlia Tower at (F-11) to reach Outer Horutoto Ruins.
  4. Navigate to Map 4 and interact with the Cracked Walls at (I-8) and (J-8).
  5. Examine the Gate to spawn four Jack Cardian NMs (magic-aggressive).
    • Mages should keep distance as they aggro magic.
    • They do not need to be defeated simultaneously.
  6. After defeating the Cardians, examine the door for a cutscene.
    • If you get a "mysterious force" message, keep trying.
  7. Enter Full Moon Fountain in Toraimarai Canal (Map 1) to complete the mission.
