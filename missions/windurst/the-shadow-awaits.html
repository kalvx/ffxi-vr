Windurst mission walkthrough

The Shadow Awaits

As written in the stars and interpreted by the Star Sibyl, you are to destined to journey to Castle Zvahl, which towers over Xarcabard, the end of the known lands. There, you are to witness what fate has in store for our world.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade 2+ Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard. You will receive [Key Item] Star crested summons.
  2. Head to the top of the Heavens Tower in Windurst Walls HP #1 and click the Door: Vestal Chamber.
    • If you're not using Trusts and are level 55-60, form a full party of players.
    • You will have to reach the Throne Room in Castle Zvahl Keep and clear the BCNM "The Shadow Lord Battle".
  3. Castle Zvahl Keep Home Point #1 is fastest way. If you don't have the HP unlocked already, check the instructions below.
  4. After you clear the BCNM, you'll get another cutscene. At the end, you'll be returned to the entrance of Castle Zvahl Baileys. You will also receive the [Key Item] Shadow fragment.
    • You can start the Zilart Missions and continue with Rhapsodies of Vanadiel Mission 1-12 from this point on, ranking up is optional.
  5. Zone into Heavens Tower for a brief cutscene.
  6. Click the Door: Vestal Chamber for a final cutscene and your reward: Rank 6 and 20,000 Gil.
    • Don't forget to speak to Apururu in Windurst Woods at (H-9) to learn Trust: Ajido-Marujido.
    • Also if you've obtained the trust permits (the Red and Blue) from the other 2 nations you can acquire Trion and Volker as trusts now by visiting their respective starting NPC's.
