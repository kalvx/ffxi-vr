Windurst mission walkthrough

The Horutoto Ruins Experiment

The minister of the Orastery in conducting an experiment in the eastern magic tower of the Horutoto Ruins, located in East Sarutabaruta. Inquire at the Orastery first, then go assist him.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to any Windurstian Gate Guard, and accept the Mission.
    • Mokyokyo - Windurst Waters (F-5)
    • Janshura-Rashura - Port Windurst (B-5)
    • Rakoh Buuma - Windurst Woods (K-10)
    • Zokima-Rokima - Windurst Walls (H-7)
  2. Head for the Orastery in Port Windurst. Speak to Hakkuru-Rinkuru at (E-7) for a cutscene.
  3. Make your way to (J-7) in East Sarutabaruta. You're looking for a large stone tower.
    • The Inner Horutoto Ruins Survival Guide places you at the right tower if you have it unlocked already.
  4. (Optional): Speak to Sama Gohjima near the entrance for some dialogue.
  5. Head down the stairs to the Inner Horutoto Ruins.
  6. Inside, you'll be in the Lily Tower on Map 1. Go down the stairs in to the main room.
  7. Run to the other side, and look for the Cracked Wall, around (H-9). Pass through it and go left.
  8. Check the Gate: Magical Gizmo at (I-9) for a cutscene with the Minister.
  9. Now, head back out and check the six different Ancient Magical Gizmos until you are given the [Key Item] Cracked Mana Orb. It will be at a random Gizmo.
  10. Head back to Hakkuru-Rinkuru and speak with him to finish the Mission.
