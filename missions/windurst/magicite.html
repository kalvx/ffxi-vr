Windurst mission walkthrough

Magicite

You must obtain three magicite items from different dungeons to prevent the Shadow Lord's resurrection.

Start NPCPakh Jatalfih
Starting zoneRu'Lude Gardens
CoordinatesI-9
RepeatableNo

NextThe Final Seal

  • [Key Item] Tenshodo membership card

Rank 5

Complete walkthrough

  1. Visit the Windurstian Embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens and speak with Pakh Jatalfih (I-9) for a cutscene.
  2. Go to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens (H-6) for another cutscene.
  3. Speak with Aldo in Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno.
    • Requires Tenshodo membership (from the "Tenshodo Membership" quest in Lower Jeuno).
  4. Obtain the three magicites from separate dungeons:
    • Magicite: Optistone — Monastic Cavern in Davoi
    • Magicite: Orastone — Altar Room in Castle Oztroja
    • Magicite: Aurastone — Qulun Dome in Beadeaux
    • Each dungeon requires specific key items to access the magicite rooms.
  5. After collecting all three, return to the Audience Chamber (H-5) in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene.
  6. Speak with Pakh Jatalfih to complete the mission.

Notes

This mission is identical across all three nations. Prerequisite quests for dungeon access do not need to be repeated if completed on a different nation.
