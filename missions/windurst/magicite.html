Windurst mission walkthrough

Magicite

You must obtain three magicite items from Davoi, Castle Oztroja, and Beadeaux to prevent the Shadow Lord's resurrection.

Start NPCPakh Jatalfih
Starting zoneRu'Lude Gardens
CoordinatesI-9, Windurstian Embassy
RepeatableNo
PreviousA New Journey
NextThe Final Seal

Required items and key items

  • Rank 4 and enough Rank Points to flag Magicite.
  • [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card — needed to enter Tenshodo HQ and reach Aldo.
  • If you do not already have the membership card, complete Tenshodo Membership in Lower Jeuno. The normal route requires Jeuno Fame 3.
  • Need fame? Use the Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame Guide for fame checks, repeatable quests, and item-trade methods.
  • Additional key items are obtained during the mission for the Davoi, Castle Oztroja, and Beadeaux magicite routes.

Rewards

Rank 5
10,000 Gil
Airship Pass (or 20,000 Gil if already owned)

Before you start

Magicite does not begin from a Windurst Gate Guard like a normal nation mission. After completing A New Journey and reaching Rank 4, make sure your Rank bar is high enough. If necessary, trade crystals to a Conquest Overseer. You can do this in Jeuno; you do not need to return to Windurst just to raise the Rank bar. Once eligible, return to the Windurstian Embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens and speak with Pakh Jatalfih.

Before heading to Aldo: Tenshodo HQ requires the Tenshodo Member's Card. If you do not have it, follow Tenshodo Membership. If Ghebi Damomohe will not offer the quest, raise Jeuno fame first using the Jeuno Fame Guide.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Flag Magicite at the Windurstian Embassy. In Ru'Lude Gardens, go to the Windurstian Embassy at (I-9) and speak with Pakh Jatalfih. If he does not advance the mission, first check that A New Journey is complete, you are Rank 4, and your Rank Points are high enough.
  2. Visit the Audience Chamber. Go to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for the Jeuno story cutscene and mission authorization.
  3. Make sure you can enter Tenshodo HQ. Travel to Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno. Access to the Tenshodo headquarters requires the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card.
    • If you already have the card, continue inside and speak with Aldo.
    • If you do not have it, follow the full Tenshodo Membership walkthrough.
    • The normal membership route requires Jeuno Fame 3. If the quest is not available, use the Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame Guide to check and raise the correct reputation.
  4. Speak with Aldo. Enter Tenshodo HQ through Neptune's Spire and speak with Aldo. Continue the dialogue/cutscenes and collect the mission key items he provides before leaving. Do not head straight to the three beastmen strongholds without completing this step.
  5. Collect all three magicites. The three objectives can be completed in any convenient order:
    • Davoi / Monastic Cavern: obtain the access key items required for the deeper Davoi area, then obtain Magicite: Optistone.
    • Castle Oztroja / Altar Room: complete the Castle Oztroja access route and obtain Magicite: Orastone.
    • Beadeaux / Qulun Dome: complete the Beadeaux access route and obtain Magicite: Aurastone.
    • Each stronghold has its own access steps. Do not assume reaching the zone entrance is enough; complete the required key-item/door sequence for that stronghold before attempting the magicite.
  6. Return to the Audience Chamber. After you possess all three magicites, return to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for the completion cutscene.
  7. Finish with the Windurstian Embassy. Return to Pakh Jatalfih at the Windurstian Embassy and complete the final mission dialogue. You should receive Rank 5, 10,000 Gil, and the Airship Pass (or the alternate gil reward if you already possess the pass).

Tenshodo membership and fame

Jeuno fame is a prerequisite for the normal Tenshodo Membership route, not a separate Magicite mission requirement. You need Jeuno Fame 3 to flag the normal membership quest with Ghebi Damomohe. Once you possess the Tenshodo Member's Card, you can access Tenshodo HQ and continue Magicite with Aldo.

Notes

The three-nation Magicite storyline shares the same major Jeuno and beastmen-stronghold objectives, although the nation-specific mission flagging and embassy dialogue differ. Prerequisite dungeon-access key items do not need to be reacquired if your character already possesses them.
