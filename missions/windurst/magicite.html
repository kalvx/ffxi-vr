Windurst mission walkthrough

Magicite

You must obtain three magicite items from Davoi, Castle Oztroja, and Beadeaux to prevent the Shadow Lord's resurrection.

Start NPCPakh Jatalfih
Starting zoneRu'Lude Gardens
CoordinatesI-9, Windurstian Embassy
RepeatableNo
PreviousA New Journey
NextThe Final Seal

Required items and key items

  • Rank 4 and enough Rank Points to flag Magicite.
  • [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card — needed later to enter Tenshodo HQ and reach Aldo.
  • Additional key items are obtained during the mission for the Davoi, Castle Oztroja, and Beadeaux magicite routes.

Rewards

Rank 5
10,000 Gil
Airship Pass (or 20,000 Gil if already owned)

Before you start

Magicite does not begin from a Windurst Gate Guard like a normal nation mission. After completing A New Journey and reaching Rank 4, make sure your Rank bar is high enough. If necessary, trade crystals to a Conquest Overseer. You can do this in Jeuno; you do not need to return to Windurst just to raise the Rank bar.

IMPORTANT — CHECK TENSHODO ACCESS BEFORE STARTING: Magicite will later send you to Aldo inside Tenshodo HQ. This is a prerequisite check, not Step 1 of the mission itself. If you already possess the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card, you are ready. If you do not, complete the Tenshodo Membership walkthrough. The normal membership route requires Jeuno Fame 3; if you need to raise it, use the Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame Guide for fame checks, repeatable quests, and item trades. Get the Member's Card now so the mission does not stop when you reach Aldo.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Flag Magicite at the Windurstian Embassy. In Ru'Lude Gardens, go to the Windurstian Embassy at (I-9) and speak with Pakh Jatalfih. If he does not advance the mission, check that A New Journey is complete, you are Rank 4, and your Rank Points are high enough.
  2. Visit the Audience Chamber. Go to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for the Jeuno story cutscene and mission authorization.
  3. Enter Tenshodo HQ and speak with Aldo. Travel to Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and enter Tenshodo HQ using your Tenshodo Member's Card. Speak with Aldo, continue the dialogue/cutscenes, and collect the mission key items he provides before leaving. Do not head to the beastmen strongholds before completing this step.
  4. Collect all three magicites. The three objectives can be completed in any convenient order:
    • Davoi / Monastic Cavern: obtain the access key items required for the deeper Davoi area, then obtain Magicite: Optistone.
    • Castle Oztroja / Altar Room: complete the Castle Oztroja access route and obtain Magicite: Orastone.
    • Beadeaux / Qulun Dome: complete the Beadeaux access route and obtain Magicite: Aurastone.
    • Each stronghold has its own access requirements. Reaching the zone entrance alone is not enough.
  5. Return to the Audience Chamber. After you possess all three magicites, return to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for the completion cutscene.
  6. Finish with the Windurstian Embassy. Return to Pakh Jatalfih at the Windurstian Embassy and complete the final mission dialogue. You receive Rank 5, 10,000 Gil, and the Airship Pass (or the alternate gil reward if you already possess the pass).

Tenshodo membership and fame

Jeuno fame is a prerequisite for the normal Tenshodo Membership route, not a separate Magicite mission requirement. Once you possess the Tenshodo Member's Card, you can access Tenshodo HQ and continue Magicite with Aldo.

Notes

The three-nation Magicite storyline shares the same major Jeuno and beastmen-stronghold objectives, although the nation-specific mission flagging and embassy dialogue differ. Prerequisite dungeon-access key items do not need to be reacquired if your character already possesses them.
