Windurst mission walkthrough

Magicite

You must obtain three magicite items from Davoi, Castle Oztroja, and Beadeaux to prevent the Shadow Lord's resurrection.

Start NPCPakh Jatalfih
Starting zoneRu'Lude Gardens
CoordinatesI-9, Windurstian Embassy
RepeatableNo
PreviousA New Journey
NextThe Final Seal

Required items and key items

  • Rank 4 and enough Rank Points to flag Magicite.
  • [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card — needed later to enter Tenshodo HQ and reach Aldo.
  • Additional key items are obtained during the mission for the Davoi, Castle Oztroja, and Beadeaux magicite routes.

Rewards

Rank 5
10,000 Gil
Airship Pass (or 20,000 Gil if already owned)

Before you start

Magicite does not begin from a Windurst Gate Guard like a normal nation mission. After completing A New Journey and reaching Rank 4, make sure your Rank bar is high enough. If necessary, trade crystals to a Conquest Overseer. You can do this in Jeuno; you do not need to return to Windurst just to raise the Rank bar.

IMPORTANT — CHECK TENSHODO ACCESS BEFORE STARTING: Magicite will later send you to Aldo inside Tenshodo HQ. This is a prerequisite check, not Step 1 of the mission itself. If you already possess the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card, you are ready. If you do not, complete the Tenshodo Membership walkthrough. The normal membership route requires Jeuno Fame 3; if you need to raise it, use the Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame Guide for fame checks, repeatable quests, and item trades. Get the Member's Card now so the mission does not stop when you reach Aldo.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Flag Magicite at the Windurstian Embassy. In Ru'Lude Gardens, go to the Windurstian Embassy at (I-9) and speak with Pakh Jatalfih. If he does not advance the mission, check that A New Journey is complete, you are Rank 4, and your Rank Points are high enough.
  2. Visit the Audience Chamber. Go to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for the Jeuno story cutscene and mission authorization.
  3. Enter Tenshodo HQ and speak with Aldo. Travel to Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and enter Tenshodo HQ using your Tenshodo Member's Card. Speak with Aldo, continue the dialogue/cutscenes, and collect the mission key items he provides before leaving. Do not head to the beastmen strongholds before completing this step.
  4. Collect all three magicites. The three strongholds can be completed in any order. The routes below include the access item and the path from the stronghold entrance to the magicite.

    4A — Davoi / Monastic Cavern — Magicite: Optistone

    • Before leaving Jeuno: obtain the Crest of Davoi. Speak with Baudin in Upper Jeuno (G-8) and trade him Coeurl Meat.
    • Enter Davoi from Jugner Forest (G-12). Invisible is useful against sight-aggressive Orcs; Sneak can help with sound-aggressive enemies.
    • From the Davoi entrance, make your way to (G-7). Two Orcs commonly stand near the objective, so clear them or use Invisible before approaching.
    • Drop Invisible before interacting. Examine the Wall of Dark Arts at (G-7) and pass through into Monastic Cavern.
    • Monastic Cavern is safe for this objective. Follow the passage into the large chamber and examine the Magicite to receive Magicite: Optistone.

    4B — Castle Oztroja / Altar Room — Magicite: Orastone

    • Before entering Oztroja: obtain the Yagudo Torch. Speak with Paya-Sabya in Upper Jeuno (I-8), then speak with Muckvix in Lower Jeuno (H-9). Make sure you actually receive the torch before making the long trip.
    • Bring Silent Oils/Sneak and Prism Powders/Invisible. Enter Castle Oztroja from Meriphataud Mountains (L-8).
    • On the first section, head to the Brass Door at (I-8). There are two levers: one opens the door and the other opens the trap floor. Stand back after pulling a lever; if the trap opens, wait for it to close and use the other lever.
    • Continue upstairs and head toward (G-7) to exit into the open courtyard section. Stay on the upper path and head north to the hallway entrance around (I-7).
    • Once inside that section, follow the right wall until you reach the sealed door at approximately (H-9). The Yagudo here are stronger, so keep Invisible up until you need to interact.
    • At the door, use/light the Yagudo Torch to pass through. Head south, turn right, and open the next Brass Door. The zone line into the Altar Room is just beyond it.
    • The Altar Room itself has no normal enemies. Examine the Magicite to receive Magicite: Orastone. If it only says the magicite glows ominously, your Yagudo Torch prerequisite/cutscene was not completed correctly; return to the Jeuno torch step before trying again.

    4C — Beadeaux / Qulun Dome — Magicite: Aurastone

    • Before the final door: obtain both Coruscant Rosary and Black Matinee Necklace from Sattal-Mansal in Lower Jeuno (J-8) by completing Mysteries of Beadeaux I and Mysteries of Beadeaux II. The required NM drops are the Quadav Charm and Quadav Augury Shell; trade the two items to Sattal-Mansal separately.
    • If you still need those drops, enter Beadeaux from Pashhow Marshlands (L-11). Beadeaux enemies primarily aggro by sound, so keep Sneak active. Mages should carry Echo Drops.
    • For the prerequisite NM route, follow the left wall to the tunnel at (H-7). Use the glowing Mute devices before passing nearby Afflictors; the Mute silences you and prevents the Afflictor from inflicting the severe Curse effect.
    • The two prerequisite NMs are De'Vyu Headhunter around (H-9/H-10) for the Quadav Charm, and Go'Bhu Gascon around (F-6) for the Quadav Augury Shell. Return to Sattal-Mansal after obtaining both drops and receive the two key items.
    • For the magicite itself, return to Beadeaux. From the entrance, hug the left wall to the tunnel at (H-7). Follow the tunnel to the intersection and take the left path to zone into Qulun Dome.
    • Qulun Dome has no normal monsters in the magicite room. Examine the sealed Door; your two Beadeaux key items allow passage. Inside, examine the Magicite to receive Magicite: Aurastone.
    CHECK BEFORE LEAVING: Your key-item list should show Magicite: Optistone, Magicite: Orastone, and Magicite: Aurastone. Do not return to the Audience Chamber until all three are present.
  5. Return to the Audience Chamber. After you possess all three magicites, return to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for the completion cutscene.
  6. Finish with the Windurstian Embassy. Return to Pakh Jatalfih at the Windurstian Embassy and complete the final mission dialogue. You receive Rank 5, 10,000 Gil, and the Airship Pass (or the alternate gil reward if you already possess the pass).

Tenshodo membership and fame

Jeuno fame is a prerequisite for the normal Tenshodo Membership route, not a separate Magicite mission requirement. Once you possess the Tenshodo Member's Card, you can access Tenshodo HQ and continue Magicite with Aldo.

Notes

The three-nation Magicite storyline shares the same major Jeuno and beastmen-stronghold objectives, although the nation-specific mission flagging and embassy dialogue differ. Prerequisite dungeon-access key items do not need to be reacquired if your character already possesses them.
