Windurst mission walkthrough

The Heart of the Matter

Assist the Manustery in the creation of Cardians. You must go to the southeastern magic tower of the Horutoto Ruins in East Sarutabaruta, and energize the Mana Orbs that serve as the hearts of the Cardians. Inquire at the Manustery first for details.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Accept the Mission from any Gate Guard.
    • Mokyokyo - Windurst Waters (F-5)
    • Janshura-Rashura - Port Windurst (B-5)
    • Rakoh Buuma - Windurst Woods (K-10)
    • Zokima-Rokima - Windurst Walls (H-7)
  2. Then make your way to the Manustery in Windurst Woods, Speak to Apururu who is at (H-9).
    • You may need to talk to her twice if you have a [Key Item] Windurst Trust permit as she will mention talking to her brother, Ajido-Marujido.
  3. You will receive the following Mana Orbs Key Items:
    • [Key Item] First dark Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Second dark Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Third dark Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Fourth dark Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Fifth dark Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Sixth dark Mana Orb
  4. You are now instructed to go to the Outer Horutoto Ruins.
  5. Head to (J-11) in East Sarutabaruta and speak to Pore-Ohre at the entrance to Marguerite Tower, they will give you the [Key Item] Southeastern star charm.
    • Make sure you get this, or you will be unable to energize the orbs later on.
  6. Check the six pedestals to place a Mana Orb.
    • Examine the Cracked Walls along the north and south sides to find hidden rooms, which contain 2 more pedestals for your Mana Orbs.
  7. After all orbs have been set, pass through the Cracked Wall on the east side and then examine the Gate: Magical Gizmo behind it for a cutscene.
  8. After the cutscene, check each pedestal again to retrieve the 6 Glowing Mana Orb Key Items:
    • [Key Item] First glowing Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Second glowing Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Third glowing Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Fourth glowing Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Fifth glowing Mana Orb
    • [Key Item] Sixth glowing Mana Orb
  9. When you've collected all 6, head back out of the tower. When you zone, you'll receive another cutscene.
    • (Optional): You can speak to Quh Berhuja nearby for some dialogue.
    • Alternatively, you can use any means of leaving the zone that will not lead you to East Sarutabaruta. Doing this will allow you to skip the cutscene and go straight back to Apururu will which lead to a slightly different cutscene upon finishing the Mission.
  10. Head back to the Manustery and speak to Apururu to complete the Mission.
