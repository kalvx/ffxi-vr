Windurst mission walkthrough

A New Journey

As requested by the Star Sibyl, you are to render service at the Embassy of Windurst in Jeuno. Great responsibility and tact are required.

Start NPCAny Windurst Gate Guard
Starting zoneHeavens Tower
RepeatableNo
PreviousWritten in the Stars
NextMagicite

Required items and key items

You will receive the Letter to the ambassador key item during the mission. To use the Delkfutt shortcut you need either the physical Delkfutt Key or the permanent Delkfutt key key item. If you have neither, Porphyrion on the 10th floor provides the key needed to reach the basement.

Rewards

Rank 4
5,000 Gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to a Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar enough to make A New Journey available, then accept it from a Windurst Gate Guard.
  2. Go to the top of Heavens Tower and examine the Vestal Chamber door. Watch the Star Sibyl cutscene and obtain the Letter to the ambassador.
  3. Travel to Ru'Lude Gardens and speak with Pakh Jatalfih at the Windurstian Embassy (I-9). Make sure you receive his mission cutscene before going to Delkfutt's Tower.
  4. Travel to Lower Delkfutt's Tower. A Survival Guide teleport is the quickest route if you have it unlocked.
  5. If you already have a Delkfutt Key: stay on Lower Delkfutt's Tower Map 1 / first floor. You do not need to climb the tower. Go to (E-8) and find the Cermet Door that provides access to the basement.
    • If you have the physical Delkfutt Key, use/trade the key as required at the Cermet Door.
    • If you have the permanent Delkfutt key key item, no physical key trade is needed.
    • The lettered map markers are not instructions to go downstairs. The important destination is the Cermet Door at E-8 on Map 1.
    • Another player opening the door does not satisfy your own key requirement.
  6. After passing the E-8 Cermet Door, you are in the Lower Delkfutt's Tower Basement. Head east through the large room and make your way to (L-7).
  7. At (L-7), examine the Cermet Door. This is the mission door and should trigger the required cutscene. If you do not receive a cutscene, verify that you are at the L-7 basement door and that you completed the Windurst Embassy cutscene with Pakh Jatalfih.
  8. After the L-7 cutscene, return to Ru'Lude Gardens (I-9) and enter the Windurstian Embassy. Speak with Pakh Jatalfih so he acknowledges your return.
  9. Examine Door: Windurstian Emb. for the completion cutscene. It is the third door inside the embassy building; several doors have the same name. If the correct final door first gives you a locked-door message instead of the cutscene, click/examine that same door again.
  10. After the completion cutscene, A New Journey is complete. You receive Rank 4 and 5,000 gil, and the next mission is Magicite.

If you do not have a Delkfutt Key

You must obtain one before using the basement shortcut. The normal route is to climb Delkfutt's Tower to the 10th floor, defeat Porphyrion for a Delkfutt Key, use the elevator beside Porphyrion's room, then descend the long staircase to the basement. You do not have to be on this mission to obtain the key from Porphyrion.

Once you reach the basement, resume the walkthrough at the L-7 Cermet Door step above.

Current Reality notes

  • The two important Delkfutt doors are different: E-8 on Lower Delkfutt Map 1 gets you into the basement; L-7 in the basement triggers the mission cutscene.
  • Do not try to use the Delkfutt Key on a Windurst Embassy door in Jeuno. The key is for Delkfutt's Tower.
  • At the final Windurst Embassy door, Current Reality may display the locked-door response on the first interaction. If that happens after completing the L-7 cutscene and speaking with Pakh Jatalfih, examine the same final door a second time.
