San d’Oria mission walkthrough

Infiltrate Davoi

The scout in Davoi needs the help of an adventurer.

Start NPCAny San d'Oria Gate Guard
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
RepeatableYes
PreviousJourney Abroad
NextThe Crystal Spring

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. This Mission is Required.
    • Trade enough Crystals (1+) to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. Speak to Prince Trion who can be reached by clicking on the Door: Prince Royal's Rm at (G/H-7) of Chateau d'Oraguille for a cutscene. It is the room on the North side of the court yard.
  3. Zone into Davoi for a cutscene.
    • Invisible is recommended for this Mission, bring Prism Powders or have a spell for Invisiblity.
    • Follow the right wall of Jugner Forest to the zone into Davoi at (G-12).
    • A map of Davoi can be purchased for 3,000 Gil from Elesca at I-8 of Northern San d'Oria or Violitte at G-10 of Southern San d'Oria.
  4. Speak with Quemaricond, patrolling around (H-7), when it is safe to drop Invisibility. You will obtain [Key Item]Royal Knights' Davoi report.
  5. Return to Prince Trion to complete the Mission.
