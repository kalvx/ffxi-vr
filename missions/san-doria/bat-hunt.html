San d’Oria mission walkthrough

Bat Hunt

Mission Orders: Exterminate bats from King Ranperre's Tomb, and bring back proof of your deeds.

Start NPCAny San d'Oria Gate Guard
Starting zoneChateau d'Oraguille
RepeatableYes
PreviousSmash the Orcish Scouts
NextSave the Children
TitleNone

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to any San d'Orian Gate Guard, and select "Bat Hunt" to begin this Mission.
  2. Go to (H-11) in East Ronfaure to zone into King Ranperre's Tomb.
    • If you do not have a map for King Ranperre's Tomb, one may be purchased from Violitte – Southern San d'Oria (G-10) or Elesca – Northern San d'Oria (I-8).
  3. Kill Ding Bats in King Ranperre's Tomb (which spawn at night from 18:00 to 6:00) until you receive Orcish Mail Scales.
    • This can be done before or after clicking the Tombstone.
    • There are 2 Bats at the entrance and more around the Tombstone.
  4. Touch the Tombstone at (H/I-10) for a cutscene.
    • There is a ghost that aggros by it at night if low level. Can just click the Tombstone to lose aggro or use sneak.
  5. Return to a Gate Guard and trade them the Orcish Mail Scales to complete the Mission.
  6. Repeating:
  7. To complete this Mission multiple times for more Ranking Points:
  8. The Gate Guard will ask for a Bat Fang which can be bought from the Auction House ( ➞ Materials ➞ Bonecraft) or farmed from Mouse Bats in the underground section of King Ranperre's Tomb.
  9. Trade the Bat Fang to the Gate Guard to complete the Mission.
    • It is not necessary to zone between multiple trades.
