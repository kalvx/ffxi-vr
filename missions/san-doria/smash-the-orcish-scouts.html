San d’Oria mission walkthrough

Smash the Orcish Scouts

Mission Orders: Hunt Orcs lurking outside San d'Oria and bring back one of their axes.

Start NPCAny San d'Oria Gate Guard
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesK-10
RepeatableYes
NextBat Hunt
TitleNone

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to any San d'Orian Gate Guard to begin this Mission.
    • Ambrotien - Southern San d'Oria (K-10)
    • Endracion - Southern San d'Oria (F-9)
    • Grilau - Northern San d'Oria (D-8)
  2. Go outside the city and kill Orcish Fodder until you receive an Orcish Axe.
    • Orcish Fodder can be found in East Ronfaure and West Ronfaure.
  3. After you receive an Orcish Axe return to the Gate Guard and trade them the Orcish Axe to finish the Mission.
