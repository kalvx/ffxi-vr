San d’Oria mission walkthrough

The Ruins of Fei'Yin

This mission involves traveling to Fei'Yin, located on the Beaucedine Glacier. Visit Chateau d'Oraguille to learn more.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Head to Chateau d'Oraguille in Northern San d'Oria via HP #2 as the fastest route.
  2. Zone into Chateau d'Oraguille for a cutscene that includes King Destin.
  3. Visit any Gate Guard and accept the Mission.
  4. Return to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak to Halver to receive [Key Item] New Fei'Yin seal.
    • If you are low level, you might want to use Trusts or form a party of lvl 50+ players.
  5. Go to Fei'Yin (located in the northeast corner of Beaucedine Glacier) for a cutscene with Zeid. You will have to reach the Qu'Bia Arena and clear the BCNM "The Rank 5 Mission".
    • The majority of enemies in Fei'Yin detect by sound, however there are several that detect spellcasting.
    • Quick Travel options:
    • Home Point #1 to Fei'Yin is the fastest way, it puts you right at the zone to Qu'Bia Arena.
    • Unity Warp (Level 128) will place you at the entrance of Fei'Yin.
    • Unity Warp (Level 125) will place you in the central area of Beaucedine Glacier.
    • Domenic will teleport characters to Qu'Bia Arena for 1,000 gil (upon completion of Beyond Infinity). Just zone out for the cutscene (and the Home Point), then zone back in.
    • From the entrance of Fei'Yin:
    • Go east to (K-8). There are 2 Dolls in front of a door named Cermet Portal, open it and you'll be able to enter Qu'Bia Arena.
    • You will be able to prepare outside of the BC, however all buffs will wear upon entering. Plan accordingly and click the Burning Circle when ready to take on The Rank 5 Mission.
  6. You only need to kill the Archlich to win.
  7. You will be facing Archlich Taber'quoan. The Archlich will spawn with 2 Ancient Sorcerers. Ancient Warriors will also spawn in the hallway and run to help of the Archlich during the fight.
    • The Archlich is a BLM, and will cast spells like Sleepga II and Freeze, he may also use Manafont.
    • The lesser skeletons have very little HP and can be easily taken out by an AoE spell.
    • Focus attacks on the Archlich, a Paladin with Invincible is useful for managing the many extra mobs. A Monk using Hundred Fists also shines in this fight, due to the skeleton blunt penalty.
  8. When you win, you'll be given another cutscene and receive [Key Item] Burnt seal.
  9. Return to Halver to complete the Mission.
