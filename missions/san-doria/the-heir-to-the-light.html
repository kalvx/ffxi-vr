San d’Oria mission walkthrough

The Heir to the Light

The Rites of Succession will be held at the cathedral in Northern San d'Oria. You are to report there for guard duty.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. Zone into Northern San d'Oria for a lengthy cutscene.
    • If you accepted the Mission from the Northern San d'Oria gate guard, you can talk to Arnau to see they are still preparing the Rites.
    • You will be prohibited from zoning into Chateau d'Oraguille due to increased security until you've triggered the cutscene in Northern San d'Oria.
  3. Zone into Chateau d'Oraguille for a cutscene.
  4. Head to Fei'Yin for a cutscene.
  5. Make your way to Qu'Bia Arena for a two-part BCNM fight, "The Heir to the Light".
    • Use Fei'Yin Home Point #1 or Domenic to teleport right outside the arena, then walk a short distance to zone into the small room with the Burning Circle.
  6. Part One will have Death Clan Destroyer (WHM Warmachine), Yukvok of Clan Death (Unsleepable Orc RNG), 3 Worgbut of Clan Death, 3 Rallbrog of Clan Death, and 3 Vangknok of Clan Death (Orcs, Sleepable).
    • Open with Elemental Seal+Sleepga II or Horde Lullaby, and start attacking the Ranger mob first. Make sure you silence the Warmachine, as it is a WHM who can Curaga and wake everything up. Defeat the Ranger, followed by the Warmachine, and then take our the rest.
    • Sleep the last mob before killing it, and give yourself a rest. There will be no time after the first part of the fight to rest.
  7. Part Two will include Prince Trion fighting by your side, against 3 Orc NMs, Warlord Rojgnoj (PLD unsleepable), Rojgnoj's Left Hand (BLM), and Rojgnoj's Right Hand (DRK).
    • Trion will randomly attack a mob, and should be helped. Preferably, the order to kill the NMs is BLM > PLD > DRK. Trion can be cured and can die in the BC. Should he die, you will be ejected from the BC. Despite being a Paladin, he isn't great at keeping himself alive.
    • Keep the other mobs silenced, a Paladin with Invincible is useful here for keeping attention.
  8. Upon clearing the battlefield, zone into Northern San d'Oria for a cutscene.
  9. Head to the Chateau and check the Great Hall door for another cutscene.
  10. Make your way to (H-8) in King Ranperre's Tomb and check the Heavy Stone Door -- the same from a previous mission -- for yet another cutscene.
  11. Go back to the Chateau and speak to Halver and you will receive your rewards.
    • (Optional): Exit to Northern San d'Oria and proceed to the Papal Chamber door.
  12. Zone into Southern San d'Oria for a final epilogue cutscene!
