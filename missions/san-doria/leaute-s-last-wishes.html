San d’Oria mission walkthrough

Leaute's Last Wishes

This mission involves the late Queen Leaute. Visit Monarlais Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille to learn more.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. Head to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak with Halver.
  3. Select the Great Hall door for a cutscene.
    • Make sure to get the cutscene where the King asks you to retrieve a Dreamrose.
  4. Speak with Halver again.
  5. Go to (G-7) in Western Altepa Desert. The Dreamrose is butted up against Revelation Rock, in the southern section of the oasis (look for the body of water on your map).
    • The Unity Warp (Level 125) will bring you close.
    • Alternatively, you could use the Survival Guide.
  6. Check the Dreamrose to spawn Sabotender Enamorado.
    • If you're low level, it is recommended that you bring enough people, pets and/or Trusts to reduce the damage done by 1000 Needles. It is possible to do it with less, but spreading the damage out will help any HP concerns.
  7. Check the Dreamrose after defeating the NM to receive the [Key Item] Dreamrose.
  8. Return to Halver.
  9. Go toward the Garden at (F-8) for another cutscene. At the end of the cutscene, you'll receive the [Key Item] Piece of paper.
