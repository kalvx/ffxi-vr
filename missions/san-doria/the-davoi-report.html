San d’Oria mission walkthrough

The Davoi Report

Retrieve the latest scouting report from the Temple Knight scout somewhere in Davoi.

Start NPCAny San d'Oria Gate Guard

CoordinatesK-6

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. If you wish to complete this Mission, trade enough Crystals to a Conquest Overseer to raise your Rank bar and unlock it.
  2. Talk to any Gate Guard and accept the Mission.
    • There are two in Southern San d'Oria, Ambrotien at (K-6) and Endracion at (F-9). There is also a gate guard in Northern San d'Oria, Grilau at (D-8).
    • A map of Davoi can be purchased for 3000 Gil from Elesca at (I-8) of North San d'Oria or Violitte at (G-10) of Southern San d'Oria, but it is not required.
  3. Make your way to Davoi. To reach Davoi, zone into Jugner Forest from La Theine Plateau at (M-8). Then follow the right wall of Jugner Forest to the zone into Davoi at (G-12).
  4. Talk to the NPC Zantaviat just inside the zone.
  5. Walk south until you reach a pond at (J-8). On the South bank of the pond you will find a ! targetable location. Click on it to receive the [Key Item] Lost document.
  6. Return to Zantaviat to recieve the [Key Item] Temple Knights' Davoi report.
  7. Return to a Gate Guard.
  8. Examine the Door: Papal Chambers, which is on the top floor of the Cathedral in Northern San d'Oria.
  9. After the cutscene, the Mission will be completed.
