Journey to Windurst

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals (3+) to a Conquest Overseer if you wish to skip Mission 2-2 and immediately unlock this one, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. You will be instructed to speak with Halver. He is located at (I-9) just inside the door of Chateau d'Oraguille, entered from Northern San d'Oria.
  3. Halver will instruct you to visit two other Nations: Bastok and Windurst.
    • After reaching Rhapsodies of Vanadiel Mission 1-7 you will be given a Cipher: Halver if you do not already possess this Trust.
  4. The next leg of this Mission can be done in two different ways:
    • Go to Bastok first and then to Windurst.
    • Go to Windurst first and then Bastok.
  5. If you have never visited Bastok or Windurst, you can "Unity warp" to either South Gustaberg or East Sarutabaruta, then make the rest of the trek on foot. Speak to Urbiolaine in Southern San d'Oria (G-10) and choose "Bring me to a Wanted encounter area", then "Content level 99 and below."
  6. The Bastok Path may be quicker due to the ease of obtaining the item involved.
    • Bastok will require obtaining a Mine Gravel, which can be bought off the Auction House.
    • Windurst will require obtaining two Parana Shields, but these are 100% drops and can be obtained in 5 minutes apart.
  7. The Windurst Path final BCNM may be a bit easier as the Petrify AoE will not be used.

Notes

1. Windurst Dragon was easily solo'ed on a level 25 RDM with 4 trusts, though I did sleep the dragon. The sleep landed first try. The fight can go badly if you don't land the sleep quickly. 2. Easily solo'd at 30BLM with four Trusts. 3. The BCNM has been solo'd by a Mnk/War, Pld/War, or Drg/Whm with meds and skill. 4. Can also be solo'd by a Thf/Nin with capped Marksmanship, Sleep Bolts, Bloody Bolts, and a few meds. 5. Easily solo'd as a 99COR.
