Ranperre's Final Rest

Prince Trion seeks an adventurer. Find the prince in his chamber to learn more.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. You'll be instructed to go see Prince Trion. Visit him in his room at (H-7) in Chateau d'Oraguille
  3. If you're low level, gather a party or use Trusts.
  4. Head for (H-8) in King Ranperre's Tomb on Map 1.
  5. When ready, check the Heavy Stone Door to spawn three (3) Skeleton NMs: Corrupted Soffeil (BLM), Corrupted Yorgos (WAR) and Corrupted Ulbrig (BLM).
    • They can be pulled one by one using Sneak.
    • All 3 must be slain.
    • The NMs are immune to Sleep and Lullaby.
    • They will use Blood Saber, so keep damage negating buffs up to reduce the HP regained.
    • Corrupted Yorgos has approximately 7250 HP.
  6. When defeated, check the Heavy Stone Door again, and enter. Check the Tombstone inside for a cutscene, at the end of which, you'll receive the [Key Item] Ancient San d'Orian book.
    • (Optional): Return to Prince Trion, and you will be instructed to go see the Gate Guard.
  7. Talk to any Gate Guard. A cutscene will play out saying they sent the book to scholars to decipher.
  8. You must talk to the Gate Guard once more to have them tell you that the deciphering will take some time.
  9. Zone and talk to the Guard again who will tell you to see Prince Trion.
  10. Return to Prince Trion and speak with him for a cutscene. You may need to talk to him twice.
  11. Return to the Heavy Stone Door in King Ranperre's Tomb again for another cutscene. You'll be instructed to defeat enemies, but you only need to activate the door again.
  12. Return to a Gate Guard to complete the Mission.
