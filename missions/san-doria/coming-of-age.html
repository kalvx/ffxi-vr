San d’Oria mission walkthrough

Coming of Age

Princess Claidie will soon celebrate her coming-of-age, and the court in d'Oraguille needs help with the ceremonies. Speak to Monarlais Halver and offer your assistance.

Start NPCAny San d'Oria Gate Guard
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
RepeatableNo
PreviousThe Secret Weapon
NextLightbringer
TitleNone

Required items and key items

Rewards

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. Go to Chateau d'Oraguille, where you'll receive a cutscene upon entering.
  3. Speak to Halver for instructions.
    • (Optional): Interact with the two doors to the Princes' quarters for short cutscenes.
  4. Travel to the Quicksand Caves Map 2.
    • The cave entrance at Eastern Altepa Desert (H-10) will place you on Map 2.
    • Located just east of the Survival Guide warp to Eastern Altepa Desert.
    • Alternatively, using the Unity Warp (Level 125) for Quicksand Caves will place you at (J-6) on Map 1. You will need to walk south and then west to (F-10) to reach Map 2.
  5. You will need to drop down a hole at (E-11) to reach an isolated portion of the map.
  6. Once you drop down, travel to the Fountain of Kings at (G-14).
  7. Clear the area and check Fountain of Kings. This will spawn 2 Kraken NMs: Honor and Valor:
    • You can pull them one at a time, they will not link, but will aggro to sound.
    • Valor uses Hundred Fists.
    • Honor casts Paralyga and Silencega.
    • It is possible to land Blind and Gravity on either, but both resist Sleep.
    • Letting one depop and then defeating the other makes this a lot easier to deal with.
  8. Check the Fountain of Kings again, after the bodies disappear, for some [Key Item] Drops of Amnio.
  9. Return to Halver.
  10. Wait 1 Earth minute, then exit the Chateau (into Northern San d'Oria) for another cutscene.
