San d’Oria mission walkthrough

Save the Children

Mission Orders: Rescue the children held captive in the Orcish camp in Ghelsba Outpost.

Start NPCAny San d'Oria Gate Guard
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
RepeatableYes
PreviousBat Hunt
NextThe Rescue Drill
TitleFodderchief Flayer

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank 2
1,000 Gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to any Gate Guard and accept the Mission.
  2. Next speak to Arnau in the Cathedral in Northern San d'Oria.
    • He is the main NPC at the Altar in the Chapel section of the Cathedral.
  3. After the cutscene, head to Ghelsba Outpost. The entrance is located at (E-4) in West Ronfaure.
    • The map for Ghelsba Outpost may be purchased for 600 Gil from either Elesca at (I-8) in Northern San d'Oria or Violitte at (G-10) in Southern San d'Oria.
  4. Make your way to the Hut Door in the large area around (G-9). The door itself is located at (F-10).
  5. Interact with the Hut Door and clear the BCNM Save the Children. You will obtain [Key Item] Orcish hut key.
    • Up to 6 people are allowed into this BCNM if they are on or have completed this Mission.
    • When entering the battlefield, a Level Restriction (Status) effect will be applied (level unlimited), which serves to reset TP, desummons Trusts, and prevents exp loss if you're defeated. Trusts can be used in the battlefield: be sure to wait until your trust magic Recast Time is ready, and summon them after entering the battlefield but before entering combat.
    • You will fight 3 Orcs.
    • Fodderchief Vokdek (WAR)
    • Strongarm Zodvad (RNG)
    • Sureshot Snatgat (RNG)
  6. Interact with the Hut Door after clearing the BCNM for a cutscene.
  7. Return to a Gate Guard to complete the Mission and receive Rank 2.
    • (Optional): There is an additional cutscene at Arnau after speaking with the Gate Guard.
