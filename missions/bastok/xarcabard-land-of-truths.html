Bastok mission walkthrough

Xarcabard, Land of Truths

You must go to Castle Zvahl in Xarcabard. The President himself will brief you in his office.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade 2 Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. Malduc right beside Naji will give the mission.
  3. Click on the Door: President's Office next to Iron Eater and enter to speak to President Karst for another cutscene.
    • If you're not using Trusts and are level 55-60, form a full party of players.
    • You will have to reach the Throne Room in Castle Zvahl Keep and clear the BCNM "The Shadow Lord Battle".
  4. Castle Zvahl Keep Home Point #1 is fastest way. If you don't have the HP unlocked already, check the instructions below.
  5. After you clear the BCNM, you'll get another cutscene. At the end, you'll be returned to the entrance of Castle Zvahl Baileys. You will also receive the [Key Item] Shadow fragment.
    • You can start the Zilart Missions and continue with Rhapsodies of Vanadiel Mission 1-12 from this point on, ranking up is optional.
  6. Return to the President for your reward: Rank 6 and 20,000 Gil.
    • Don't forget to speak to Lucius in the Metalworks at (I-9) to learn Trust: Volker.
    • Also if you've obtained the trust permits (the Red and Green) from the other 2 nations you can acquire Trion and Ajido-Marujido as trusts now by visiting their respective starting NPC's.
