Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. You'll be instructed to go see Medicine Eagle (G/H-6, all the way north) in Bastok Mines
  3. You must then speak with Drake Fang in Zeruhn Mines (H-6), near the boat.
    • You can access the Zeruhn Mines from Bastok Mines (D-7) or Survival Guide teleport.
  4. You will get a cutscene telling you to go to Western Altepa Desert.
    • Quick Travel options:
    • Unity Warp (Level 125) brings you close, at (I-7).
    • Survival Guide teleports you at (K-7).
  5. In Western Altepa Desert, seek out a ??? on the west side of (G-8), in-between 3 glowing rocks. It will spawn 2 level 62 Manticore NMs, Western Sphinx and Eastern Sphinx.
    • You only need to defeat one, but it is difficult to pull only one. You cannot obtain the Key Item until the other is either dead or de-aggroed. Either pull one and wait for the other to depop, or just defeat both.
    • They are immune to Sleep and Lullaby. They have around 7,000 HP each.
  6. Once defeated, check the ??? again to receive the [Key Item] Altepa moonpebble.
    • Note: If you zone before receiving the [Key Item] Altepa moonpebble, you will have to fight both NMs again.
  7. Return to Bastok Mines HP #3 and speak with Tall Mountain (J-7) on the bottom level near the restricted area.
  8. After the cutscene, the Mission will be complete.
