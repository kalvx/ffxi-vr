Bastok mission walkthrough

Jeuno

You are to be sent to work for the Embassy of Bastok in Jeuno. First obtain a letter of recommendation from the Presidential Aide, Sir Lucius.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals (3+ if you skipped the previous mission) to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
  2. Speak to Lucius, located at the Aide's Office (J/I-9) in the Metalworks HP #1 until you receive [Key Item] Letter to the ambassador.
  3. Head to the Bastokan Embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens (H-10) and speak with Goggehn.
  4. Head to Lower Delkfutt's Tower (E-8) Map 1 and enter the Basement by clicking the Cermet Door. Once you go through the door, proceed to (K/L-9) and click the Cermet Door for a cutscene. (Note: Another player using a key does not work.)
    • If you already possess the Delkfutt Key trade it to the Cermet Door for the [Key Item] Delkfutt key then you may skip this climb:.
    • If you do not possess the Key, start the climb process detailed below.
    • You may be Tractored through this Basement door without the Key. If you decide to go this route, proceed to the "Obtaining Delkfutt Key Short Way" section.
    • Otherwise, read the "Obtaining Delkfutt Key" section below.
  5. Return to the Bastokan Embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens and click the Door: Bastokan Emb. for a cutscene to complete the Mission.
  6. Obtaining Delkfutt Key Short Way:
    • This is the shortest route. You may be Tractored through the door to the Basement at Lower Delkfutt's Tower (E-8) Map 1 without the Key if a party member already has it.
  7. Next, you can open the door at (J-8).
  8. Click it to travel to Upper Delkfutt's Tower. Walk forward then click the ??? at the bottom of the stairs to be teleported directly to:
  9. Porphyrion, kill this NM then trade the Delkfutt Key it drops to the Elevator directly beside it.
    • You do not need to kill the NM, Click the shimmering ??? near the elevator for the [Key Item] Delkfutt key
  10. After using the elevator, proceed down the spiral stair case for two zones, through the Cermet Door then walk through the large room heading South-East to another Cermet Door at (K-9) Lower Delkfutt's Tower for a cutscene.
  11. Return to the Bastokan Embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens and click the Door: Bastokan Emb. for a cutscene to complete the Mission.
  12. Obtaining Delkfutt Key:
    • This process involves a multi-zone, ten-floor run up the entire tower. Expect the climb up alone to take roughly 15 minutes. If you are below level 50, consider bringing the following:
  13. Sneak/Invisible (Status) or Prism Powder /Silent Oil .
  14. Some form of Reraise and Warp is also helpful.
    • Set your Home Point in Jeuno if you plan on warping out after the Mission.
  15. A map of Qufim Island and a Map of Delkfutt's Tower may be purchased from Promurouve at (H-7) in Lower Jeuno (inside the building) or Rusese at (H-6) in Upper Jeuno for 3,000 Gil each.
  16. Make your way to Port Jeuno and zone into Qufim Island.
  17. Enter Lower Delkfutt's Tower at (F-6).
  18. Once inside prepare for a long walk. s and Dancing Weapons will aggro to Sound and s and will aggro to Sight. and s are Aggressive to Magic.
  19. Navigation:
  20. Floor 1: (Lower Delkfutt's Tower)
    • Enter and follow the right wall until you reach the stairs at (E/F-6).
  21. Floor 2:
    • Follow the left wall until you reach the stairs at (I-9).
  22. Floor 3:
    • Follow the right wall until you reach the Teleporter at (G-6).
  23. Floor 4: (Middle Delkfutt's Tower Map 1)
    • Follow the right wall past 2 Teleporters and go up the stairs at (J-6).
  24. Floor 5: (half way there)
    • Enter the door into a hallway on your right at (I-7) then follow the left wall until you see a stairway leading north at (H-10).
  25. Floor 6:
    • At the top of the stairs, follow the right wall to a Teleporter at (J-10).
  26. Floor 7:
    • In this big room, head northwest to a stairway at (G-7).
  27. Floor 8:
    • Follow the left wall to another stairway at (J-7).
  28. Floor 9:
    • Head south to a stairway that leads back down at (J-10).
  29. Floor 8: (again)
    • Head west to a stairway leading back up at (G-10). Do not fall down the hole in the floor!
  30. Floor 9: (again)
    • Follow the left wall until you reach a Teleporter at (F-6).
  31. Floor 10: (Upper Delkfutt's Tower Map 1)
    • When you zone in head through the door to the south-east and down a hallway. You will find a door on your right at (H-7). This door is guarded by Mimas, a Gigas NM that uses Hundred Fists.
    • After killing Mimas and clearing the hallway enter the door at (H-7) into a large room. This room will be filled with Gigas and a NM named Porphyrion.
    • Try to pull Porphyrion out into the hallway without linking any of the other Gigas. Summoner or a high level helper is useful for this.
  32. Kill Porphyrion and any aggro you may have. Porphyrion will drop 6 Delkfutt Key s.
    • After the kill, a glowing ??? will spawn. Check this to receive the [Key Item] Delkfutt key key item variant that doesn't take up an inventory space.
    • After defeating Porphyrion, you may choose to head up two more floors to reach the Home Point on the twelfth floor. This will save you from having to ever do this climb again. Be advised that higher floors will contain enemies that are level 65+ and will aggro.
  33. Once you have your Key, trade it to the elevator on the floor at (H-8) inside Porphyrion's room.
  34. Walk down the long staircase after using the elevator to get to the Basement.
  35. Finishing the Mission:
  36. Once you reach the Basement, head east into a large room and trade your Delkfutt Key (or interact if you have the [Key Item] Delkfutt key) to the Bastok door listed below for a cutscene.
    • Windurst's Door is at (L-7)/(M-7).
    • San d' Oria's Door is at (L-8)/(M-8).
    • Bastok's Door is at (K-9)/(L-9).
    • If you do not get the cutscene you are probably at the wrong door.
  37. Return to the Bastokan Embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens and click the Door: Bastokan Emb. for a cutscene to complete the Mission. You may now safely toss your Delkfutt Key
    • This is the 3rd door inside the embassy building. There are multiple doors with the same name.

Notes

1. You do not have to be on this Mission to receive a Delkfutt Key from Porphyrion.
