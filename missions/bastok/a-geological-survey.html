Bastok mission walkthrough

A Geological Survey

Assist Chief Engineer Cid in conducting an analysis of the gaseous emissions in the Dangruf Wadi. Meet with Cid for a more detailed explanation.

Start NPCAny Bastok Gate Guard
Starting zoneZeruhn Mines
PreviousThe Zeruhn Report
NextFetichism

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to any Bastokan Gate Guard to accept the Mission.
    • Malduc right beside Naji will start this Mission.
  2. Go to Cid's Lab at (G-8) in Metalworks top floor and talk to Cid for a cutscene. You'll receive the [Key Item] Blue acidity tester.
  3. Head to (D-9) in South Gustaberg to enter Dangruf Wadi.
  4. Once you enter the zone, turn right at the fork and head north to (I-8).
  5. In the south-east corner of (I-8), stand on the geyser until it pushes you up onto the ledge.
    • Check your detector Key Item to make sure that it has changed to a [Key Item] Red acidity tester.
  6. Return to Cid for a cutscene that finishes the Mission.
