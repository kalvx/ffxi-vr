Bastok mission walkthrough

The Chains That Bind Us

The president has ordered a full-scale investigation of Zepwell Island. You are to travel to the Quicksand Caves located in the central Altepa Desert. Report to Iron Eater for a briefing.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from any Bastok Gate Guard after raising your Rank bar via Crystal trades.
  2. Visit Iron Eater at the President's Office in Metalworks. Continue dialogue until the text repeats.
  3. Travel to Western Altepa Desert and enter the tunnel at (G-5) to reach Quicksand Caves.
  4. Navigate to the weighted Ornate Door on Map 7 at (H-8).
    • The door requires 3 weight points to open: Galka = 3, Hume/Elvaan/Mithra = 2, Tarutaru = 1.
    • Alternatively, use the Loadstone Key Item (from the "Open Sesame" quest) to pass solo.
  5. Open the second door at (I-10) and proceed to the small room at (G-11).
  6. Defeat 3 level 70 Antica NMs: Triarius IV-XIV (BLM), Princeps IV-XLV (PLD), Centurio IV-VII (WAR).
  7. After the fight, interact with the Galka Statue for a cutscene with Zeid.
  8. Enter Quicksand Caves Map 5 via the (D-12) entrance in Western Altepa Desert.
  9. Navigate through the Map 5-6 weighted doors to the mural room at (H-8) for a second cutscene with Zeid.
  10. Return to Iron Eater in Metalworks for the final cutscene.

Notes

Completing the "Open Sesame" quest grants the Loadstone Key Item, which allows solo passage through all weighted doors in Quicksand Caves.
