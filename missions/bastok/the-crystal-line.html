Bastok mission walkthrough

The Crystal Line

Find a section of the strange structure on the Konschtat Highlands that drains the energy from crystals. Bring a faded crystal back to Chief Engineer Cid for analysis.

Start NPCAny Bastok Gate Guard
Starting zoneMetalworks
PreviousFetichism
NextWading Beasts

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
    • 4 Crystals seems to be enough.
    • (Optional): Talk to Cid in Bastok Metalworks for more information.
  2. You will have to obtain a Faded Crystal and trade it to Cid to receive the [Key Item] C. L. report.
    • Faded Crystal s are not Exclusive, and can be traded or purchased from Bazaars or the Auction House. ( ➞ Others ➞ Misc. 1)
    • Alternatively, it can be obtained by trading a Synthesis Crystal to any Crag's Telepoint:
    • Crag of Dem - Konschtat Highlands (I-6)
    • Crag of Holla - La Theine Plateau (K-8)
    • Crag of Mea - Tahrongi Canyon (I-6)
    • You can easily teleport to any of these zones by talking to a Unity NPC and using the Lvl 99 Unity Warps.
    • (Optional): Talk to Naji.
  3. Speak to Ayame in the Cannonry at (K-7) in the Metalworks to finish the Mission.
    • You may have to speak to her several times to receive the correct dialogue.

Notes

1. If you're leveling up, make sure to accept the Records of Eminence objective Colorless (W) for extra EXP. - This is under: Quests → Objective List → Vana'versary (scroll down to see it) → 15th Vana'versary II 2. This Mission is an excellent opportunity for first-time players to obtain a [Key Item] Dem gate crystal. 3. Since the implementation of the Chains of Promathia expansion, the north platform of the Crag of Dem is the only remaining Telepoint in Konschtat Highlands that can be used to obtain a Faded Crystal .
