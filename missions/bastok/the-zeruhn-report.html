Bastok mission walkthrough

The Zeruhn Report

Collect Overseer Makarim's report from the Zeruhn Mines, in the western section of the Bastok Mines District. Deliver said report to Sir Naji, a Musketeer stationed near the President's Office.

Start NPCAny Bastok Gate Guard
Starting zoneBastok Markets
CoordinatesL-7
NextA Geological Survey

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Rank Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to any Bastokan Gate Guard and accept the Mission.
    • Argus (NPC) - Port Bastok (L-7)
    • Cleades - Bastok Markets (D-11)
    • Rashid - Bastok Mines (H-10)
    • Malduc - Metalworks (J-8)
  2. You will have to head to the Zeruhn Mines. The mines can be entered from Bastok Mines at (D-7).
  3. Speak to Makarim in Zeruhn Mines (H-11) for the [Key Item] Zeruhn report.
  4. Speak to Naji in the Metalworks top floor (J-8) for a cutscene and to complete the Mission.
    • Lucius' dialog in the cutscene will change depending on whether you examine the [Key Item] Zeruhn report or not.
