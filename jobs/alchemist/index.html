Alchemist

The Current Reality advanced job: recovery, mixtures, prepared magic and gun support.

Learn the art

Alchemist combines restorative mixtures, ailment removal, elemental weaknesses, prepared magic and ranged utility. Carry your medicines in main inventory; job mixtures use their presence without consuming them.

A Remedy Without a Name is listed with the other advanced-job unlocks. Begin with Edrien Rikku, upstairs at the Alchemy Guild in Bastok Mines, once any saved job reaches level 30.

A practical party rhythm

Pull with your gun or an enemy-targeted spell, restore missing HP with mixtures, and use Diagnose to choose an ailment remedy. Plan Volatile Mixture’s random weakness around your party’s elemental damage. Resourcefulness lets you make that attempt while keeping TP for a weapon skill.

Float helps with travel and Gravity; Mana Shield is useful on an ally who has MP to absorb damage. Normal target, range, resistance and recast rules apply. Use the Jobs dossier for searchable ability, spell, weapon-skill and equipment records.
