Alchemist Guns, Bullets & Grips

Aldric Basin’s seal exchange, effect ammunition, staff grips and armor vendors.

Aldric Basin · Gun Exchange

Find Aldric Basin upstairs beside the Alchemy Guild in Bastok Mines. Speak to him to browse, then trade exactly the listed number of Beastmen’s Seals for one gun. Keep a free inventory slot. These are separate Alchemist-only items using existing gun designs; their equip levels apply.

The +1 adds Ranged Accuracy +4; the +2 adds Ranged Accuracy +8. Existing icon artwork is retained. No gil, Kindred seals, previous gun or artifact quest is required. Existing guild services and Edrien Rikku’s gear shop are unchanged. Guns use normal bullets; this exchange does not change ammunition restrictions or combat skill ranks.

Trade physical Beastmen’s Seals only; gil, Kindred seals and mixed trades are not accepted. These guns cannot be listed on the Auction House.

Alchemist effect bullets

Two separate Alchemist-only bullets add a choice of utility to gun attacks. They stack to 99 and are auctionable under Weapons → Ammo & Misc. → Ammunition.

BulletLevelDamageAdditional effect
Concussion Bullet501Stun, up to 5 seconds
Corrosive Bullet5520Defense down 12%, up to 60 seconds

Both use Delay 240 and a base 10% effect chance on a landed ordinary ranged attack, reduced against higher-level targets. Resistance can shorten or prevent the effect; Stun immunity is respected. Stronger existing debuffs are preserved. These are consumable utility rounds, not guaranteed interrupts or weapon-skill effects.

Buy the rotating bullet selection through the Auction House under Weapons → Ammo & Misc. → Ammunition. Stock rotates; each seller offers a selected bullet type. Bullets are consumed when fired.

Grips with a staff

Equip an eligible grip in the sub slot while using a two-handed staff in the main slot. The grip’s own job and level requirements still apply. All-job grips include Alchemist. A grip is not a gun attachment and cannot accompany a dagger.

The Auction House now rotates grip stock alongside its other categories. Check the item encyclopedia for an individual grip’s level and effects.

Artifact and job armor

Edrien Rikku, upstairs at the Alchemy Guild in Bastok Mines, offers Alchemist Gear after the job is unlocked and your saved Alchemist level reaches 50. Each piece has its own equip level.

Open the existing armor guide → for all six families, 20 versions, five slot bonuses and per-piece gil prices.
