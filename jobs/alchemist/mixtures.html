ALC · Current Reality job guide

Mixtures & Resourcefulness

All 20 job abilities, carried medicines, healing and the Resourcefulness window.

Carried medicines

Carry one of each required medicine in main inventory. Mixtures do not consume them; ordinary item use still consumes the item. Alchemist job abilities require Alchemist as the main job.

Resourcefulness and Volatile Mixture

Resourcefulness: level 25, 30-second duration, 5-minute recast. It waives carried medicines for mixtures and prepared spells. During the same window, Volatile Mixture also works at zero TP and preserves any TP held. Its own recast still applies.

Volatile Mixture: level 10, 30-second base recast, 10-yalm range and any weapon. Outside Resourcefulness it requires and spends 1,000 TP. It hits with a random element and can lower that element’s resistance for 30 seconds. The weakness does not stack with another Volatile weakness, and stronger unrelated effects remain. AF head bonuses improve the attack; eligible feet shorten its recast.

When the window ends, the normal medicine and TP requirements return. Resourcefulness does not reset cooldowns or bypass job levels and target rules.

Healing and Final Elixir

Max Potion restores up to 500 HP; Mix: Max Potion restores up to 700 HP, before AF body bonuses. Each has its own 15-second recast, so using one leaves the other available. Recovery is capped by the target’s missing HP and creates healing enmity.

Final Elixir remains the 2-hour ability, restoring HP and MP to nearby party members. It needs no carried medicine.

Independent mixture timers

Every Alchemist mixture has its own timer. Max Potion and Mix: Max Potion each have a 15-second base recast. Life Water and Guard Drink each have a 30-second base recast. Using one does not start the other’s cooldown.

Remedies and other enhancements also have separate timers. Resourcefulness retains its 5-minute recast, Volatile Mixture its 30-second recast, and Final Elixir its 2-hour recast. AF feet can shorten eligible recasts; Resourcefulness does not reset them.

Requires client update 1.0.10 and the server’s Map restart to load the new timer groups. Close and reopen the game after updating.
