ALC · Current Reality job guide

Prepared Alchemist Magic

Learn levels, recipes, cast times, Float, Mana Shield and celestial mixtures.

Prepare, then cast

These seven spells are automatically learned at their listed Alchemist levels. Carry one Potion, one Ether and one Antidote together in main inventory. None is consumed. Resourcefulness waives this requirement.

Select the normal Magic-menu entry or use a macro. A mixture animation precedes normal casting; AF feet shorten the preparation. Movement, silence, range, target validity and recast still apply. Internal commands and numeric control messages are hidden; useful cast results and errors remain visible.

Float

Level 37: hover while standing, walking and running, gain +20% movement speed and resist Gravity for 3 minutes. Existing Weight is cleared. The airy effect is retained with the wings hidden. The height is visual; normal terrain collision applies. Combat and casting actions take priority, then hover resumes.

Float uses the Quickening icon. A remaining duration can survive logout and resume on login. It supports yourself, players and your party’s trusts.

Mana Shield

Level 40: for 60 seconds, each point of HP damage spends one MP first. Enough MP prevents HP loss; if a hit exceeds the MP remaining, its overflow reaches HP and the shield ends. Expiry, dispel or death also removes it. Recast: 3 minutes.

The target must have current MP. An Alchemist with a subjob that grants no MP should use an MP-bearing job/subjob or choose an ally with MP. The spell explains an absent or empty MP pool. It uses the Mana Wall icon and supports players and party trusts. Healing stays ordinary.

Impact and the Meteors

The Magic menu uses Mix: Impact, Mix: Meteor and Mix: Meteor II for the Alchemist versions. Impact retains dark damage and its seven-stat reduction. The Meteors deal non-elemental damage, with Meteor II providing the stronger version.

Impact costs no MP. Mix: Meteor costs 25 MP at level 60; Mix: Meteor II costs 35 MP at level 72. Previously learned Meteor II stays saved but requires level 72 to cast. Original mage versions remain separate.
