Beginner walkthrough · Follow in order

From three Trusts to five

Unlock the fourth slot first, then continue directly into the fifth-slot route. The required story detours are included where you need them.

What you are unlocking

Trusts at onceRequirementWhere this guide takes you
3Normal starting capacity after learning Trust magic.Your starting point.
4Rhapsody in WhiteFinish Flames of Prayer in Norg.
5Rhapsody in Crimson, after WhiteContinue through From the Ruins in Aht Urhgan Whitegate.

Five Trusts plus your character fill a six-member party. These are permanent story unlocks, not five new Trust spells. You still need to learn enough different Trusts to fill the slots.

If you already have White, start the route toward five. If you already have both key items, check your party and test the fifth summon.

Get ready

  • Open Missions → Rhapsodies of Vana’diel and note your current mission title. Use titles to match your progress; some references number these missions differently.
  • If Trust magic is already working and you can call three, continue below. Otherwise complete one starter tutorial: San d’Oria, Bastok, or Windurst. The tutorial starts at level 5.
  • Keep several inventory slots open for mission rewards and Ciphers. Touch home points as you visit places so you can return easily.
  • The fourth slot is an early story reward. The fifth is a longer project with boss fights and other mission chains; gaining levels and improving equipment between checkpoints is normal.

Use the World Route Finder to search an area and open its maps, exits, outpost and nearby crystal information.

1 · Unlock your fourth Trust

Start with either Selbina or Mhaura. Complete one path, then both paths join in Norg.

  1. Rhapsodies of Vana’diel — trigger the introduction.

    On a job at level 3 or higher, leave your Mog House and enter a starting-city district such as Bastok Markets, Southern San d’Oria or Windurst Woods. Watch the introductory cutscene. If it played earlier, check the mission log instead of restarting the sequence.

  2. Resonance — enter Selbina or Mhaura.

    Zone into your chosen port for a cutscene. Keep using that same port for the next steps.

  3. Emissary from the Seas — accept the request.

    In Selbina, speak to Naillina, then Abelard. In Mhaura, speak to Numi Adaligo, then Ekokoko. Accept the mission dialogue.

  4. Set Free — collect and trade the correct three items.

    Selbina path: collect 3 clumps of Bee Pollen from Huge Wasps in La Theine Plateau or Konschtat Highlands. Trade all three to Abelard.

    Mhaura path: collect 3 Mandragora Dewdrops from Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon. Trade all three to Ekokoko.

    The mission should advance to The Beginning. If you receive Gilgamesh’s introductory letter instead of a voucher, you can still continue this route.

  5. The Beginning — take the offered trip to Norg.

    Speak to Pacomart in Selbina or Tonasav in Mhaura and accept the passage to Norg. In Norg, touch its home point, then examine the Oaken Door at K-8 for the story scene.

  6. Flames of Prayer — examine the Oaken Door again.

    Check the same Oaken Door once more. After this cutscene, receive Rhapsody in White. Your mission log should move to The Path Untraveled.

Checkpoint: four Trusts. Confirm White in your key items. In an area where Trusts are allowed, outside combat and as party leader, summon four different learned Trusts. You do not need to equip or trade the key item.

2 · Continue from White toward Crimson

Do these in order. White already raised your limit to four; Umber and Azure below are intermediate story rewards and do not add another Trust slot.

  1. The Path Untraveled — visit a Shattered Telepoint.

    Examine the original Shattered Telepoint at Holla in La Theine Plateau, Dem in Konschtat Highlands, or Mea in Tahrongi Canyon for a cutscene. Use the story target, rather than the nearby blue travel crystal we added.

  2. At Heaven’s Door — go to Qufim Island.

    Examine the Undulating Confluence at G-8 for a cutscene. Stay nearby afterward.

  3. The Lion’s Roar — defeat Ophiotaurus.

    Summon your four Trusts before interacting with the Confluence again. Examine it to spawn Ophiotaurus and defeat it.

  4. Eddies of Despair (I) — examine the Confluence again.

    Watch the next scene and follow the transition into Escha - Zi’Tah. Finish the arrival cutscene before continuing. Then return through the Confluence or use your available travel.

  5. A Land After Time — revisit a Shattered Telepoint.

    Return to one of the same original crag targets and watch the next scene. Receive Rhapsody in Umber and Cipher: Lion II. Leave an inventory slot free so the reward can be received.

Next: finish the nation-story checkpoint →

Nation story checkpoint · Reach Rank 6

At Fate’s Call, finish your home nation’s missions through its 5-2 Shadow Lord finale. Report back to complete the mission and reach Rank 6. You only need one nation’s story for this checkpoint.

Follow your nation’s existing walkthroughs in order: San d’Oria missions · Bastok missions · Windurst missions. If the next mission is not offered, speak to the mission guard and check your rank-point requirement.

  1. Fate’s Call — enter a starting-city district again.

    After the nation checkpoint, zone into a starting-city district to trigger the next Rhapsodies scene. The mission should change to What Lies Beyond.

  2. What Lies Beyond — return to Norg’s Oaken Door.

    Examine the door and finish the scene.

  3. The Ties that Bind — examine the ??? in Sea Serpent Grotto.

    Leave Norg into Sea Serpent Grotto and find the story ??? on the Norg approach. Its location is approximately X 111, Y 0, Z -7. Examine it for the scene.

  4. Impurity — find the ??? in Yuhtunga Jungle.

    Go to the story ??? at approximately X -410, Y 17, Z -381 in Yuhtunga Jungle and watch the cutscene. Remain there for the next mission.

  5. The Lost Avatar — defeat Siren, then check the ??? again.

    Prepare your party first. Examine the same target to spawn Siren. After winning, interact with the target again to receive Rhapsody in Azure and advance. If the fight is too difficult, keep White’s four-slot benefit while you level and return later.

  6. Volto Oscuro — return to the Oaken Door.

    Watch the Norg scene and receive Cipher: Zeid II.

  7. Ring My Bell — check the Oaken Door again.

    Finish the next scene to reach Spirits Awoken. If other story progress is holding Tenzen unavailable, use the Promathia section next, then return to this door.

  8. Spirits Awoken — enter Lower Delkfutt’s Tower from Qufim.

    Walk through the entrance from Qufim Island into Lower Delkfutt’s Tower. Finish the Rhapsodies cutscene. If this also starts Promathia, finish those introductory scenes and check your mission logs.

Next: make Tenzen available through Promathia →

Promathia prerequisite · Complete A Vessel Without a Captain

If your current Chains of Promathia mission is already The Road Forks or later, you have passed the minimum story checkpoint. Continue with Rhapsodies, provided Tenzen is currently available. Otherwise follow this chain; there are several separate fights.

  1. The Rites of Life.

    Enter Lower Delkfutt’s Tower from Qufim for the opening scenes. Enter Upper Jeuno, then speak to Monberaux in the infirmary for the remaining scenes.

  2. Below the Arks.

    Speak to Pherimociel in Ru’Lude Gardens’ palace. At one of the three crags, examine its Shattered Telepoint, enter the Hall of Transference, and use the appropriate apparatus to enter that crag’s Promyvion.

  3. The Mothercrystals — clear all three Promyvions.

    Climb Promyvion - Holla, Promyvion - Dem and Promyvion - Mea. Defeat the Memory Receptacles needed to open upward paths, reach each Spire, and win its mission battlefield. Finish the post-fight scene after each win. All three different clears are required; repeating one does not replace another. The third clear sends you toward Tavnazia.

  4. An Invitation West.

    Finish the scene in Lufaise Meadows and enter Tavnazian Safehold for the next scene.

  5. The Lost City.

    Speak to Despachiaire upstairs in Tavnazian Safehold, then examine the Sewer Entrance on the lower level. Enter Phomiuna Aqueducts.

  6. Distant Beliefs — finish the Aqueducts route.

    Defeat Minotaur on the second map at J-3. Continue afterward; the kill alone does not finish the mission. Obtain a Bronze Key from Fomors for the locked Iron Gate at G-8, or use a main-job Thief with lockpicking tools. Go west to the Wooden Ladder at E-8, examine it for the story scene, and climb up.

    Enter the library area and use the ??? at F-7 to open the southern hidden passage. Activate the correct pair of elemental Oil Lamps promptly, pass the opened door, and examine the Ornate Gate for the next scene. Return to Justinius in Tavnazian Safehold.

    Oil Lamp pairs by Vana’diel day
    FiresdayFire + Ice
    IcedayIce + Wind
    WindsdayWind + Earth
    EarthsdayEarth + Thunder
    LightningsdayThunder + Water
    WatersdayWater + Fire
    Lightsday or DarksdayLight + Dark

    These are the elemental lamps, not the end lamps that operate the corridor doors.

  7. An Eternal Melody.

    Examine the Walnut Door upstairs in Tavnazian Safehold. Go through the Safehold’s southwest exit and examine the Dilapidated Gate at I-11 in Misareaux Coast, then return to the Safehold and approach the bridge/ramp area for the next scene.

  8. Ancient Vows.

    Take the Safehold’s northwest exit, examine the Dilapidated Gate at F-7 in Misareaux Coast, and enter Riverne - Site #A01. Collect two Giant Scales from the wyverns to pass the required unstable distortions. Reach Monarch Linn, win the Ancient Vows battlefield against the Mammets, and finish the ending scene.

  9. The Call of the Wyrmking.

    Finish the South Gustaberg scene, visit the airship-dock area in Port Bastok for another, then speak to Cid in the Metalworks.

  10. A Vessel Without a Captain.

    Examine Door: Neptune’s Spire in Lower Jeuno. Then walk toward the palace entrance in Ru’Lude Gardens for the final scene. Confirm that your Promathia log now says The Road Forks. That is the stopping point needed here; return to Rhapsodies.

Next: the Jeuno scenes and Aht Urhgan access →

Back to Rhapsodies · Jeuno to Whitegate

  1. Crashing Waves.

    With A Vessel Without a Captain completed, approach the palace entrance in Ru’Lude Gardens for the Rhapsodies scene. Receive Cipher: Tenzen II. If inventory was full, clear a slot and check the Mystic Retriever there.

  2. Call to Serve.

    Zone into Port Jeuno for a scene and Cipher: Prishe II. Use the Port Jeuno Mystic Retriever if you could not receive the item.

  3. Numbering Days.

    Examine the Door: Marble Bridge at the Marble Bridge Eatery in Upper Jeuno. The next mission is Inescapable Binds.

  4. Inescapable Binds / The Road to Aht Urhgan.

    If you do not have a Boarding Permit, speak to Faursel inside the Tenshodo in Lower Jeuno. Accept his travel quest, speak again, and choose “Where’s Tenzen?” while Inescapable Binds is active. This story option grants the permit without the normal item collection or gil purchase.

    If you already have the permit, keep it and proceed to Whitegate; you do not need to buy another one.

  5. Desert Winds / arrival in Aht Urhgan.

    From Mhaura, take the ship bound for Al Zahbi / Aht Urhgan, rather than the Selbina ferry. Finish the arrival scenes in Aht Urhgan Whitegate and touch its home points. Depending on prior access, your log may advance straight from Inescapable Binds to Ever Forward.

Next: get the required Aht Urhgan story progress →

Aht Urhgan prerequisite · Reach Royal Puppeteer

To finish the Whitegate Rhapsodies sequence, complete Treasures of Aht Urhgan through Imperial Schemes, leaving Royal Puppeteer as your current mission. You do not need to finish the entire expansion for the fifth slot. If you are already beyond this checkpoint, resume the final Rhapsodies steps when Aphmau is available.

  1. Land of Sacred Serpents.

    Enter Salaheem’s Sentinels in Whitegate and approach Naja Salaheem to receive a Supplies Package.

  2. Immortal Sentries.

    Deliver that key-item package to an Immortal at a staging point. For Daswil at Mamool Ja Staging Point, leave Whitegate for Wajaom Woodlands, head to E-12 and enter Mamook. Cross to Mamook’s F-10 exit into Bhaflau Thickets, then head south to the staging point’s Heavy Iron Gate near H-11. Accept the delivery dialogue. Use the nearby Runic Portal to register the staging point and return to Whitegate, then speak to Naja to finish the mission.

  3. President Salaheem.

    Speak to Rytaal at the Commissions Agency, then Naja. Leave Whitegate, return, and approach Naja again for the next scene.

  4. Knight of Gold.

    Speak to Cacaroon in Whitegate, accept his information offer, then trade 1,000 gil or one Imperial Bronze Piece. Walk into Walahra Temple for the scene, then approach Shararat Teahouse for another. Receive Raillefal’s Letter.

  5. Confessions of Royalty.

    Travel to Chateau d’Oraguille in San d’Oria and speak to Halver to deliver the letter and finish the scene.

  6. Easterly Winds.

    Approach the palace entrance in Ru’Lude Gardens and finish the scene. Keep inventory room for any offered reward.

  7. Westerly Winds.

    Return to Shararat Teahouse in Whitegate for the scene, then report to Naja. You receive Imperial Silver Pieces during this part of the story; keep them for Ruins entry.

  8. A Mercenary Life.

    Leave Whitegate and return. Walk back into Salaheem’s Sentinels and approach Naja’s platform to trigger the next story scene.

  9. Undersea Scouting.

    Speak to Naja. Go to Bhaflau Thickets F-6 and trade one Imperial Silver Piece to Kamih Mapokhalam to enter Alzadaal Undersea Ruins. Follow the corridor toward the large door until the mission scene triggers.

  10. Astral Waves.

    Return to Naja in Whitegate and finish the scene.

  11. Imperial Schemes.

    Leave Whitegate, wait until the next Vana’diel day, then return to Salaheem’s Sentinels and walk onto its entrance platform for the scene. This is a game-day rollover, not a real-world day. Confirm your mission log changes to Royal Puppeteer.

Next: finish the Crimson unlock →

3 · Finish the fifth-slot unlock

  1. Ever Forward / Aphmau’s Light.

    With the Aht Urhgan checkpoint reached, examine Imperial Whitegate at L-9 in Aht Urhgan Whitegate. Finish the Rhapsodies scene and receive Cipher: Nashmeira II. Some interlude scenes depend on your earlier progress; if your current mission is Aphmau’s Light, the same gate is your next target. Continue when the log says Reunited.

  2. Reunited.

    Speak to Nadeey inside Walahra Temple for the next scene.

  3. Take Wing.

    Walk into the Shararat Teahouse area at K-12 to trigger the cutscene.

  4. Prime Number.

    Zone into Alzadaal Undersea Ruins again for a Rhapsodies scene. You can repeat the Bhaflau Thickets F-6 entrance with one Imperial Silver Piece. An already available Nyzul Isle Runic Portal is another way in.

  5. From the Ruins.

    Return to Aht Urhgan Whitegate and examine Imperial Whitegate at L-9 once more. Finish the scene to receive Rhapsody in Crimson. The next Rhapsodies mission is Cauterize; you can stop the story here if this guide’s goal was your fifth slot.

Checkpoint: five Trusts.

Confirm both White and Crimson in your key items. Make a solo party, enter an area where Trusts can be called, stay outside combat and summon five different learned Trusts. Your character plus five Trusts should now fill all six party slots.

Trade the Ciphers you earned to a starter-nation Trust NPC to learn those alter egos. Earning a Cipher, learning a Trust spell and raising the number you can summon at once are separate things.

If a step does not trigger

The door repeats an old scene.
Finish it, check your current mission title and interact again. Some scenes advance only after the previous one has finished.
A mission says a character is unavailable.
Check your other story logs. Tenzen or Prishe can be occupied by Promathia; Aphmau can be occupied by Aht Urhgan. Finish the current blocking story segment before returning. Completing a minimum checkpoint does not make every later story state safe to pause in.
I got a scene but the mission did not finish.
Clear inventory space for its Cipher or other item. Check the local Mystic Retriever when the game directs you there, or examine the mission target again.
I have the key item but cannot summon another Trust.
Check party size, leadership, combat state, area restrictions and duplicate-character restrictions. Other players occupy party slots too. Dismiss and retry with different learned Trusts while solo and outside combat.
I leveled while traveling.
Dismiss and resummon your Trusts before the next fight so their levels refresh.
