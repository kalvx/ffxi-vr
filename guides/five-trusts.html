Current Reality companion guide

Purchase and summon five Trusts

Find Trusty-Musky in Mhaura, purchase your companions with Kindred’s Seals, and fill all five Trust slots.

Five companions from purchases

Current Reality allows five purchased, compatible Trusts alongside your character. The old three → four → five Rhapsodies capacity chain has been replaced. You do not need Rhapsody in White, Rhapsody in Crimson, a nation Trust tutorial, or a Shadow Lord clear for these five slots.

Buying five different Trusts gives you five summons to choose from. Normal duplicate/version restrictions still apply: five purchases do not make an incompatible combination summonable.

Trusty-Musky’s location photo, purchase steps, and menu options →

Find Trusty-Musky

Travel to Mhaura. He stands near Home Point #1, at X −10.990, Y −15.813, Z 88.810. Use the World Route Finder for the journey.

  1. Speak to Trusty-Musky. Read the introduction or select Skip to catalogue.
  2. Browse All shadows, Basic shadows (1–2 seals), or Other shadows (3–10 seals).
  3. Select the Trust and Choose this shadow.
  4. Trade exactly its listed Kindred’s Seal price within three minutes. Do not include other items. Speak to him again to cancel your selection.
  5. Repeat for the companions you want. Each purchase permanently teaches its summon; owned entries remain marked [Learned].

Summon your purchased party

In an area that permits Trusts, use /ma "Trust Name" <me> or Magic → Trust. You may fill all five companion slots immediately, subject to the ordinary party rules. Human party members also occupy the six available slots.

  • Be solo or the party leader, outside an alliance.
  • Summon before gaining enmity, and stop seeking a party first.
  • Wait out the normal delay after a party member joins.
  • Battlefields retain their own Trust permissions, participant limits, and any Rhapsody in Umber access requirement.
  • Different versions of the same companion may be incompatible.

Purchases and old unlock routes

No Trusts yet? The old roster resets once on your first login after this change. Purchase the ones you want from Trusty-Musky; future logins preserve purchases.

Quest or Cipher obtained? Legacy meetings and Cipher redemptions record a one-time one-seal discount for the matching Trust, minimum one seal. They no longer give a free summon. Nation tutorials can still be completed, and other quest and mission progression remains.

Monberaux? He is deliberately unavailable because Alchemist is a playable job.

Wrong trade? Beastmen’s Seals, extra seals, gil, and mixed trades are rejected. No items are taken on a rejected trade.

Fourth or fifth summon fails? Check party size, compatibility, combat status, party-leader status, and area rules. White and Crimson are no longer the capacity gates.
