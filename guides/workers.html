Vapor’s worker expeditions

Trade supplies, send out a worker, and collect the proceeds when the expedition returns—even while you are offline.

Vapor · Lower Jeuno

Find Vapor near Home Point #1, at X −94.000, Y −0.091, Z −178.000. She dispatches one worker per character. Supply the requested items; the worker sells its findings and returns with gil. You do not receive physical loot.

Actual return time is randomly fixed when dispatched: 50–60 minutes, 3h20–4h, or 6h40–8h. Offline time counts. Work and earnings stop when the worker returns; leaving a completed contract uncollected does not add more income.

  1. Speak to Vapor and select a named worker.
  2. Select the contract length. She states the exact required item and quantity.
  3. Within three minutes, trade exactly those supplies and nothing else. Supplies are consumed on a successful dispatch. Speak to her again to cancel a pending selection.
  4. Return later. Open Expedition progress or Collect / view sales to inspect the ledger.
  5. When the worker has returned, select Collect [amount] gil. Collect the whole payment before hiring another worker. If your gil purse is too full, make room and return; your contract remains available.

Reports and menu options

Worker messages: On/Off saves your preference. When enabled, online workers send brief field reports and returning workers announce completion. Login gives one concise summary instead of replaying every missed message. When disabled, use Vapor’s menu for progress and results.

Last sales report keeps the most recently collected expedition’s item totals, stacks, singles, and gil. How it works explains contracts; Leave closes the menu. Use Next and Previous where the native menu is paginated.

Findings and sales

Each completed ten-minute work period can produce poor, ordinary, good, or lucky findings. Longer trips provide more chances. Results are fixed for that expedition: reopening a menu or logging in again does not reroll them.

Vapor combines findings into full stacks of 12 where possible, then sells remaining singles. The displayed payment is exactly the sum of those sales. Her fixed broker prices below are separate from live Auction House listings.
