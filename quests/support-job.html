Unlock Your Support Job

At level 18, complete either the Selbina or Mhaura route to unlock the ability to equip a second job as your support job.

At a glance

RequirementLevel 18+ on at least one job
Selbina routeElder Memories — Isacio, Selbina (G-10)
Mhaura routeThe Old Lady — Vera, Mhaura (G-10)
RewardAbility to select a Support Job

Route A — Selbina: Elder Memories

  1. Reach level 18 and speak with Isacio in Selbina (G-10).
  2. Obtain a Damselfly Worm, Magicked Skull, and Crab Apron. These are the classic Valkurm-area support-job items.
  3. Trade the items to Isacio in the requested sequence: Damselfly Worm, Magicked Skull, then Crab Apron.
  4. After the final accepted trade, the Support Job system is unlocked.

Route B — Mhaura: The Old Lady

  1. Reach level 18 and speak with Vera in Mhaura (G-10).
  2. Obtain a Wild Rabbit Tail, Dhalmel Saliva, and Bloody Robe.
  3. Trade them to Vera in the requested sequence: Wild Rabbit Tail, Dhalmel Saliva, then Bloody Robe.
  4. After the final accepted trade, the Support Job system is unlocked.

Rhapsodies shortcut: On retail behavior, completing Rhapsodies of Vana’diel mission Set Free before obtaining a subjob can provide Gilgamesh’s introductory letter, which replaces the three-item collection when speaking with Isacio or Vera at level 18+.

After unlocking

Return to a Mog House or Nomad Moogle and select a Support Job. The support job contributes abilities, traits, spells and stats according to its effective level and normal FFXI restrictions. You only need to complete one of the two unlock quests.
