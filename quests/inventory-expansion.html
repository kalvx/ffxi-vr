Lower Jeuno inventory quest chain

Inventory Expansion: Gobbiebag I–X

Expand your carried Inventory from 30 to the maximum 80 slots. Every stage below lists the exact trade and a practical way to obtain each item when the Auction House is empty.

Quest NPCBluffnix
LocationMuckvix’s Junk Shop, Lower Jeuno (H-9)
Starting capacity30 Inventory slots
Final capacity80 Inventory slots
Each upgrade+5 slots
OrderParts I through X must be completed in sequence

Basic loop: Talk to Bluffnix to activate the next quest, obtain its four items, and trade all four to him together. Zone or speak to him again if the next part does not appear immediately. If he refuses to offer a part, finish the previous part and raise Jeuno fame.

When the Auction House is empty: most Gobbiebag materials are crafted. Farm the named hide, ore, thread, cloth, or gem source and ask the matching guild crafter to finish it. The Compendium’s Item Encyclopedia gives server-derived recipes, vendors, drops, and AH paths for individual items.

Part I — 30 → 35 slots

  1. Dhalmel Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis. Farm a Dhalmel Hide from dhalmels in Tahrongi Canyon or Buburimu Peninsula, then have a leatherworker tan it.
  2. Steel Ingot
    Smithing synthesis. Mine or purchase the iron-based ingredients from metal guild vendors, then have a smith make the ingot.
  3. Linen Cloth
    Clothcraft synthesis from Linen Thread. Flax Flowers can be harvested or purchased from clothcraft-related vendors, spun into thread, then woven into cloth.
  4. Peridot
    Obtain through mining, treasure and battlefield rewards, Goblin Box results, or gem desynthesis; a goldsmith can also help process eligible gem materials.

Trade all four items together to Bluffnix. Reward: Inventory expands to 35 slots.

Part II — 35 → 40 slots

  1. Ram Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis. Farm a Ram Skin from rams in La Theine Plateau or Konschtat Highlands and have it tanned.
  2. Mythril Ingot
    Smithing or Goldsmithing synthesis from mythril materials. Mine Mythril Ore in suitable mining areas or obtain ore from beastmen and treasure, then commission the ingot.
  3. Wool Cloth
    Clothcraft synthesis from Wool Thread. Sheep wool comes from sheep and rams; spin it into thread, then weave the cloth.
  4. Turquoise
    Mine it, obtain it from treasure/battlefield rewards or Goblin Box results, or use a goldsmithing/desynthesis route.

Trade all four together. Reward: 40 slots.

Part III — 40 → 45 slots

  1. Black Tiger Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis from a Tiger Hide. Farm tigers in Batallia Downs or Beaucedine Glacier, then have the hide tanned.
  2. Gold Ingot
    Goldsmithing synthesis. Obtain Gold Ore or gold-bearing materials through mining, beastmen, treasure, or guild stock and commission a goldsmith.
  3. Velvet Cloth
    Clothcraft synthesis from Velvet Thread. Farm crawler silk, process the required thread, and have a weaver make the cloth.
  4. Painite
    Mining, treasure/battlefield rewards, Goblin Box, or gem desynthesis are the usual non-AH routes.

Trade all four together. Reward: 45 slots.

Part IV — 45 → 50 slots

  1. Cermet Chunk
    Alchemy synthesis. Gather the glass/stone components through mining or vendors and ask an alchemist to make the chunk.
  2. Darksteel Ingot
    Smithing synthesis from Darksteel Ore. Mine the ore in higher-tier mining areas or obtain it through beastmen/treasure, then commission a smith.
  3. Silk Cloth
    Clothcraft synthesis from Silk Thread. Farm crawlers for Silk Thread, then have a weaver turn the thread into cloth.
  4. Goshenite
    Obtain through mining, treasure/battlefield rewards, Goblin Box, or eligible gem desynthesis.

Trade all four together. Reward: 50 slots.

Part V — 50 → 55 slots

  1. Bugard Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis from a Bugard Skin. Farm bugards in Lufaise Meadows or Misareaux Coast and have the skin tanned.
  2. Paktong Ingot
    Smithing synthesis from the required copper, zinc, and tin metals. Mine or buy the base ores from guild vendors, then commission a smith.
  3. Moblinweave
    Clothcraft synthesis. Farm Moblin textile materials in Movalpolos or obtain them through Moblin-related drops, then ask a weaver to make the cloth.
  4. Rhodonite
    Mining, treasure/battlefield rewards, Goblin Box, or gem desynthesis provide non-AH routes.

Trade all four together. Reward: 55 slots.

Part VI — 55 → 60 slots

  1. Balloon Cloth
    Clothcraft synthesis from Moblin/Balloon textile drops. Farm the relevant Moblins in Movalpolos and commission a weaver.
  2. Shakudo Ingot
    Goldsmithing synthesis from copper and gold materials. Mine or purchase the base metals and have a goldsmith combine them.
  3. H.Q. Eft Skin
    Farm higher-level Efts, especially in Bibiki Bay and related Chains of Promathia areas. This is a direct drop, so Treasure Hunter helps.
  4. Iolite
    Obtain from higher-tier mining, treasure/battlefield rewards, Goblin Box, or gem-processing/desynthesis routes.

Trade all four together. Reward: 60 slots.

Part VII — 60 → 65 slots

  1. Lynx Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis from a Lynx Hide. Farm lynxes in Wings of the Goddess-era areas such as Meriphataud Mountains [S], then commission a leatherworker.
  2. Adaman Ingot
    High-level Smithing synthesis from adaman ore/materials. Mine or obtain the ore from high-level treasure and beastmen, then use an experienced smith.
  3. Rainbow Cloth
    High-level Clothcraft synthesis from Rainbow Thread. Farm or synthesize the thread and ask an experienced weaver to make the cloth.
  4. Deathstone
    High-tier gem obtained from mining, battlefields/treasure, Goblin Box, or goldsmithing/desynthesis sources.

Trade all four together. Reward: 65 slots.

Part VIII — 65 → 70 slots

  1. Smilodon Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis from a Smilodon Hide. Farm smilodons in Wings of the Goddess-era outdoor zones and have a leatherworker tan it.
  2. Electrum Ingot
    Goldsmithing synthesis from silver and gold materials. Mine or buy the base metals from guild vendors and commission a goldsmith.
  3. Cilice
    High-level Clothcraft synthesis. Gather the required specialty thread/fiber through drops or guild stock and use an experienced weaver.
  4. Angelstone
    High-tier gem from mining, battlefields/treasure, Goblin Box, or goldsmithing/desynthesis.

Trade all four together. Reward: 70 slots.

Part IX — 70 → 75 slots

  1. Orichalcum Ingot
    High-level Goldsmithing synthesis. Obtain orichalcum ore/material through mining, high-level treasure and battlefield sources, then commission an experienced goldsmith.
  2. Peiste Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis from a Peiste Skin. Farm peistes in Wings of the Goddess-era zones such as Pashhow Marshlands [S], then have the skin tanned.
  3. Oil-Soaked Cloth
    Specialty cloth obtained from high-level monster/treasure sources and related crafting routes. Check the Item Encyclopedia for the exact drops recorded by the Current Reality server build.
  4. Oxblood Orb
    Bonecraft synthesis from Oxblood. Obtain Oxblood from sea-creature and treasure sources, then ask an experienced boneworker to shape the orb.

Trade all four together. Reward: 75 slots.

Part X — 75 → 80 slots

  1. Molybdenum Ingot
    High-level Smithing synthesis from molybdenum ore/material. Obtain it through high-level mining, battlefields or treasure, then commission an experienced smith.
  2. Griffon Leather
    Leathercraft synthesis from a Griffon Hide. Farm griffons in Wings of the Goddess-era zones such as Grauberg [S], then have the hide tanned.
  3. Foulard
    High-level Clothcraft synthesis from specialty thread. Gather the thread through its crafting/drop route and use an experienced weaver.
  4. Angel Skin Orb
    Bonecraft synthesis from Angel Skin. Obtain Angel Skin from high-level sea-creature, fishing/Inside the Belly, battlefield, or treasure sources and commission a boneworker.

Trade all four together. Reward: 80 Inventory slots — the maximum Gobbiebag capacity.

Goblin Stew 880 shortcut

Goblin Stew 880 can replace the four-item set for any one Gobbiebag stage on versions of FFXI that support it. Trade one stew to Bluffnix after accepting the active part. Retail sources include Repeat Login Campaign rewards, the Green Thumb Moogle during applicable Mog Garden campaigns, and Pawkrix for gil.

Current Reality note: campaign availability and custom-server NPC stock can differ from retail. Confirm that Goblin Stew 880 exists on the server before planning around it; the four listed materials remain the normal route.

If Bluffnix will not continue

  • Accept first: speak to Bluffnix before trading.
  • Trade together: place all four required items in the same trade window.
  • Follow the order: the next part cannot begin until the previous part is complete.
  • Raise Jeuno fame: later parts require stronger Jeuno fame. Complete repeatable Jeuno-area quests, then return.
  • Make room: keep at least one open Inventory slot and re-zone if the next quest dialogue does not trigger.
  • Verify names: similar hides, cloths, ingots, gems, and orbs are not interchangeable.
