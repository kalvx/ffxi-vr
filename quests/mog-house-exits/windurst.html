Windurst exit unlock

Flower Child

Give Ojha Rhawash the flower she wants to unlock district selection when leaving your Windurst Mog House.

Start NPCOjha Rhawash
LocationWindurst Walls (C-13)
PrerequisitesNone
Required itemLilac ×1
RewardWindurst Mog House exit selection

Step-by-step

  1. Obtain one Lilac. Other flowers can trigger dialogue but will not complete the exit unlock.
  2. Travel to Windurst Walls (C-13) and speak with Ojha Rhawash.
  3. Trade exactly one Lilac to Ojha Rhawash and complete the event.
  4. Enter a Windurst Mog House, use the exit, and verify that you can choose a Windurst district.

Correct flower: the quest-completion check is for Lilac, not Marguerite or Amaryllis.
