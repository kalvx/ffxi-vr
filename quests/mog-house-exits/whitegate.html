Aht Urhgan Whitegate

Whitegate Mog House Exit

Whitegate does not have a separate Mog House exit unlock quest.

Quest requiredNone
NPC requiredNone
Item requiredNone
Exit destinationAht Urhgan Whitegate

How it works

  1. Gain access to Aht Urhgan Whitegate through normal Treasures of Aht Urhgan progression.
  2. Enter the Whitegate Mog House.
  3. Use the door to return to Aht Urhgan Whitegate. Because Whitegate is one city zone, there are no separate Whitegate districts to unlock.

Related but different: Mog Locker access and Imperial Bronze Piece lease extensions are separate systems. They do not unlock additional Whitegate exit destinations.
