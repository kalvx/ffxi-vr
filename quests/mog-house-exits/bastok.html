Bastok exit unlock

A Lady’s Heart

Bring Valah Molkot the correct flower to unlock district selection when leaving your Bastok Mog House.

Start NPCValah Molkot
LocationPort Bastok (J-12)
PrerequisitesNone
Required itemAmaryllis ×1
RewardBastok Mog House exit selection

Step-by-step

  1. Obtain one Amaryllis. Other flowers can be accepted during the dialogue, but they do not complete the unlock.
  2. Travel to Port Bastok (J-12) and speak with Valah Molkot.
  3. Trade exactly one Amaryllis to Valah Molkot and complete the event.
  4. Enter a Bastok Mog House, use the exit, and verify that the district-selection menu appears.

Item check: Current Reality’s LandSandBoat script specifically completes the quest for an Amaryllis; do not substitute a similarly named flower.
