Why complete the exit quests?

Each quest unlocks district selection for its own city. Complete the San d’Oria, Bastok, Windurst, and Jeuno quests to gain the full set of back-alley exit choices. The three starting-nation flower quests are also associated with the retail Mog House second-floor and Mog Safe 2 unlock; exact availability can depend on the Current Reality server revision.
