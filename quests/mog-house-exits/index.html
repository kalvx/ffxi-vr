Why complete all three nation quests?

Besides choosing any district when leaving that nation’s Mog House, completing the San d’Oria, Bastok, and Windurst exit quests is the retail prerequisite associated with unlocking the Mog House second floor and Mog Safe 2. Exact availability can depend on the Current Reality server revision.
