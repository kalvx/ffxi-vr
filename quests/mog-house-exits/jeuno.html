Jeuno Rent-a-Room

Jeuno Exit Selection

Jeuno does not use a flower-trade Mog House exit quest.

Quest requiredNone
NPC requiredNone
Item requiredNone
Available districtsLower Jeuno, Upper Jeuno, Port Jeuno, Ru’Lude Gardens

How to use it

  1. Enter a Jeuno Rent-a-Room from any Jeuno residential entrance.
  2. When leaving, choose the Jeuno district you want from the exit menu.
  3. If the expected options do not appear on Current Reality, re-enter once and test again. A persistent failure is a server/client behavior issue, not a missing Jeuno quest.

Why there is no quest walkthrough: Current Reality’s LandSandBoat exit flags are awarded by the three nation flower quests. There is no separate Jeuno exit quest script to complete.
