Jeuno exit unlock

Pretty Little Things

Bring Zona Shodhun the one “pretty little thing” she really wants to learn Jeuno’s back alleys and choose any Jeuno district when leaving your Rent-a-Room.

Start NPCZona Shodhun
LocationPort Jeuno (E-8)
PrerequisitesNone
Required itemYellow Rock ×1
RewardJeuno Mog House exit selection
Quest logJeuno → Pretty Little Things

Complete walkthrough

  1. Obtain exactly one Yellow Rock. The Auction House is the simplest place to check first. Yellow Rocks can also come from gardening, harvesting or mining results, certain monsters, and several battlefields.
  2. Travel to Port Jeuno (E-8) and find Zona Shodhun, the Mithra who explains gardening.
  3. Speak with Zona Shodhun to begin Pretty Little Things. Her dialogue hints that she wants something pretty but does not plainly name it.
  4. Trade one Yellow Rock to Zona Shodhun. The trade completes the quest and displays the message that you have learned your way through Jeuno’s back alleys.
  5. Enter any Jeuno Rent-a-Room and use its exit. You can now choose Lower Jeuno, Upper Jeuno, Port Jeuno, or Ru’Lude Gardens.

Use a Yellow Rock—not a flower and not another colored rock. Flowers and the red, blue, green, translucent, purple, black, or white rocks do not complete the quest. The server can consume incorrect gifts while the quest is active, so check the item name before trading.

If it does not complete

  • Confirm that the item is named Yellow Rock, not Yellow Stone, Earth Ore, or another yellow-colored item.
  • Trade only one Yellow Rock by itself.
  • Make sure you are trading to Zona Shodhun in Port Jeuno (E-8).
  • If the quest was already completed, further trades are not consumed; test the exit menu from a Jeuno Rent-a-Room.
