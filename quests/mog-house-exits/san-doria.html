San d’Oria exit unlock

Growing Flowers

Give Kuu Mohzolhi the flower she wants to unlock district selection when leaving your San d’Oria Mog House.

Start NPCKuu Mohzolhi
LocationNorthern San d’Oria (F-8)
PrerequisitesNone
Required itemMarguerite ×1
RewardSan d’Oria Mog House exit selection

Step-by-step

  1. Obtain one Marguerite. The quest accepts other flowers as gifts, but only a Marguerite completes this exit unlock.
  2. Travel to Northern San d’Oria (F-8) and speak with Kuu Mohzolhi, the young Mithra near the residential area.
  3. Trade exactly one Marguerite to Kuu Mohzolhi and finish the event.
  4. Enter a San d’Oria Mog House, use the exit, and confirm that you can choose among the city districts.

Do not confuse this with Exit the Gambler. That Aurege/Varchet quest awards EXP and a map; Current Reality’s server script grants the San d’Oria Mog House flag through Growing Flowers.
