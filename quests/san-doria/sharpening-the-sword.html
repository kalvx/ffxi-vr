San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Sharpening the Sword

The boy needs a whetstone to sharpen an old blade. Find one for him in Ordelle's Caves.

Start NPCAilbeche
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesJ-8
FameSan d'Oria
RepeatableYes
PreviousA Knight's Test · Father and Son
NextA Boy's Dream

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] Ordelle whetstone

Rewards

Honor Sword

Complete walkthrough

  1. In order to start this quest, it's necessary to trade back the fishing rod to Ailbeche from the previous quest Father and Son.
  2. Speak with Ailbeche near Home Point #2 to begin the quest.
    • He will ask you for an Ordelle whetstone.
  3. Talk to Sobane in (D-6) of Southern San d'Oria.
    • Be sure to talk to her twice if you get a cutscene the first time.
  4. Enter Ordelle's Caves from the entrance in La Theine Plateau position (F-7). This will put you on map 2.
    • Taking the survival guide to Ordelle's Caves is the fastest route.
  5. Take the first left and walk towards (I-6) on map 2 on to use exit B which will put you on map 1. Walk towards (H-9) exit D, which will put you back on map 2 again.
  6. Examine the Stalagmite in the circular room at (H-10) on map 2 to pop the NM Polevik an Earth Elemental.
    • Be careful not to fall down the hole in this room.
  7. Defeat the Polevik, then examine the Stalagmite again to obtain the key item: Ordelle whetstone.
  8. Return to Ailbeche to end the quest and receive your reward.
