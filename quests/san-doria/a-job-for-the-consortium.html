San d’Oria quest walkthrough

A Job for the Consortium

The Brugaire Consortium wants you to smuggle a package onto the airship and take it to the Tenshodo in Jeuno. Nighttime is the safest option.

Start NPCPortaure
Starting zonePort San d'Oria
CoordinatesH-9
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level5
RepeatableYes

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] Airship Pass

Rewards

1,000 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Portaure in the Cargo Room B near Port San d'Oria Home Point #1 to accept this quest and obtain [Key Item] Brugaire goods.
  2. Note: To guarantee going through customs undetected, perform the next step in Jeuno during nighttime hours (18:00-6:00).
    • An airship arriving in San d'Oria will reach Jeuno 3 hours later (1h to depart, 2h in the air).
  3. Travel to Port Jeuno.
    • Fastest method is to Home Point warp to Port Jeuno.
    • You must still go through Jeuno's Air Travel Agency before delivering to the Tenshodo or the package will be damaged, failing the quest.
    • You do not need to buy an airship ticket in San d'Oria.
    • Airship, Jeuno - San d'Oria is the original route.
  4. Speak to Haubijoux at the Air Travel Agency - Arrivals Entrance in Port Jeuno.
    • If you are caught, your airship pass is revoked until midnight Japan time and the quest is failed.
  5. If you cleared customs, go to the Tenshodo HQ in Lower Jeuno and speak to Yin Pocanakhu (J-8).
  6. Return to Portaure for your reward.

Notes

1. Completing this quest raises your fame in Norg. 2. Portuare's hint about waiting until nightfall and an updated quest description were added in the June 2016 update. - This quest update also removed completion of The Brugaire Consortium as a requirement.
