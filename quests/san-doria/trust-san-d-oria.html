San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Trust: San d'Oria

Gondebaud of the Jeuno Institute of Magical Studies has introduced you to the Trust initiative and asked you to visit Excenmille in Northern San d'Oria.

Start NPCGondebaud
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
FameSan d'Oria
TitleThe Trustworthy

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Red institute card
San d'Oria Trust permit
Able to use San'dorian Trust Spells
Trust Spell: Excenmille

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gondebaud in Southern San d'Oria at (L-6), he will give you the Red institute card.
  2. Head to Northern San d'Oria and speak to Excenmille at (D-9).
    • If this is not your first Trust quest that you are completing, at this point you will be finished with the quest and receive the San d'Oria Trust permit.
    • If this is your first Trust quest, you must use the Trust magic spell "Excenmille" in either West Ronfaure or East Ronfaure. After, return to Excenmille for the San d'Oria Trust permit.
