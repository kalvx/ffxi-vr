San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Forest for the Trees

The overworked Ramua asks for your aid in collecting timber samples for a guild survey that she's been too busy to conduct herself.

Start NPCRamua
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesE-3
FameSan d'Oria

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Trainee Axe

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Ramua in Northern San d'Oria (E-3) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
    • You need to have already signed up with the Woodworking Guild for this quest to be activated. Speak to Guild Master: Cheupirudaux - Northern San d'Oria (F-3)
  2. Ramua will give you the [Key Item] Timber survey checklist and a Hatchet.
  3. Travel to Jugner Forest and log the following items:
    • Arrowwood Log
    • Ash Log
    • Yew Log
    • Willow Log
    • Walnut Log
  4. Trade these logs to Ramua, for your reward. She will also return the logs to you.

Notes

1. For each item on the list that you log, you will get a message that data was recorded on your checklist. It appears that you must acquire these items yourself, you cannot buy them from shops or the auction house.
