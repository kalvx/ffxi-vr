San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Her Majesty's Garden

Bring some Derfland humus to use as fertilizer so that flowers might grow in the late queen's garden.

Start NPCChalvatot
Starting zoneChateau d'Oraguille
CoordinatesF-7
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level4

Required items and key items

  • Derfland Humus

Rewards

Map of the Northlands area (If you do not already own it)
2,000 Experience Points
2,000 Gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Chalvatot to begin this quest.
  2. Trade Chalvatot a Derfland Humus to complete the quest.
    • Derfland Humus drops off Doom Scorpions in the Crawlers' Nest. It can also be purchased off the Auction House.
