San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Signed in Blood

Sobane has heard some disturbing news from her husband, the head royal chef. She wishes to make a request concerning that tale, but first requires you to bring a "cathedral tapestry" to prove your integrity.

Start NPCSobane
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesD-6
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level3
NextTea with a Tonberry?

Required items and key items

  • Cathedral Tapestry
  • [Key Item] Torn-out pages

Rewards

Cunning Earring
3,500 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Sobane who will request you bring her a Cathedral Tapestry.
    • Cathedral Tapestry drops off Orcish Fighters in Fort Ghelsba or the Yughott Grotto.
  2. Trade the Cathedral Tapestry to Sobane.
  3. Travel to Selbina and speak to Abelard (G-9).
  4. Head to Ordelle's Caves obtain an Ordelle Chest Key off the monsters inside.
    • Certain monsters in the cave drop the key. To reach that area, take path D on map 1, drop into the southeastern hole depicted on map 2, then take path E.
    • The high-level mobs Buds Bunny, Swagger Spruce, Targe Beetle do not aggro.
  5. Use the key on a chest inside to receive [Key Item] Torn-out pages.
  6. Return to Abelard.
  7. Speak to Sobane to complete this quest.
