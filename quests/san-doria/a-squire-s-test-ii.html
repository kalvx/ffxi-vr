San d’Oria quest walkthrough

A Squire's Test II

Journey to the waterfall in Ordelle's Caves, and bring back proof of your visit.

Start NPCBalasiel
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesF-7
FameSan d'Oria
RepeatableNo
PreviousA Squire's Test
NextA Knight's Test
TitleSpelunker

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

[Key Item] Squire certificate

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Balasiel to start the quest.
  2. Proceed to Ordelle's Caves at map 2 (G-7).
    • The fastest route here is to use the Proto-Waypoint warp to La Theine Plateau. Turn around to enter Ordelle's Caves, then travel north until you reach the map.
    • An alternative route is to go to La Theine Plateau (H-7), and enter Ordelle's Caves.
    • You will be on map 1 (K-3). Head to (I-5) a room with Fly Agarics.
    • Take the southwest passage (to Point C, G/H-6) which leads you to map 2. On map 2, proceed to the large room at (G-7).
    • You may also use the Ordelle's Caves Survival Guide, which will put you on map 2 (G-2).
    • Head west to map 1 (Point A, G-4) then head to (I-5) a room with Fly Agarics.
    • Take the southwest passage (to Point C, G/H-6) which leads you to map 2. On map 2, proceed to the large room at (G-7).
  3. Examine the ??? in the pool of water, which will read "You place your hands into the pool." Quickly move towards the middle of the room and examine the ??? for the [Key Item] Stalactite dew.
    • If you do not check the second ??? quickly enough, you will have to try again.
  4. Speak to Balasiel to complete the quest.
