San d’Oria quest walkthrough

A Timely Visit

A criminal suspect has been spotted on La Theine Plateau. You have been asked to assist in his capture.

Start NPCDeraquien
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesG-8

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Medieval Collar

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Deraquien for a cutscene.
  2. Go to La Theine Plateau and speak with Narvecaint (F-7) for another cutscene.
    • Voidwatch warp to Ordelle's Caves will put you right next to the NPC.
  3. Return to Deraquien and speak to him again.
  4. Head to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak to Halver.
    • If you just cleared The Voracious Resurgence, you will have to rezone after the cutscene you get and talk to him again.
  5. Speak to Deraquien again.
  6. Speak to Phillone (D-7).
  7. Go back to speak with Deraquien.
  8. Head to Jugner Forest through King Ranperre's Tomb.
    • If you have access to the Proto-Waypoint, this is the fastest method of reaching the location.
  9. Click on the ??? at (F-5) between 18:00 and 4:00 to spawn two NMs; Giollemitte B Feroun and Skeleton Esquire.
    • Both NMs must be defeated to proceed.
  10. Once defeated, click on the ??? again for a cutscene.
  11. Return to Phillone for a cutscene.
  12. Speak to Narvecaint (F-7) in La Theine Plateau.
  13. Return to Phillone to receive your reward.
