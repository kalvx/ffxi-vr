San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Growing Flowers

Give the tiny Mithra some flowers so she can plant them in Queen Leaute's garden. Perhaps something white?

Start NPCKuu Mohzolhi
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesF-8
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level1
RepeatableNo

Required items and key items

  • Marguerite

Rewards

Exit to any zone within the city of San d'Oria from Mog House.
This also allows you to to warp to your second floor and Mog Garden if you've unlocked those.

Complete walkthrough

  1. (optional) Talk to Kuu Mohzolhi.
  2. She won't come out and say it at first, but she would like a Marguerite.
  3. Marguerite can be bought off auction houses, or Areebah in Upper Jeuno at (H-6).
  4. Trade it to her to complete the quest.
    • If you trade the wrong flower for the other 2 quests she will take it and you will have to acquire another.
