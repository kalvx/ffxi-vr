San d’Oria quest walkthrough

To Cure a Cough

Bring Amaura some thyme moss, and she'll concoct a brew with curative powers.

Start NPCNenne
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesF-6
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level3
RepeatableNo
PreviousThe Medicine Woman
TitleA Moss Kind Person

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

3,000 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Nenne, Southern San d'Oria (F-6)
    • Important note: this quest will not immediately appear on your quest log until the actual 'item hunt' portion of the quest has been activated (see below).
  2. Once you have heard Nenne's story, go into the first house on Watchdog Alley (G-7) and go upstairs into a bedroom and on the table there is a ???. Click this and the option to read a diary will appear. Finish reading the diary entirely (continue paging through) to continue to the next portion.
  3. Speak to Amaura (G-6) who will ask for Thyme moss.
    • To get the moss, you need to examine a ??? at (F-9) in Davoi.
  4. Return the moss to Amaura who will give you Cough medicine.
  5. Speak to Nenne who will give you a Scroll of treasure as your reward.
  6. The scroll leads you to the signpost at (H-6) in East Ronfaure.
  7. Examine the signpost for 3,000 gil.
