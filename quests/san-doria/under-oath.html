San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Under Oath

Knights whisper of a traitor in their midst. An outsider is needed to investigate.

Start NPCPrince Trion
Starting zoneChateau d'Oraguille
CoordinatesH-8
FameSan d'Oria
RepeatableNo
PreviousA Boy's Dream
TitleParagon of Paladin Excellence

Required items and key items

  • Zvahl Coffer Key
  • [Key Item] Mique's paintbrush
  • [Key Item] Strange sheet of paper

Rewards

Gallant Surcoat

Complete walkthrough

  1. Examine the door to Prince Trions room (Prince Royal's Rm) for a cutscene while you are a Paladin.
    • If you were a Paladin when you completed A Boy's Dream, you may have already received this cutscene.
  2. The rest of this quest can be completed as any job from this point on.
  3. Speak to Vemalpeau, Southern San d'Oria (M-7), inside a house.
  4. Speak to Najjar, Southern San d'Oria (K-6) inside lion springs who will mention (Key Item) Mique's paintbrush.
  5. Speak to Ullasa, Southern San d'Oria (B-6) inside the count's manor who will mention (Key Item) Mique's paintbrush.
  6. Obtain a Zvahl Coffer Key and trade it to any coffer in Castle Zvahl Baileys to get (Key Item) Mique's paintbrush.
    • The coffer key drops off Ahriman, Abyssal Demon, Arch Demon, Blood Demon, and Doom Demon. Conveniently, these mobs are located very nearby all of the coffer spawn locations.
    • Or come as a Thief and picking the coffer lock with either Thief's Tools, Living Key, or Skeleton Key.
    • Or purchase the key from the Curio Vendor Moogle for 5,000 gil if you have the "Rhapsody in Umber" KI.
  7. Return to Vemalpeau.
  8. Head to the second story in Vemalpeau's house and examine the ??? on the painting. Look behind the painting and read the letter for (Key Item) Strange sheet of paper.
  9. Speak to Exoroche (K-7) in Helbort's Blades who will tell you how to read the letter.
  10. Head to Davoi and locate the Village Well at (G-9).
  11. Examine the Village Well will spawn 2 NMs:
    • Three-eyed Prozpuz (RNG)
    • One-eyed Gwajboj (PLD)
    • Drops the Well Weight you need to continue this quest.
    • Only one Well Weight drops at a time. If you are doing this for several Paladins, you will have to wait 3 minutes after defeating them to do the fight for each of them.
  12. Trade the Well Weight to the Village Well for a cutscene and (Key Item) Knight's confession.
  13. Speak to Ailbeche, Northern San d'Oria (J-9).
  14. Travel to Jugner Forest and head for Maiden's Spring (E-6). When you get close to the spring a cutscene will start.
  15. Finally, head back to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak to Prince Trion for your reward.
  16. Optional component:
  17. You may additionally talk to Ailbeche, Exoroche, and Vemalpeau for more quest-related dialogue.
    • Vemalpeau will take your Knight's confession key item.
