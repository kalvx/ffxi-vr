San d’Oria quest walkthrough

The General's Secret

Fetch some water from the hot spring on the peak of Horlais.

Start NPCCurilla
Starting zoneChateau d'Oraguille
CoordinatesI-9
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level2

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Lynx Baghnakhs

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Curilla and agree to help her to receive [Key Item] Curilla's bottle.
  2. Head to Horlais Peak by going through Yughott Grotto.
  3. Once in Horlais Peak, head to the Hot Springs in the springs and check it to get [Key Item] Curilla's bottle again. To confirm you filled it, because the name doesn't change, observe the text description of the item under "Temporary Items". It should read "The bottle from general Curilla. It is full of water from the hot spring that flows from the Horlais Peak"
  4. Return to Curilla for your reward.
