San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Thick Shells

Collect five beetle shells suitable for making medicine.

Start NPCVounebariont
Starting zonePort San d'Oria
CoordinatesI-10
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level2
RepeatableYes
TitleBug Catcher

Required items and key items

  • 5 Beetle Shells

Rewards

750 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Vounebariont to begin this quest.
  2. Trade Vounebariont 5 Beetle Shells to complete this quest.
  3. 5-6 Stacks of Beetle Shells increase fame from 1~ to 9 Max Fame.
