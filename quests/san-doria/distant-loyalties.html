San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Distant Loyalties

A goldsmith is needed to fill an order. Only Elvaan need apply.

Start NPCFemitte
Starting zoneSouthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesI-8
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level4
RepeatableNo

Required items and key items

  • Mythril Ingot

Rewards

White Cape

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Femitte, who needs a goldsmithing order fulfilled. She requests an Elvaan specifically.
    • She will provide you with a Goldsmithing order.
  2. Head to Bastok Markets (HP#1), and look for Michea at (F-10). She's located upstairs inside Brunhilde the Armorer's shop.
  3. Michea requests a Mythril Ingot. After trading it to her, you must zone out of Bastok Markets once.
  4. Zone back into Bastok Markets and return to Michea to receive [Key Item] Mythril hearts.
  5. Travel back to Southern San d'Oria and talk to Femitte to complete the quest.
