San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Escort for Hire (San d'Oria)

You are to escort Cannau to her desired destination within the Eldieme Necropolis.

Start NPCRondipur
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesF-6
FameSan d'Oria

RepeatableYes

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

10,000 gil (for first time completion)
Page from Miratete's Memoirs

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rondipur to begin this quest.
  2. Head to Eldieme Necropolis from Batallia Downs (I-10).
  3. As soon as you zone in you will receive a cutscene.
  4. Once the cutscene is over, an NPC named Cannau will spawn.
    • You will have 30 minutes to complete this quest.
  5. She will begin to run West towards the door at (H-8). You will either need a [Key Item] Magicked astrolabe, or to bring someone with you, to flip the switch to let you through this door.
    • Speaking to Cannau will make her stop running. Talking to her again will make her continue.
    • Cannau will draw aggro from just about everything inside Eldieme Necropolis. You need to stop her and kill everything in her path.
    • If her HP drops too low she will blood aggro any undead near by.
    • She can be cured, so pay attention to her health.
  6. After you get past the gate at (H-8), Cannau will continue to (F-8) taking either the North or South path.
  7. Once she reaches (F-8) she will stop on her own.
  8. Quickly speak to her to obtain the [Key Item] Completion certificate.
    • If she disappears before you talk to her you will not get credit for escorting her.
  9. Return to Rondipur for your reward.
