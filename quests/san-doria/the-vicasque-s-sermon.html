San d’Oria quest walkthrough

The Vicasque's Sermon

Deliver blue peas to a friar on retreat. Find him in a tower in the southeast area of East Ronfaure.

Start NPCAbioleget
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesM-7
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level2
RepeatableNo
PreviousWaters of the Cheval
TitleThe Benevolent One

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Brass Ring

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Abioleget, who needs you to deliver Blue Peas.
  2. Trade him 70 gil to receive the peas.
  3. Look for Andelain in East Ronfaure at (J-11).
  4. After giving him the peas, return to Abioleget for your reward.
