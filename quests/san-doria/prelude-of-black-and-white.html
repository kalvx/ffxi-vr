San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Prelude of Black and White

White mages of renown will be given a blessed item for the war against the undead. But first, they must bring Yagudo holy water to prove their worth.

Complete walkthrough

  1. As a White Mage, examine the Door: Prince Regent's Rm in Chateau d'Oraguille at (H-8) to start this quest.
    • You must be able to access Chateau d'Oraguille.
    • Players from San d'Oria must be rank 2, but players from other nations must be on Bastok / Windurst Mission 2-3.
  2. Go to Castle Oztroja or Castle Zvahl Keep and kill Yagudo Abbots until a Yagudo Holy Water drops.
  3. Yagudo Abbots spawn behind the brass door at (G-8) on map 3.
    • The combination to these levers can be found behind the secret door at (I-10) on the same map.
    • Yagudo Abbots located at (G-9) & (G-10) on map 2 in Castle Zvahl Keep can also drop Yagudo Holy Water. Although fewer of them spawn in this zone, they are 1-7 levels lower than Yagudo Abbots in Castle Oztroja.
  4. Find a pair of Moccasins
    • Purchasable from the Auction House
    • Dropped from Multiple Enemies
    • Synthesized from a level 59 Leathercraft synth
  5. Take the pair of Moccasins and the Yagudo Holy Water to Narcheral (M-6) in Northern San d'Oria to complete this quest and obtain your Healer's Duckbills.
