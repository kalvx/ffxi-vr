San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Exit the Gambler

Varchet of the Carpenters' Guild has run off again. Find the slacker and get him back to work.

Start NPCAurege

Required items and key items

  • Various amount of Gil

Rewards

Map of King Ranperre's Tomb (If you do not already own it)
2,000 Experience Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. Start the quest by talking to Aurege (F-3) near Northern San d'Oria Home Point #4, outside of the Carpenter's Guild.
  2. (Optional): Talk to Nonterene (I-10) at the Parade Grounds near Victory Arch.
  3. Varchet is located in Southern San d'Oria at (L-6) right next to the fountains.
  4. You'll need to gamble against him. Each attempt against him costs 5 gil. Keep trading until you win.
    • Gambling against him is still possible after this quest is complete.
  5. After winning, return and talk to Aurege for your reward.

Notes

1. This quest does not appear in your log until it is completed. 2. Exit the Gambler was taken a bit literally when Varchet was temporarily removed from the game in an emergency maintenance in September 2007. - A Moogle was put in his spot, who would inform adventurers that Varchet fell into financial ruin or had the flu or something and couldn't gamble. - This action was taken due to an exploit where his gambling mini-game could be manipulated by 3rd-party tools, causing an infinite gil glitch. - Varchet returned to his usual spot to try to win his fortune back a few weeks later.
