San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Knight Stalker

Rahal wants you to gather information regarding the attacks on the Royal Knights.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rahal in Chateau d'Oraguille at (H-9) inside the Royal Knight Quarters as a Dragoon to begin this quest.
  2. Speak to Ceraulian, Port San d'Oria (I-10) inside Cargo Room A for a cutscene.
  3. Speak to Ceraulian once more after the cutscene.
  4. Obtain a Kuftal Coffer Key. Use the key to open any coffer in Kuftal Tunnel. This will give you (Key Item): Challenge to the Royal Knights.
    • Use a Survival Guide or UNM warp to reach the area.
  5. Return to Rahal and speak to him again.
  6. Speak to Balasiel, Southern San d'Oria (F-7)
  7. Speak to Rahal again.
  8. Head to Temple of Uggalepih for a NM fight.
    • NOTE: Make sure atleast one person present is on Dragoon as having a wyvern out is a requirement to spawn the NM. Other people can be on any job and still get the reward after the fight.
    • The survival guide is most likely the fastest warp available
  9. Once inside, head to (F-10) on the first map to find a ???.
    • This spot is on a hidden area of the map at (F-9).
  10. Click the ??? with your wyvern summoned to spawn two NMs; Cleuvarion M Resoaix and Rompaulion S Citalle. The NMs will not spawn unless your wyvern is out with you.
    • Only Rompaulion S Citalle needs to be defeated to continue.
    • The battle can be handled with little difficulty at level 60 with a full party of Trusts.
  11. Once the NM's are dead, check the ??? again for a cutscene and your reward.
  12. Although it is not necessarry, you can return to Ceraulian after defeating the NM's for an additional cutscene.
  13. Rahal will also have some additional dialogue for you after the fight.
