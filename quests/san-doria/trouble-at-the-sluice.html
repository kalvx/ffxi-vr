San d’Oria quest walkthrough

Trouble at the Sluice

It seems poison was thrown into the water near the sluice gates. Find an antidote before someone falls ill.

Start NPCBelgidiveau
Starting zoneNorthern San d'Oria
CoordinatesG-8
FameSan d'Oria
Fame level3
RepeatableNo
PreviousThe Rumor

Required items and key items

  • Dahlia

Rewards

Heavy Axe

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Belgidiveau to begin the quest.
  2. Speak to Novalmauge, Bostaunieux Oubliette (G-8).
  3. Trade Novalmauge a Dahlia to receive [Key Item] Neutralizer.
  4. Return to Belgidiveau to complete this quest.
