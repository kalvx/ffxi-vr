Bastok quest walkthrough

Love and Ice

Carmelo wants to start his life anew. You must go throw the song sheet he had written for his former love into the Mirror Lake on the Beaucedine Glacier.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Locate the [Key Item] Carrier pigeon letter in your permanent key items and "examine" it.
    • This is the key item reward from the pre-req quest: The Stars of Ifrit. Without this key item you may not continue.
    • You receive this key item before the end of The Stars of Ifrit, and thus you do not need to complete that quest before starting this one.
  2. Speak with Carmelo inside the Steaming Sheep to receive the actual quest. After a cutscene, you will receive [Key Item] Carmelo's song sheet.
    • If you have The Siren's Tear active. You will get a prompt to ask him about the Siren's Tear instead.
  3. Travel to G-10 in Beaucedine Glacier (at the ice pond) and click on Mirror Pond which only appears during days without snow or clouds.
    • "Gloom" single dark weather with sun is fine.
    • This location can be reached quickly using the Beaucedine Glacier Voidwatch warp.
  4. After the cutscene, return to Bastok and speak to Carmelo to complete quest
