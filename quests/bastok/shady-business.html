Bastok quest walkthrough

Shady Business

Talib of the Tenshodo is buying zinc. Bring him four chunks of the ore to receive gil.

Start NPCTalib
Starting zonePort Bastok
CoordinatesF-6
FameNorg
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
PreviousBeauty and the Galka

Required items and key items

  • Zinc Ore ×4

Rewards

350g

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Talib to start quest.
    • Homepoint #3 is the closest
  2. Obtain 4 Zinc Ore via:
    • Teerth in Bastok Markets for 200 Gil (HP #4)
    • Celestina in Mhaura for 200 Gil
    • The Auction House
    • By Mining
    • Mineral Vein
  3. Trade 4 Zinc Ore to Talib to complete the quest.

Notes

1. This is one of the best and fastest way to earn Tenshodo Fame. Zoning is not required to repeat the quest. 2. After the February 2014 version update, 20 quest completions are required to max Tenshodo Fame from 1.
