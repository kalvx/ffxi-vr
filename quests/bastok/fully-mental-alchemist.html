Bastok quest walkthrough

Fully Mental Alchemist

An eccentric alchemical innovator has beseeched your aid in completing his revolutionary research. Procure twenty grains of gold dust from Grauberg and deliver them back to his laboratory for your reward.

Start NPCTitus
Starting zoneBastok Mines
CoordinatesL-7
FameBastok
Fame level1

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Trainee Sword

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Titus on the second floor of the Alchemy Guild in Bastok Mines for a cutscene that begins the quest.
    • You will receive the [Key Item] Prospector's pan and [Key Item] Corked ampoule.
  2. Travel to Grauberg (S) and find the Riverbed at (F-13).
    • Fastest travel method is the North Gustaberg (S) Survival Guide
  3. When you examine the Riverbed, you will receive the following options:
    • A sizable portion
    • A moderate portion
    • A small portion
  4. When you select one, you will then receive the following options:
    • Wash vigorously.
    • Wash thoroughly.
    • Wash gently.
  5. To extract gold, you must wash away as much sediment as possible without washing away any gold dust. The results are random, but generally choosing "A sizable portion", "Wash vigorously", "Wash thoroughly", then selecting "Wash gently" over and over again will work. Repeat washing until you you get the following message added to the options:
    • Wash carefully to extract gold.
  6. Choose that and you will gain a random amount of gold dust grains (generally between 3 to 11). Repeat examining the Riverbed and the washing steps until you get at least 20 grains of gold, and thusly an [Key Item] Ampoule of gold dust.
    • If you leave Grauberg (S), you will lose any gold dust that you have acquired and will have to start over.
  7. Speak to Titus again for a cutscene to receive your reward.
