Bastok quest walkthrough

Altana's Sorrow

Virnage needs a bucket of divine paint to restore the statue of Altana to Her former glory. He advised you to look for it in a citadel on the Sauromugue Champaign.

Start NPCVirnage
Starting zoneBastok Mines
CoordinatesI-5
FameBastok
Fame level4
TitlePilgrim to Dem

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] Bucket of divine paint

Rewards

Scroll of Teleport-Dem

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak with Virnage, located at (I-5) in the Bat's Lair Inn on the second level of Ore Street of Bastok Mines, to begin quest.
  2. Travel to Garlaige Citadel and locate a ??? at (H-8) (First floor Map 1).
    • It's in a room with a bunch of bats so if you're at aggro level, make sure you have sneak up.
  3. Touch the ??? to receive a [Key Item] Bucket of divine paint.
  4. Return to Bastok and speak with Virnage. You will receive a [Key Item] Letter from Virnage.
  5. Travel to Northern San d'Oria and speak with Eperdur at M-7 (Cathedral) to complete quest.
