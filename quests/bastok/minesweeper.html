Bastok quest walkthrough

Minesweeper

Rid the Zeruhn Mines of a few monsters to keep them off the miners' backs. Bring three pinches of Zeruhn soot to Gerbaum as proof of your success.

Start NPCGerbaum
Starting zoneBastok Mines
CoordinatesF-8
FameBastok
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
TitleZeruhn Sweeper

Required items and key items

  • Zeruhn Soot ×3

Rewards

150 Gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Gerbaum asks you to exterminate the pests that roam inside Zeruhn Mines.
  2. Collect 3 Zeruhn Soot either by killing the monsters in Zeruhn Mines, or by buying them off the auction house.
  3. Trade them to Gerbaum for your reward.
