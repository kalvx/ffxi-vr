Bastok quest walkthrough

Cid's Secret

Do Cid a personal favor. Go see if there is any way you can help the manager of the Steaming Sheep Restaurant.

Start NPCCid
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesH-8
FameBastok
Fame level4
RepeatableNo
NextThe Usual

Required items and key items

  • Rolanberry 874

Rewards

Ram Mantle

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Cid to begin the Quest.
  2. Travel to Port Bastok (Home Point #3) and visit the Steaming Sheep Restaurant.
  3. Locate Hilda at (E-6) and speak with her. You can interact with her through the floor if you stand under her (near the bar) while she's upstairs.
  4. Obtain a Rolanberry 874 in one of two ways:
    • Via the Auction House. (Food ➞ Ingredients)
    • Defeating the Drone Crawler () in Crawler's Nest. (Survival Guide ➞ Derfland Region)
  5. Trade the Rolanberry 874 to Hilda and to receive [Key Item] Unfinished letter.
  6. Return to Cid and speak with him to complete the Quest.
