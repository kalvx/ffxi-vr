Bastok quest walkthrough

Mom, the Adventurer?

Bring Nbu Latteh's daughter a copper ring as a gift from her mother.

Start NPCNbu Latteh
Starting zoneBastok Markets
CoordinatesJ-9
FameBastok
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
NextThe Signpost Marks the Spot
TitleRingbearer

Required items and key items

  • Copper Ring

Rewards

100g or 200g

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Nbu Latteh on Gold Street to start quest.
    • She will give you a Fire Crystal you can use to craft your Copper Ring if you choose.
  2. Obtain a Copper Ring either via Auction House, Merchants or Crafting.
  3. Trade Copper Ring to Roh Latteh in Bastok Mines (H-7), she is located on the first floor house on Ore Street.
  4. Upon NPC dialogue completion, you will obtain [Key Item] Letter from Roh Latteh.
  5. If you 'check' the letter (as in read it from your Key Item menu) you will receive a penalty and get only half the actual reward.
  6. Return to Nbu Latteh to complete quest.
  7. This quest is repeatable for players below Bastok fame level 2. Zone out each time and you can re-do it.
