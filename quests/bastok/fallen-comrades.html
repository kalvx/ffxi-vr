Bastok quest walkthrough

Fallen Comrades

Bring Pavvke the name tags of the Galka who lost their lives in the Palborough Mines twenty years ago.

Start NPCPavvke
Starting zoneBastok Mines
CoordinatesI-7
FameBastok
Fame level2
RepeatableYes

Required items and key items

  • Silver Name Tag

Rewards

550 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Pavvke, who asks of you to bring back name tags.
  2. Silver Name Tags can be bought off auction houses. Alternately, Onyx Quadav or Greater Quadav will drop them in Palborough Mines.
  3. Since the tags are rare, you can only turn one in at a time.
  4. Trade it to him for your reward.
