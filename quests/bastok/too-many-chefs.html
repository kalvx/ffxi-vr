Bastok quest walkthrough

Too Many Chefs

The new apprentice cook is being blamed for everything that goes wrong in the eatery, and today doesn't seem to be any different...

Start NPCFerghus
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesG-9
FameBastok
Fame level5

Required items and key items

  • Red Oven Mitt

Rewards

Aileen's Delight

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Ferghus for a cutscene. He can be found in in the Craftsmen's Eatery, south of Cid's workshop.
    • Metalworks HP#1 is the closest.
  2. Speak to Leonhardt for another cutscene.
  3. Go to Bastok Markets (S) and speak to Raginmund near Harmodio's Music Shop (L-10).
  4. Obtain a Red Oven Mitt from Quadavs in Grauberg (S).
  5. Trade the Red Oven Mitt to Leonhardt for a brief cutscene.
  6. Speak to Umberto in Bastok Markets near Harmodio's Music Shop (L-10) for a cutscene and your reward.
