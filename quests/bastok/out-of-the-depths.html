Bastok quest walkthrough

Out of the Depths

A Goblin lair known as Movalpolos has suddenly appeared in North Gustaberg. Ayame wishes you to investigate this lair with the cooperation of a visiting scholar waiting in Port Bastok.

Start NPCAyame
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesK-7
FameBastok
Fame level3
NextA Question of Faith
TitleTrash Collector

Required items and key items

  • Hoary Bomb Ash ×11

Rewards

1,200 to 2,200 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Ayame for a starting cut-scene, agree twice to start the quest.
  2. Go to Port Bastok and talk with Ravorara (E-7) outside the Steaming Sheep Restaurant for a cutscene.
  3. Go to Oldton Movalpolos and talk to Brakobrik (K-10).
    • Closest warp is via Proto-Waypoint to the Geomagnetic Fount at (K-11) if you have it.
  4. Kill Ancient Bombs in Oldton Movalpolos to get Hoary Bomb Ash. At least 1 ash will always drop from a bomb, with up to 3 dropping with Treasure Hunter. Obtain 11 bomb ash in total.
  5. Trade between 1 to 4 Hoary Bomb Ash to Brakobrik at (K-11) to receive 4 different key items. You can only have one of each key item; trading the same amount of ashes twice will just waste the ashes. He'll offer you the following key items in exchange for the ash:
    • Trade 1 ash for the [Key Item] Dusty tome - (100 gil from Ravorara)
    • Trade 2 ash for the [Key Item] Pointed jug - (200 gil from Ravorara)
    • Trade 3 ash for the [Key Item] Cracked club - (300 gil from Ravorara)
    • Trade 4 ash for the [Key Item] Peeling hairpin - (400 gil from Ravorara)
  6. After getting the 4 key items, return to Port Bastok and talk to Ravorara four (4) times, once for each key item. You will see a cutscene and receive gil for each key item, as listed above.
  7. Return to Oldton Movalpolos and trade the final/11th ash to Brakobrik at (K-11), but choose "No Thanks" when he offers to give you something in return. He'll give you the [Key Item] Old nametag.
  8. Return to Port Bastok and talk with Ravorara for another cutscene.
  9. Go to Bastok Mines and talk with Pavvke in his residence on the lower level (I-7) for a cutscene, completing the quest and receiving your reward.
