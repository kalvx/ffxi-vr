Bastok quest walkthrough

Out of One's Shell

Ronan wants you to help him get his son, Corann, "to come out of his shell."

Start NPCRonan
Starting zonePort Bastok
CoordinatesK-6
FameBastok
Fame level2
RepeatableNo
PreviousThe Quadav's Curse
TitleShell Outer

Required items and key items

  • Shell Bug ×3

Rewards

Monk's Headgear

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Ronan to activate quest.
  2. Obtain 3 Shell Bugs from Quadavs or the Auction House, and trade them to Ronan.
    • Shell bugs can be obtained from the Green Thumb Moogle if you have a Baby eft memento (Goods and rank one and two creatures).
  3. Zone out (leave the area) and back in again, then speak to Ronan to complete quest.
