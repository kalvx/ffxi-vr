Bastok quest walkthrough

Ghosts of the Past

You are to go to Gusgen Mines and pass the same trial as Oggbi's former student, Cornelia. Use a pickaxe on a certain hole in the mines to lure the Wandering Ghost out. Defeat it and bring back its "miner's pendant."

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Oggbi inside the Steaming Sheep Restaurant for a cutscene.
    • You must be on Monk to receive this cutscene. Once the quest is active you can complete it on any job.
    • If you have over 250 H2H Skill he will offer you the Asuran Fists trial first.
  2. Get a Pickaxe from the Auction House or from any source.
    • View the Pickaxe page for merchants.
    • Recommended to get Pickaxes from Numa (E-7) he's in the store right in front of Steaming Sheep Restaurant down the stairs.
  3. Travel to Gusgen Mines. Go to the 2nd map by going straight ahead, down the staircase, and use the west switch to open the Door.
  4. Get to the 3rd map by taking the ramp down at (F-5) and trade a Pickaxe to the ??? at (G-7) to spawn Wandering Ghost.
    • Repop is about 3 minutes if doing multiple.
  5. Wandering Ghost drops a Miner's Pendant and the Pickaxe is still in your inventory.
  6. Trade the Miner's Pendant to Oggbi and receive Beat Cesti.
