Bastok quest walkthrough

Drachenfall

Black Mud has asked you to fetch him some Drachenfall water from North Gustaberg.

Start NPCBlack Mud
Starting zoneBastok Mines
CoordinatesJ-7
FameBastok
Fame level2
TitleDrachenfall Ascetic

Required items and key items

  • Drachenfall Water

Rewards

2000g

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak with Black Mud to begin quest. You will obtain a Brass Canteen.
  2. Travel to Dangruf Wadi and head north from the entrance.
    • The quickest method is via Survival Guide, otherwise zone in from South Gustaberg (D-9).
    • Invisible or Prism Powders are recommended as there are aggressive level 90 Goblins along the way.
  3. Once inside Dangruf Wadi, travel to (I-8) and go up the geyser by standing on top of it until being shot up the ledge.
  4. Head North East towards (J-3) to go up a second geyser.
  5. Continue following this path and you will zone out into North Gustaberg
  6. Travel easterly when you zone out and cross the bridge.
  7. Continue following the river towards the base of the waterfall.
  8. Locate the Waterfall Base and trade the Brass Canteen to it to receive Drachenfall Water
    • There is a ??? nearby, it is for unlocking the relic weapon Apocalypse, and not for this quest.
  9. Return to Bastok and trade water to Black Mud to complete the quest.

Notes

1. There is a headstone for the quest An Explorer's Footsteps along the way. You may wish to complete that at the same time.
