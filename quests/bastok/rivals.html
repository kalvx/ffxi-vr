Bastok quest walkthrough

Rivals

Lend Detzo a mythril sallet so he can win a contest against his rival.

Start NPCDetzo
Starting zoneBastok Mines
CoordinatesI-6
FameBastok
Fame level3
RepeatableNo
TitleContest Rigger

Required items and key items

  • Mythril Sallet

Rewards

Wolf Gorget

Complete walkthrough

  1. Obtain a Mythril Sallet from the auction house (Armor ➞ Head) or for 437 Sparks.
  2. Speak to Detzo to begin quest.
  3. Trade Sallet to Detzo to complete quest and receive your reward.
    • You will also have returned to you the Sallet, so make sure you have space in your inventory!
