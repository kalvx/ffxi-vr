Bastok quest walkthrough

The Gustaberg Tour

You have been asked to form a party with two or more people of level fifteen and below to test Izabele's business idea. Your party is to make its way to Drachenfall in North Gustaberg where Hunting Bear, Izabele's partner, is waiting.

Start NPCIzabele
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesG-8
FameBastok
Fame level1
TitleGustaberg Tourist

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

500g

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Izabele to begin quest
  2. Invite at least one other player to form a party of 2. Change to a job that's level 15 or below.
    • Alter egos and fellows do not count toward the requirements.
    • Level sync does work
  3. Together with your party, travel to North Gustaberg and locate the galka Hunting Bear at F-8 (near waterfalls)
    • The Survival Guide also puts you at the outpost, which is fairly close to him.
    • Note any characters under level 20 will have to walk, as mounts require level 20.
  4. Speak with Hunting Bear to complete quest

Notes

Similar to When Wills Collide, Mercenary Camp, and The Kuftal Tour this CS displays fellow party members, a very rare occurrence. 1. It's not much of a different angle than you'd see in game. 2. You can watch this CS again with the Goblin Footprint Originally this quest required 6 players and required a level 5 or below job, but was dropped to 2 players and a level 15 or below job with the June 2015 Update 3. This had an added effect of allowing players who did not have a level 5 or below job to complete the quest with 2008's Level Sync functionality, as with the previous level cap, you could not sync due to the minimum level for sync being level 10. - While a silly idea, one could have de-leveled their character to level 5. However, EXP loss on death was removed in the May 2011 update, so only new jobs could give a player access to this if they were leveled above 5.
