Bastok quest walkthrough

Mean Machine

One of the factory's machines needs oiling, but Unlucky Rat is out of machine oil. Help him out by bringing him something that can be used as a substitute.

Start NPCUnlucky Rat
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesG-7
FameBastok
Fame level2
RepeatableNo

Required items and key items

  • Slime Oil

Rewards

Warp (Scroll)

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Unlucky Rat located on the first floor in the Cermet Refinery, who is having a machine problem.
  2. Although he doesn't ask for help, bring him a Slime Oil to alleviate his problem, and to obtain your reward.
