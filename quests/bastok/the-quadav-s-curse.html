Bastok quest walkthrough

The Quadav's Curse

Corann wants to shut himself away from the rest of the world. Find him an item to hide in--something big enough to hide his whole body.

Start NPCCorann
Starting zonePort Bastok
CoordinatesK-6
FameBastok
Fame level1
RepeatableNo
NextOut of One's Shell

Required items and key items

  • Quadav Backplate

Rewards

Bronze Subligar

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Corann to start the quest (he's inside the northmost house)
  2. Obtain a Quadav Backplate either from the Auction House or by farming Amethyst Quadavs in North Gustaberg, South Gustaberg, Palborough Mines or Konschtat Highlands.
  3. Turn in backplate to Corann to complete quest.
