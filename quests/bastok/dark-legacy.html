Bastok quest walkthrough

Dark Legacy

You have been asked to find and bring back a book pertaining to the Galka's darksteel formula.

Start NPCRaibaht
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesG-8
FameBastok
RepeatableYes
PreviousBlade of Darkness
NextDark Puppet

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] "Darksteel Formula"
  • Yagudo Cherry

Rewards

Raven Scythe

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak with Cid's Assistant Raibaht to start quest.
    • After this point, the rest of the quest can be completed on any job.
  2. Speak with Mighty Fist in Metalworks (G-9) (lower level; inside Darksteel Forge) and you will receive a key item: Letter from the Darksteel Forge
  3. Travel to Windurst Waters and speak to Cochal-Monchal (F-8) (HP #1 - North Map - Optistery)
  4. Obtain a Yagudo Cherry from either a vendor or the auction house.
    • One is enough for multiple people doing the quest.
  5. Travel to Giddeus and trade the Yagudo Cherry to the ??? at H-14. This will spawn a NM: Vaa Huja the Erudite.
    • You may want to clear the area before doing so.
  6. Defeat the NM and click on the ??? again. You will obtain a key item: "Darksteel Formula"
  7. (Optional) Return to Metalworks and speak with Mighty Fist again for additional dialogue.
  8. Return to Raibaht and speak with him to complete quest.
