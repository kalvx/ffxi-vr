Bastok quest walkthrough

The Siren's Tear

Find the Siren's Tear for Wahid. This rare gem is known to occasionally appear on the shores of the Gustaberg River. Seek the bard in the Port Bastok restaurant and listen to his story.

Start NPCWahid
Starting zoneBastok Mines
CoordinatesI-8
FameBastok
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
NextLove and Ice
TitleTearjerker

Required items and key items

  • Siren's Tear

Rewards

150 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Wahid (HP #1 Bastok Mines), he will ask for a "Siren's Tear".
  2. Travel to J-9 in North Gustaberg and look for a ???. The ??? will appear randomly up and down the length of the river.
    • Port Bastok HP #1 to North Gustaberg is the quickest way to get to this river
  3. Make sure you have 1 free inventory slot in active bag.
  4. Unequip all weapons and shield in "Main" and "Sub" slot if applicable, then examine the ??? and you will receive the message "... has obtained a Siren's Tear".
  5. Go back to Wahid, trade Siren's Tear to complete quest.
  6. Can be repeated as many times as you want.
