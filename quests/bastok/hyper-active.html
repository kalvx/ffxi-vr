Bastok quest walkthrough

Hyper Active

The adventurer hired to bring Cid a hyper altimeter has been arrested and ordered to kill a monster named Orna in Delkfutt's Tower. Find the man and obtain the equipment.

Start NPCRaibaht
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesG-8
FameBastok
Fame level4
PreviousTeak Me to the Stars
NextThe Naming Game

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] Hyper altimeter

Rewards

3000 Gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. If you have just completed Teak Me to the Stars, you must zone before starting this quest.
  2. Speak with Raibaht to begin quest. You will receive a [Key Item] Molybdenum box.
  3. Travel to Lower Delkfutt's Tower and proceed to the basement.
    • A Delkfutt Key or [Key Item] Delkfutt key is required.
  4. Travel to (I-6) of the basement map and touch the Cermet Door to pop 3 NMs: Orna (the boss) and 2 Fomorian Spear.
  5. Defeat Orna (which will make the other two depop) and touch the Cermet Door again.
  6. Travel to Lower Jeuno and inspect the Street Lamp targets until you find a [Key Item] Hyper altimeter.
  7. Return to Bastok and speak with Raibaht to complete quest.
