Bastok quest walkthrough

The Naming Game

Cid's dream of completing a "semi- perpetual motion engine" can only be realized if he obtains a chunk of legendary ordrynite.

Start NPCRaibaht
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesG-8
FameBastok
Fame level5
RepeatableNo
PreviousHyper Active
TitleHyper Ultra Sonic Adventurer

Required items and key items

  • Ordrynite

Rewards

Set name of new airship which appears in CoP 5-1
First time only: 3,600 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Obtain an Ordrynite
    • Enter Pso'Xja from Beaucedine Glacier (G-9) and kill Cryptonberry Plaguer or Cryptonberry Stalker for them.
    • Outpost Warp or Survival Guide to Beaucedine Glacier -> Mount -> (G-9)
    • HP1 in Pso'Xja -> Escape -> Mount -> (G-9)
    • Do Chocobo Digging in Beaucedine Glacier
    • With Rank 10 Chocobo Digging, you can probably get 10 in a day (accepting that they are Rare, so you have to turn each in).
  2. Trade it to Raibaht to complete quest
