Bastok quest walkthrough

Dark Puppet

Zeid's letter to Cid reports him finding one of the weapons forged by Gerwitz, the legendary darksteel smith...but it has taken the form of an evil weapon. You must meet Zeid and help him with his task.

Complete walkthrough

  1. <!--
    • ->
  2. Speak with Cid as a Dark Knight to begin quest.
    • You can be on any job from here on.
  3. Obtain a Darksteel Ingot from the Auction House or Crafting.
    • One is enough for multiple people doing the quest.
  4. Zone into Ordelle's Caves for a cutscene.
    • Survival guide and voidwatch warp bring you to the same entrance.
  5. Trade the Darksteel Ingot to the ??? at (I-8) on map 1 to spawn Gerwitz's Axe (NM).
    • If you zoned in from La Theine Plateau or used Survival Guide, you should take the east path (B on the wiki maps).
  6. Defeat Gerwitz's Axe and it will drop Gerwitz's Axe.
    • If you are defeated you will need another Darksteel Ingot to respawn the NM.
  7. Head South up the ramp (D on the wiki maps). At (H-11/12) drop down the hole (the southeast hole). Go east to reach map 3 (E on the wiki maps).
  8. Trade Gerwitz's Axe to the ??? at (H-8) on map 3 to spawn Gerwitz's Sword (NM).
  9. Defeat Gerwitz's Sword and it will drop Gerwitz's Sword.
  10. Head South to enter a large square room.
  11. Trade Gerwitz's Sword to the ??? at (H-10) and it will spawn Gerwitz's Soul.
  12. Defeat Gerwitz's Soul then head for La Theine Plateau at (G-10) on the same map. You will receive your reward after the cutscene.
    • All party members in zone receive credit for Gerwitz's Soul's defeat for quest progression even if they are too far to gain exp.
