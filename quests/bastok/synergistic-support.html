Bastok quest walkthrough

Synergistic Support

Ever interested in seeing his students expand their horizons, Bastok's chief synergy researcher has given you a valuable lesson in synthesizing your own fewell. He's even offered you a selection from his own stock to boot!

Start NPCHildolf
Starting zoneMetalworks
CoordinatesF-8
FameBastok
Fame level1

Required items and key items

  • Slime Oil

Rewards

Three of any Fewell element

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Hildolf at (F-8) to learn how to create your own Fewell.
  2. Trade him a Slime Oil.
  3. Choose a type from the list and you will be given three of that choice and the quest will be completed.
