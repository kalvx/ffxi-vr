Bastok quest walkthrough

The Wisdom of Elders

The chimney in Benita's house is clogged with soot. Her mother-in-law, who's gone outside with Benita's daughter, knows the perfect item for clearing chimneys. Go consult the elderly woman, and find the item.

Start NPCBenita
Starting zonePort Bastok
CoordinatesJ-6
FameBastok
Fame level1

Required items and key items

  • Bomb Ash

Rewards

Traveler's Hat

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak with Benita to begin quest.
  2. Speak to Tete at (I-5) of Port Bastok
  3. Obtain a Bomb Ash from the Auction House, Tenshodo vendors or Alchemy vendors, or by farming Lanterns/Bomb type mobs.
  4. Trade Bomb Ash to Benita to complete quest.
