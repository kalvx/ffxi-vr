Expansion access

The Road to Aht Urhgan

NPCFaursel, Tenshodo HQ, Lower Jeuno (J-8)
RewardBoarding permit
BEFORE YOU START: You need Tenshodo access to reach Faursel. If necessary, complete Tenshodo Membership first.

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Tenshodo HQ and speak with Faursel. Choose the hidden/third option and tell him you want to go to Aht Urhgan.
  2. Choose an access route. Faursel offers item-based requirements of varying difficulty. Collect the requested item set and trade it to him. A high-cost gil shortcut may also be available depending on implementation.
  3. Wait until the next Vana'diel day and zone. Return to Faursel to receive the Boarding permit.
  4. Travel to Mhaura. Use the Near East ship route to reach Al Zahbi/Aht Urhgan and begin the Treasures of Aht Urhgan storyline.
The exact item route you choose changes what must be traded, so read Faursel's options before buying or farming anything.
