Lower Jeuno quest

Deal with Tenshodo

PREREQUISITE: Start A Clock Most Delicate in Upper Jeuno first.
NPCGarnev, Lower Jeuno (J-7), Tenshodo
Required itemGold Orcmask
RewardClock tower oil (key item)

Walkthrough

  1. After starting A Clock Most Delicate, speak with Garnev inside the Tenshodo.
  2. Obtain a Gold Orcmask. It drops from Orcish Troopers in Davoi/Monastic Cavern and Orcish Overlord in Monastic Cavern.
  3. Trade the Gold Orcmask to Garnev. Receive the Clock tower oil.
  4. Return to the clock-tower quest line. The oil is required to finish A Clock Most Delicate and connects into Save the Clock Tower.
