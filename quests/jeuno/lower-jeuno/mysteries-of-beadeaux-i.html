Magicite prerequisite

Mysteries of Beadeaux I

REQUIRED FOR MAGICITE: Flag Magicite and speak with Aldo before doing this prerequisite.
NPCSattal-Mansal, Lower Jeuno (J-8)
Required itemQuadav Charm
RewardCoruscant rosary (key item)

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Sattal-Mansal inside the Tenshodo area after Aldo advances Magicite.
  2. Travel to Beadeaux. Reach the upper section and defeat De'Vyu Headhunter at approximately (I-9).
  3. Obtain a Quadav Charm.
  4. Return to Sattal-Mansal and trade the Quadav Charm. Receive the Coruscant rosary.
While in Beadeaux, also obtain the Quadav Augury Shell for Mysteries of Beadeaux II, but trade the two items separately.
