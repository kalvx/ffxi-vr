Lower Jeuno quest

All in the Cards

NPCChululu, Lower Jeuno (I-8)
FameJeuno Fame 5
RepeatableYes
Reward600 gil

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Chululu. She gives you five copies of one random Tarut card type.
  2. Build a complete set. Obtain one each of Tarut: Death, The Hermit, The King, and The Fool. Trade with other players or use bazaars; additional cards can be obtained from Chululu after the applicable wait.
  3. Trade all four different cards to Chululu. Receive 600 gil.
This repeatable quest helps circulate cards needed for Collect Tarut Cards.
