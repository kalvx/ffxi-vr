Lower Jeuno quest

A Minstrel in Despair

NPCMertaire, Lower Jeuno (I-8)
Level30+
Required itemPoetic Parchment
Reward2,100 gil
This quest does not appear in the active quest log. It is part of the Bard-unlock progression.

Walkthrough

  1. Obtain the Poetic Parchment. Complete the preceding Song Runes/Old Monument portion of the bard story so you possess the Poetic Parchment.
  2. Return to Mertaire. Go to the Merry Minstrel Meadhouse in Lower Jeuno (I-8).
  3. Trade the Poetic Parchment to Mertaire. Watch the cutscene and receive 2,100 gil. Continue with Path of the Bard.

Walkthrough cross-checked against BG Wiki's A Minstrel in Despair quest reference.
