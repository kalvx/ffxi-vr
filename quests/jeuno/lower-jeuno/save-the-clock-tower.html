Lower Jeuno quest

Save the Clock Tower

NPCDerrick, Lower Jeuno (H-7)
FameJeuno Fame 5
Key itemClock Tower Petition

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Derrick and agree to gather signatures. Receive the Clock Tower Petition.
  2. Get all 10 signatures by trading the petition to: Zauko (Lower Jeuno I-6), Teigero-Bangero (Lower Jeuno H-10), Pitantimand (Port Jeuno H-7), Radeivepart (Ru'Lude Gardens H-9), Souren (Upper Jeuno G-9), Constance (Upper Jeuno G-9), Monberaux (Upper Jeuno G-10), Baudin (Upper Jeuno G-8), Zuber (Upper Jeuno F-7), and Collet (Upper Jeuno G-7).
  3. Return the signed petition to Derrick. The quest completes and you receive the Clock Tower Preservationist title.
DON'T STOP EARLY: Derrick expects all ten signatures.
