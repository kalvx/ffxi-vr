Tenshodo Membership

Join the Tenshodo and obtain the membership card used to enter Tenshodo headquarters.

Start NPCGhebi Damomohe
ZoneLower Jeuno — Neptune's Spire
CoordinatesI-7
Required fameJeuno Fame 3 for the standard application route
RepeatableNo
Reward[Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card

Before you begin

IMPORTANT — JEUNO FAME REQUIRED: The standard application route requires Jeuno Fame 3. If Ghebi Damomohe will not offer the membership dialogue, use the Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame Guide to check your fame and raise it before continuing.

Magicite connection: You need the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card to enter Tenshodo HQ and speak with Aldo during the Rank 4 mission Magicite.

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Ghebi Damomohe. Go to Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and speak with Ghebi Damomohe (I-7).
  2. Select the membership option. When the menu appears, move to the third blank option and select it to begin the membership dialogue.
  3. Travel to Port Bastok. For the application-form route, enter Warehouse #2, across from the Airship Agency.
  4. Get the application form. Speak with Jabbar in Warehouse #2 to obtain the Tenshodo Application Form.
  5. Return to Ghebi. Go back to Ghebi Damomohe in Lower Jeuno and complete the membership process.
  6. Receive the membership card. You receive the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card, allowing access to the Tenshodo headquarters.

Tenshodo Invite route

If you already possess a Tenshodo Invite, bring it to Ghebi Damomohe and use it for the membership process.
