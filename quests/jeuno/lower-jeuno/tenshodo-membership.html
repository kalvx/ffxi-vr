Tenshodo Membership

Join the Tenshodo and obtain the membership card used to enter Tenshodo headquarters.

Start NPCGhebi Damomohe
ZoneLower Jeuno — Neptune's Spire
CoordinatesI-7
Required fameJeuno Fame 3 for the standard application route
RepeatableNo
Reward[Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card

Before you begin

The standard quest route requires Jeuno Fame 3. If Ghebi does not offer the membership dialogue, check your Jeuno fame with Mendi in Lower Jeuno at (H-8) and complete additional Jeuno quests.

Mission connection: Tenshodo membership is needed to enter the Tenshodo headquarters and speak with Aldo during the Rank 4 mission Magicite.

Walkthrough

  1. Go to Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and speak with Ghebi Damomohe (I-7).
  2. When the menu appears, move to the third blank option and select it to begin the membership dialogue.
  3. For the application-form route, travel to Port Bastok and enter Warehouse #2, across from the Airship Agency.
  4. Speak with Jabbar in Warehouse #2 to obtain the Tenshodo Application Form.
  5. Return to Ghebi Damomohe in Lower Jeuno and complete the membership process.
  6. You receive the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card, allowing access to the Tenshodo headquarters.

Tenshodo Invite route

If you already possess a Tenshodo Invite, bring it to Ghebi Damomohe and use it for the membership process.
