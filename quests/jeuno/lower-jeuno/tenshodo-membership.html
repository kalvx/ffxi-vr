Jeuno → Lower Jeuno

Tenshodo Membership

Join the Tenshodo and obtain the key item needed to enter Tenshodo headquarters.

Start NPCGhebi Damomohe
ZoneLower Jeuno — Neptune's Spire
CoordinatesI-7
Required fameJeuno Fame 3 for the normal quest route
RepeatableNo
Reward[Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card

Before you begin

For the normal route, raise Jeuno fame to level 3. If Ghebi does not offer the membership dialogue, check Jeuno fame with Mendi in Lower Jeuno (H-8) and complete Jeuno fame quests.

Magicite: obtain this membership before the Aldo portion of Magicite.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Go to Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and speak with Ghebi Damomohe (I-7).
  2. When the menu appears, select the third blank option. Ghebi explains the membership requirement.
  3. For the application route, travel to Port Bastok and enter Warehouse #2 across from the Airship Agency.
  4. Speak with Jabbar to obtain the Tenshodo Application Form.
  5. Return to Ghebi in Lower Jeuno and complete the membership process.
  6. You receive the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card and can enter Tenshodo headquarters.

Invite route

If you already possess a Tenshodo Invite, bring it to Ghebi. Current Reality behavior should be verified before treating the invite as a guaranteed fame bypass.
