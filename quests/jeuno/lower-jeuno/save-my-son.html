Lower Jeuno quest

Save My Son

StartDoor: Merchant's House, Lower Jeuno (G-11)
Level30+
RewardBeast Whistle + 2,100 gil

Walkthrough

  1. Examine the Merchant's House door on the second floor at Lower Jeuno (G-11), above the chocobo stables. Agree to help Dietmund.
  2. Optional: speak with Shalott at Upper Jeuno (G-7) for additional story.
  3. Go to Qufim Island (F-8) at night. Examine the Night Flowers between 21:30 and 04:00 Vana'diel time for the cutscene.
  4. Return to the Merchant's House door in Lower Jeuno for Beast Whistle and 2,100 gil.
