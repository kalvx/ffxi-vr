Reputation guide

Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame

Jeuno fame and Norg/Tenshodo fame are separate reputation groups. Raising one does not directly substitute for the other.

Jeuno fame

Jeuno fame is used by Jeuno quests, including the normal route for Tenshodo Membership. The membership quest normally requires Jeuno Fame 3.

Repeatable: Community Service

NPC: Zauko, Lower Jeuno (I-6). Speak to him at night, roughly 18:00–01:00 Vana'diel time. Light all 12 Street Lamps along Lower Jeuno, then return to Zauko before the deadline. The quest is repeatable and raises Jeuno fame.

Fast indirect route: Only the Best

NPC: Melyon in Selbina. This repeatable quest accepts one of the following sets per turn-in: 5 La Theine Cabbages, 3 Millioncorn, or 1 Boyahda Moss. Fame gained in the starting nations contributes to Jeuno reputation, making this a commonly used route for reaching Jeuno Fame 3.

Norg / Tenshodo fame

Norg fame is also called Tenshodo fame. It covers Norg and quests associated with the Tenshodo and its affiliates. It becomes important for several Norg quest lines, including Ninja scroll and other Norg content.

Mihgo's Amigo — Yagudo Necklace

NPC: Nanaa Mihgo, Windurst Woods. Farm Yagudo Necklaces, commonly from Yagudo in Giddeus, and trade them in sets of 4. The quest is repeatable and raises Norg/Tenshodo fame.

Shady Business — Zinc Ore

NPC: Talib, Port Bastok (F-6). After completing its prerequisite, trade 4 Zinc Ore at a time. Shady Business is repeatable and raises Norg/Tenshodo fame. This is one of the simplest ways to deliberately grind Norg fame.

A Job for the Consortium

The repeatable Tenshodo-affiliated quest in Port San d'Oria is another route for raising the same Norg/Tenshodo reputation.
