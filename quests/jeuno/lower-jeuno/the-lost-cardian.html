Cardian quest line

The Lost Cardian

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Teigero-Bangero at Lower Jeuno (H-10) and Panta-Putta at Lower Jeuno (G-10) while investigating the missing Two of Swords.
  2. Go to Monberaux's Infirmary in Upper Jeuno (G-10) and speak with Monberaux.
  3. Continue talking if necessary until the correct quest cutscene plays; Monberaux participates in many stories and may have other dialogue queued.
  4. Receive the Two of Swords key item and 2,100 gil. This opens The Kind Cardian.
