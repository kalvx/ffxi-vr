Magicite prerequisite

Mysteries of Beadeaux II

REQUIRED FOR MAGICITE: This is separate from Part I. Trade each quest item separately.
NPCSattal-Mansal, Lower Jeuno (J-8)
Required itemQuadav Augury Shell
RewardBlack matinee necklace (key item)

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Sattal-Mansal to make sure this half of the prerequisite is active.
  2. Travel to Beadeaux and reach the upper section.
  3. Defeat Go'Bhu Gascon at approximately (F-6) and obtain the Quadav Augury Shell.
  4. Trade the shell to Sattal-Mansal. Receive the Black matinee necklace.
For the companion item and route, see Mysteries of Beadeaux I. Both feed directly into the Magicite walkthrough.
