Bard Artifact Armor · AF2

The Requiem

REQUIREMENT: Bard level 50+ and the preceding Bard AF quest, Painful Memory.
NPCBki Tbujhja, Lower Jeuno (H-8)
BringHoly Water
RewardChoral Slippers

Walkthrough

  1. Flag the quest with Bki Tbujhja in the Merry Minstrel and trade her a Holy Water. She returns it and sends you to The Eldieme Necropolis.
  2. Reach the correct Eldieme entrance. Travel via Beaucedine Glacier to the isolated Batallia Downs area and enter The Eldieme Necropolis at (F-5).
  3. Go to the five-sarcophagus room at (D-4). Trade Holy Water to the correct sarcophagus; the correct one is random.
  4. Defeat the three skeleton NMs: Yum Kimil, Dog Guardian, and Owl Guardian.
  5. Examine the same sarcophagus again after the fight to receive the Star ring (tarnished).
  6. Return to Bki Tbujhja in Lower Jeuno and receive Choral Slippers.
