Lower Jeuno quest

Collect Tarut Cards

NPCChululu, Lower Jeuno (I-8)
FameJeuno Fame 2
RewardCard Collector title; Chululu fortune-telling access

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Chululu. She gives you five identical Tarut cards.
  2. Collect all four types: Death, The Hermit, The King, and The Fool. Trade duplicates with other adventurers or obtain cards from player bazaars.
  3. Trade one of each card to Chululu. This completes the collection and unlocks her fortune-telling service.
Need more cards later? See All in the Cards, the repeatable follow-up.
