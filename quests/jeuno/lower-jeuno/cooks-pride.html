Lower Jeuno quest

Cook's Pride

NPCNaruru, Lower Jeuno (H-10)
FameJeuno Fame 4
NeededRoyal Jelly
RewardMythril Ring + 3,000 gil

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Naruru at Lower Jeuno (H-10). She needs a replacement Super Soup Pot.
  2. Go to the Culinarians' Guild in Windurst Waters. Speak with Maysoon at Windurst Waters North (E-9).
  3. Give Maysoon a Royal Jelly to obtain the Super Soup Pot.
  4. Return to Naruru. Receive the Mythril Ring and 3,000 gil.
