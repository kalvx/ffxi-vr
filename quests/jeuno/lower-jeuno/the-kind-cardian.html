Cardian quest line

The Kind Cardian

PREREQUISITE: Complete The Lost Cardian and possess the Two of Swords key item.

Walkthrough

  1. Take Two of Swords to Apururu at the Manustery in Windurst Woods (H-9).
  2. Enter the southwest tower of Outer Horutoto Ruins from West Sarutabaruta (F-11).
  3. Find and defeat Ten of Cups around (J-8) in the hidden section and obtain the Ten of Cups card.
  4. Trade Ten of Cups to Apururu in Windurst Woods.
  5. Return to Panta-Putta at Lower Jeuno (G-10) to finish and receive the Green Cape.
