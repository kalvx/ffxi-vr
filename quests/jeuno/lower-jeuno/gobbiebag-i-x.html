Inventory expansion

The Gobbiebag Parts I-X

Ten upgrades from 30 inventory slots to 80.

NPC: Bluffnix in Muckvix's Junk Shop, Lower Jeuno (H-9). Each completed part adds 5 slots. Higher parts require more Jeuno fame.
Part I → 35Dhalmel Leather · Steel Ingot · Linen Cloth · Peridot
Part II → 40Ram Leather · Mythril Ingot · Wool Cloth · Turquoise
Part III → 45Tiger Leather · Gold Ingot · Velvet Cloth · Painite
Part IV → 50Cermet Chunk · Darksteel Ingot · Silk Cloth · Goshenite
Part V → 55Bugard Leather · Paktong Ingot · Moblinweave · Rhodonite
Part VI → 60Balloon Cloth · Shakudo Ingot · H.Q. Eft Skin · Iolite
Part VII → 65Lynx Leather · Adaman Ingot · Rainbow Cloth · Deathstone
Part VIII → 70Smilodon Leather · Electrum Ingot · Cilice · Angelstone
Part IX → 75Orichalcum Ingot · Peiste Leather · Oil-Soaked Cloth · Oxblood Orb
Part X → 80Molybdenum Ingot · Griffon Leather · Foulard · Angel Skin Orb

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Bluffnix and make sure he offers the next Gobbiebag part. If he does not, raise Jeuno fame and confirm all earlier parts are complete.
  2. Obtain all four items for your current part. Most can be crafted or purchased through the Auction House.
  3. Trade all four required items to Bluffnix at the same time. Your inventory expands by five slots immediately.
  4. Repeat for the next part until Part X raises the Gobbiebag to 80 slots.
IMPORTANT: Do not mix materials from different parts. Bluffnix expects the exact four-item set for the part currently active.
