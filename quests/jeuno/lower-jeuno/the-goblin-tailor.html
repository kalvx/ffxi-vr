Race Specific Equipment

The Goblin Tailor

NPCGuttrix, Lower Jeuno (H-10)
FameJeuno Fame 3
Level10+
RepeatableYes

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Guttrix. He explains the weekly Race Specific Equipment pattern and which dungeon is active for your race/sex window.
  2. Obtain the appropriate chest key for Gusgen Mines, Ordelle's Caves, or Maze of Shakhrami.
  3. Open a Treasure Chest during your active RSE window to obtain the Magical Pattern.
  4. Return the Magical Pattern to Guttrix and choose/receive the applicable RSE reward.
The active dungeon and time window rotate. Check Guttrix before farming so you do not hunt chests during the wrong RSE period.
