Jeuno → Lower Jeuno

Community Service

Light Lower Jeuno's street lamps at night to earn a reward and raise Jeuno fame.

Start NPCZauko
ZoneLower Jeuno
CoordinatesI-6
TimeApproximately 18:00–01:00 Vana'diel time
RepeatableYes
RaisesJeuno fame

Walkthrough

  1. Go to Zauko (I-6) in Lower Jeuno during the evening/night period.
  2. Speak to Zauko and accept the job to light the street lamps.
  3. Run along Lower Jeuno and examine each of the 12 Street Lamp targets on the seaward side of the street.
  4. Make sure all 12 lamps are lit before the time limit expires.
  5. Return to Zauko to complete the quest and receive credit toward your Jeuno reputation.

This quest can be repeated and is a direct Jeuno-fame route. If you are trying to unlock Tenshodo Membership, see Jeuno & Norg Fame for additional options.
