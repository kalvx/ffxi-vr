Advanced job unlock

Path of the Bard

LEVEL REQUIREMENT: Level 30+. Complete the preceding bard/minstrel steps first.
NPCBki Tbujhja, Lower Jeuno (H-8)
RewardUnlock Bard + 3,000 gil

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Bki Tbujhja inside the Merry Minstrel in Lower Jeuno (H-8).
  2. Travel to Valkurm Dunes. Go through the tunnel around (C-6) to reach the secret beach.
  3. Examine the Song Runes at (B-7). Watch the final cutscene.
  4. Bard is unlocked. You also receive 3,000 gil. Change jobs at your Mog House to use BRD.
This quest does not appear in the active quest log until completion.
