Reputation guide

Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame

Jeuno fame and Norg/Tenshodo fame are separate reputation groups. Raising one does not directly substitute for the other.

Jeuno fame

Use Jeuno fame for the normal Tenshodo Membership route. The standard membership quest requires Jeuno Fame 3. If you are preparing for Magicite, this is the fame you need to obtain the Tenshodo Member's Card through the standard route.

Repeatable: Community Service

NPC: Zauko, Lower Jeuno (I-6). Speak to him at night, roughly 18:00–01:00 Vana'diel time. Light all 12 Street Lamps along Lower Jeuno, then return to Zauko before the deadline. The quest is repeatable and raises Jeuno fame. See the full Community Service walkthrough.

Fast indirect route: Only the Best

NPC: Melyon in Selbina. This repeatable quest accepts one of the following sets per turn-in: 5 La Theine Cabbages, 3 Millioncorn, or 1 Boyahda Moss. Fame gained through this route contributes toward Jeuno reputation and is commonly used to reach Jeuno Fame 3.

Norg / Tenshodo fame

This is a separate reputation track. Norg/Tenshodo fame matters for Norg and Tenshodo-affiliated quest lines, but it is not a substitute for the Jeuno Fame 3 requirement used by the normal Tenshodo Membership route.

Mihgo's Amigo — Yagudo Necklaces

NPC: Nanaa Mihgo, Windurst Woods. Farm Yagudo Necklaces, commonly from Yagudo in Giddeus, and trade them in sets of 4. The quest is repeatable and raises Norg/Tenshodo fame.

Shady Business — Zinc Ore

NPC: Talib, Port Bastok (F-6). After completing its prerequisite, trade 4 Zinc Ore at a time. Shady Business is repeatable and raises Norg/Tenshodo fame.

A Job for the Consortium

The repeatable Tenshodo-affiliated quest in Port San d'Oria is another route for raising the same Norg/Tenshodo reputation.
