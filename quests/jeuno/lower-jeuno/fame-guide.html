Jeuno & Norg / Tenshodo Fame

Jeuno fame and Norg/Tenshodo fame are separate reputation groups. Raising one does not directly substitute for the other.

Jeuno fame

Use Jeuno fame for the normal Tenshodo Membership route. The standard membership quest requires Jeuno Fame 3 (125 points). If you are preparing for Magicite, this is the fame you need to obtain the Tenshodo Member's Card through the standard route.

Repeatable: Community Service

NPC: Zauko, Lower Jeuno (I-6). Speak to him at night, roughly 18:00–01:00 Vana'diel time. Light all 12 Street Lamps along Lower Jeuno, then return to Zauko before the deadline. The quest is repeatable and raises Jeuno fame. See the full Community Service walkthrough.

Fast indirect route: Only the Best

NPC: Melyon in Selbina. Each successful turn-in raises Jeuno, Bastok, and San d'Oria fame. Current Reality's quest values are shown below.

Fastest simple Fame 3 route: if you are starting at 0 Jeuno fame, trade 5 Boyahda Moss to Melyon one at a time. That produces 150 Jeuno fame, clearing the 125-point Fame 3 threshold. If you already have Jeuno fame from other quests, you may need fewer; check with Mendi before buying or farming the full amount.

Norg / Tenshodo fame

This is a separate reputation track. Norg/Tenshodo fame matters for Norg and Tenshodo-affiliated quest lines, but it is not a substitute for the Jeuno Fame 3 requirement used by the normal Tenshodo Membership route.

Mihgo's Amigo — Yagudo Necklaces

NPC: Nanaa Mihgo, Windurst Woods. Farm Yagudo Necklaces, commonly from Yagudo in Giddeus, and trade them in sets of 4. The quest is repeatable and raises Norg/Tenshodo fame.

Shady Business — Zinc Ore

NPC: Talib, Port Bastok (F-6). After completing its prerequisite, trade 4 Zinc Ore at a time. Shady Business is repeatable and raises Norg/Tenshodo fame.

A Job for the Consortium

The repeatable Tenshodo-affiliated quest in Port San d'Oria is another route for raising the same Norg/Tenshodo reputation.

Repeat-item trade macro

Useful when a repeatable quest accepts one item per trade. Target the NPC and use:
/item "Boyahda Moss" <t>

You can also use <stnpc> to select an NPC when the macro runs:

/item "Boyahda Moss" <stnpc>
Important: FFXI's /item macro does not let you specify a quantity such as 3 Millioncorn or 4 Zinc Ore. For quests requiring several items in one trade, open the Trade window and place the required quantity into it normally. The macro is ideal for single-item repeat turn-ins such as Boyahda Moss.
