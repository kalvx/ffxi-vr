Lower Jeuno quest

Hook, Line, and Sinker

PREREQUISITE: Progress Chains of Promathia through Promathia Mission 3-2.
NPCOmer, Lower Jeuno (G-10)
Required itemEgret Fishing Rod
Reward3,000 gil

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Omer at Lower Jeuno (G-10).
  2. Travel to Qufim Island and fish up Krakens. The required Egret Fishing Rod drops from Krakens pulled up while fishing. Fishing skill is not required simply to pull up the monster.
  3. Return the Egret Fishing Rod to Omer for 3,000 gil.
