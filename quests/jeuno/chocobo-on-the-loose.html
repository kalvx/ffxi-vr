Jeuno quest • chocobo raising

Chocobo on the Loose!

Recover the missing chocobo and receive your own chocobo egg. This route records every return trip and meeting choice used by Current Reality.

At a glance

StartsBrutus, Upper Jeuno (G-7)
Related requirementMain job level 20 or higher; Wings of the Goddess/ToAU quest feature enabled on the server
TravelUpper Jeuno → La Theine Plateau → Upper Jeuno → Southern San d’Oria → Upper Jeuno
WaitOne Vana’diel day after the final pre-reward Brutus scene, plus a zone change
RewardChocobo egg (faintly warm)

Do not skip the return to Brutus after examining the tracks. The quest does not send you straight from La Theine Plateau to Hantileon.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Brutus in Upper Jeuno (G-7). The chocobo incident is introduced while following the Brutus chocobo quests. At the choice between “Free the chocobo!” and “No, think of Brutus!”, choose “Free the chocobo!”.
  2. Travel to La Theine Plateau and examine the Chocobo Tracks around E-4. Examine the target once and watch the complete cutscene. The message about being the last person to see the chocobo is progress, not the end of the quest.
  3. Return to Brutus in Upper Jeuno (G-7). Talk to him and watch the next scene. This required conversation advances the quest to the San d’Oria meeting.
  4. Travel to Southern San d’Oria and speak to Hantileon at the chocobo stables (I-11).
  5. Work through every proposal at the meeting. Select each of these three suggestions as it appears: “Return the chocobo to the wild,” “Give the chocobo a rest,” and “Reform the chocobo raising system.” They are intentionally rejected. After all three have been exhausted, choose the newly available correct answer: “Bring the chocobos together.”
  6. Speak to Hantileon again. Watch the follow-up meeting scene/dialogue completely. If he still offers quest dialogue, continue talking until it repeats normally.
  7. Return to Brutus in Upper Jeuno (G-7). Watch his complete scene. This starts the final wait.
  8. Wait until the next Vana’diel day and change zones. Both conditions matter. Crossing midnight while remaining in the same zone is not enough; leave Upper Jeuno and return after the day has changed.
  9. Speak to Brutus again. The quest completes and you receive a chocobo egg (faintly warm).

After receiving the warm egg

  1. Choose one VCS chocobo trainer. This permanently selects the stable where this chocobo will be raised: Hantileon in Southern San d’Oria (I-11), Zopago in Bastok Mines (J-9), or Pulonono in Windurst Woods (K-12). Brutus in Upper Jeuno does not accept the egg for raising.
  2. Trade the Chocobo Egg (faintly warm) to that trainer. Trade the egg itself—do not merely speak to the NPC. Confirm that you want the VCS trainer to care for it. The egg is consumed only when the raising record is created successfully.
  3. Return to the same trainer to check the egg. In this Current Reality build, a raising day defaults to one real-world day and the egg reaches the chick stage on raising day 4. Changing zones or waiting for a Vana’diel day will not shorten that real-time wait.
  4. During the egg stage, use the trainer’s care menu and “Watch over the chocobo.” Food cannot be given until the egg hatches.
  5. After it hatches, return to the same trainer and name the chick. Choose its two-part name, then use the trainer menu to set its care schedule, check its condition, care for it, and feed it. Continue returning to this same city’s VCS trainer throughout raising.

If trading the egg does nothing: confirm ENABLE_CHOCOBO_RAISING = true in the server’s main settings and restart the Map Server after changing it. The unmodified a89969976e raising script also contains an egg-start logic defect: it can return when no existing chocobo record is found, before creating the first record. If the setting is already enabled and no handoff cutscene starts, the script needs that defect corrected; do not discard or sell the egg.
