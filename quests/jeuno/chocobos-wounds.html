Chocobo’s Wounds

Heal the injured chocobo at the Upper Jeuno stables to earn the license used for chocobo rentals.

At a glance

StartsBrutus, Upper Jeuno (G-7)
RequirementYour currently equipped main job must be level 20 or higher
Required item4 × Gausebit Wildgrass
Current Reality wait45 real-world seconds between accepted feeding attempts
RewardsChocobo License key item, Chocobo Trainer title, and 30 Jeuno fame

Use Gausebit Wildgrass—not Gysahl Greens. Trade one clump at a time to the injured Chocobo NPC, and wait the full 45 seconds between attempts.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Equip a main job at level 20 or higher. A level-20 subjob does not satisfy the requirement.
  2. Speak to Brutus at the chocobo stables in Upper Jeuno (G-7). Watch the scene and choose the affirmative option offering to help the injured chocobo. The server checks the first response option. If you decline, speak to Brutus again and accept.
  3. Speak to Osker beside the injured Chocobo. He explains that the animal may accept Gausebit Wildgrass.
  4. Obtain 4 clumps of Gausebit Wildgrass. You will make six successful attempts, but the chocobo rejects the first two without consuming the grass. The final four attempts each consume one clump.
  5. Trade one Gausebit Wildgrass to the injured Chocobo NPC—not to Brutus or Osker. After each accepted attempt, wait at least 45 real-world seconds before trading again. Stay near the stables; no Vana’diel-day wait or zone change is required.

Completion and troubleshooting

On the sixth accepted trade, you receive the Chocobo License key item, the Chocobo Trainer title, and Jeuno fame. You can then rent chocobos from rental stables, subject to the normal area and rental restrictions.
