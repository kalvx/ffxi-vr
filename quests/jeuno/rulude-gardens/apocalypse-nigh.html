Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Zilart + Promathia finale

Apocalypse Nigh

StartEsha’ntarl · Ru’Lude Gardens
Bring / prerequisiteZilart Mission 17; Promathia 8-4; Shadows of the Departed
RewardChoice of Ethereal, Hollow, Magnetic, or Static Earring
  1. After Shadows of the Departed, zone and wait until the next Vana'diel day, then enter the palace in Ru'Lude Gardens.
  2. Enter Sealion's Den for a scene. If an older return-to-Al'Taieu scene plays first, zone out to Tavnazian Safehold and re-enter.
  3. Enter the Grand Palace of Hu'Xzoi, use the Gate of the Gods to reach the Garden of Ru'Hmet, then travel to the Empyreal Paradox.
  4. Examine the Transcendental Radiance for the scene, then examine it again to enter the battle against Eald'narche and Kam'lanaut.
  5. Focus Eald'narche first while controlling or kiting Kam'lanaut. Prepare for AoE Silence, Sleep, Terror, Petrification, hate reset, and elemental blades that absorb matching magic.
  6. After victory, speak with Aldo in Lower Jeuno (J-8). You must be at least Rank 5 in your current nation.
  7. Zone and wait until the next Vana'diel day, then speak with Gilgamesh in Norg. Choose one earring carefully to complete the quest.
