Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 66+ · cap 70 → 75

Shattering Stars

StartMaat · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisiteJob Testimony
RewardRaises the level cap to 75
  1. Speak with Maat on the job you will use. Obtain that job's Testimony from the appropriate high-level beastman or enemy.
  2. Trade the Testimony to Maat. For the fifteen original/Rise of the Zilart jobs, the assigned arenas are: Balga's Dais (MNK/WHM/SMN), Horlais Peak (WAR/BLM/RNG), Qu'Bia Arena (PLD/DRK/BRD), Waughroon Shrine (RDM/THF/BST), and Chamber of Oracles (SAM/NIN/DRG).
  3. Prepare job food, medicines, capped primary skills, and macros before entering. The fight lasts 10 minutes; your support job is removed, TP is reset, and most buffs are lost.
  4. Trade the Testimony to the Burning Circle and defeat Maat or satisfy the job-specific win condition. WHM can survive for roughly five minutes; THF can win by successfully stealing the Instant Warp scroll if not already carrying one.
  5. Return to Maat after the victory to complete the quest and unlock level 75.
Current Reality: Trust availability depends on Rhapsody in Umber and server implementation. Prepare to win solo rather than assuming Trusts will enter.
