Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Post-Maat achievement

Beyond the Sun

StartMaat · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisiteDefeat Maat on all 15 original jobs
RewardMaat’s Cap

This is not a level-cap quest. It is the Maat's Cap achievement.

  1. Defeat Maat through Shattering Stars on WAR, MNK, WHM, BLM, RDM, THF, PLD, DRK, BST, BRD, RNG, SAM, NIN, DRG, and SMN.
  2. Each job requires its matching Testimony and assigned battlefield. Repeat victories after the first are no longer level capped, but the job must meet the entry requirement.
  3. Keep your own checklist; the quest log does not provide a convenient complete tracker.
  4. After the fifteenth qualifying victory, speak with Maat for the completion scene and Maat's Cap.

Reference checked against BG Wiki quest documentation and rewritten for the Current Reality Compendium. Server behavior is authoritative where it differs.
