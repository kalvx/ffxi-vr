Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 91+ · cap 95 → 99

Beyond Infinity

StartNomad Moogle · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisiteSeasoning Stone; Soul Gem and clasp progression
RewardRaises the level cap to 99
  1. Speak with the Nomad Moogle and explicitly accept the challenge. Speak with Maat until he directs you to Degenhard.
  2. Visit Degenhard, Bastok Markets (I-10). Speak once, then trade a Seasoning Stone, Fossilized Fang, and Fossilized Bone for an Olde Rarab Tail. Obtaining multiple tails is strongly recommended.
  3. Return to the Nomad Moogle, provide the required stone/payment, and choose a battlefield destination. Party members who already cleared the quest may assist but must travel to the battlefield themselves.
  4. Fight Atori-Tutori. Use an Olde Rarab Tail to remove his extreme damage reduction and Terror him temporarily. Coordinate additional tails after the prior effect expires.
  5. If defeated, obtain another entry using the requested Merit Point or High Kindred's Crest payment. After victory, return to the Nomad Moogle to unlock level 99.
Do not warp immediately after accepting. If you skip Maat's Degenhard dialogue, Degenhard may refuse the enfeebling-item trade.
