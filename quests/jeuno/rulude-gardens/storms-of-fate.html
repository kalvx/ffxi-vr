Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Chains of Promathia endgame

Storms of Fate

StartEsha’ntarl · Palace approach
Bring / prerequisitePromathia Mission 8-4 and post-Dawn scenes
RewardWhisper of the Wyrmking
  1. Complete Promathia Mission 8-4 and all post-Dawn epilogue scenes, including Blueblade Fell.
  2. Walk toward the palace in Ru'Lude Gardens to trigger Esha'ntarl's opening scene.
  3. Examine the Dilapidated Gate in Misareaux Coast at (F-7).
  4. Enter Riverne – Site #B01 and reach the green Unstable Displacement at (E-7). The Home Point route is fastest; the long route requires Giant Scales.
  5. Examine it once for the scene and again to enter Storms of Fate. Defeat Bahamut.
  6. Return to the palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for the completion scene and Whisper of the Wyrmking.
