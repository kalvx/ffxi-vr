Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Chains of Promathia endgame

Shadows of the Departed

StartEsha’ntarl / palace progression
Bring / prerequisiteComplete Storms of Fate and required waits
RewardUnlocks Apocalypse Nigh
  1. After Storms of Fate, zone and observe the required Vana'diel-day wait before returning to Ru'Lude Gardens.
  2. Follow the palace cutscene, then travel to the three Promyvion areas named by the quest.
  3. Enter each Promyvion through its Shattered Telepoint and middle Cermet Gate, then climb to the fourth floor.
  4. Examine the Memory Flux in Promyvion-Holla (J-10) for the Holla sliver, Promyvion-Dem (K-4) for the Dem sliver, and Promyvion-Mea (K-6) for the Mea sliver.
  5. Carry Sneak/Invisible where useful, Reraise, and a Warp method; each Promyvion leg is independent and may be completed in any order.
  6. After all three shadows are recorded, return for the Ru'Lude Gardens/palace scene that finishes the quest and opens Apocalypse Nigh after its wait.
Current Reality: if the next scene does not trigger, zone and wait until the next Vana'diel day before re-entering Ru'Lude Gardens.
