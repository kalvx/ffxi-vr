Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 56+ · cap 60 → 65

Whence Blows the Wind

StartMaat · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisiteOrcish, Quadav, and Yagudo Crests
RewardRaises the level cap to 65
Best pairing: complete or flag nation mission 4-1 “Magicite.” The same access key items are required for the strongholds.

Required access

  • Davoi: Crimson Orb
  • Beadeaux/Qulun Dome: Silver Bell, Coruscant Rosary, Black Matinee Necklace
  • Castle Oztroja: Yagudo Torch

Orcish Crest

  1. In Davoi, speak with Sedal-Godjal near (J-8), examine the Wall of Banishing at (J-9), and return for the White Orb.
  2. Touch the four ponds at (L-9), (I-6), (E-8), and (H-10). The fourth curses you; bring Holy Water or Cursna.
  3. Return to Sedal-Godjal for the Crimson Orb. Open the Wall of Banishing at (J-9), enter Monastic Cavern, hug the west wall, and examine the ??? at (J-6).

Quadav Crest

  1. In Beadeaux, use The Mute at Map 2 (G-7) before passing The Afflictor. Maintain Sneak with Silent Oil while silenced.
  2. Return to Map 1, climb the upper route, reach (K-6), and follow the passage to Qulun Dome at (M-8).
  3. Use the three Magicite access key items on the door. Avoid Ruby Quadav and the other true-sound NMs; hug the safer wall and examine the ??? near (I-7).

Yagudo Crest

  1. Climb Castle Oztroja, recording the three daily passwords. Operate the first trapdoor lever, then use the hidden lever pattern on Floor 2 to continue upward.
  2. On Floor 4, light a corner torch and run through the Brass Door before it closes. Use the Yagudo Torch access key item where required.
  3. Enter the three passwords at the Brass Statue. Dismiss Trusts first so they do not remain above when the floor opens.
  4. After dropping, examine the ??? on the first northern platform at (H-6). Two true-sight Yagudo High Priests patrol here; wait, use a tank, then Warp.

Finish

Return to Maat with all three crest key items to raise the cap to 65.
