Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 81+ · cap 85 → 90

Beyond the Stars

StartNomad Moogle · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisite10 Kindred’s Crests · 5 Merit Points
RewardRaises the level cap to 90
  1. Bring 10 Kindred's Crests and hold 5 Merit Points.
  2. Trade the crests to the Nomad Moogle, then speak again to begin the contest.
  3. Win five rounds of the choice game: Raging Beast beats Silent Wind; Silent Wind beats Soaring Dragon; Soaring Dragon beats Raging Beast.
  4. If you fail an attempt, retry; the required number of wins decreases after losses. Finish the scene to unlock level 90.
