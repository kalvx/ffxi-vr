Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 50 → 55

In Defiant Challenge

StartMaat · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisiteAncient Papyrus · Exoray Mold · Bomb Coal
RewardRaises the level cap to 55
THIS IS YOUR LEVEL-50 CAP QUEST. Speak to Maat while on a level-50 job before collecting or trading anything.

Prepare before leaving Jeuno

  • Silent Oils or Sneak
  • Prism Powders or Invisible
  • Reraise item and Warp method
  • Three open inventory slots if using monster drops

You can complete each objective by obtaining the direct monster drop or by touching three ??? points for fragments. The fragment method avoids waiting for rare drops. If the full item is already anywhere in your inventories, the zone's ??? points may report that nothing is there.

1 · Bomb Coal — Garlaige Citadel

  1. Enter Garlaige Citadel from Sauromugue Champaign. Obtain the Pouch of weighted stones key item from the floor ??? at Map 1 (G-8); it lets one player operate Banishing Gates.
  2. Pass the first Banishing Gate and avoid the holes. Undead can detect low HP even through Sneak.
  3. Collect the three Bomb coal fragments on Map 2 at (I-8), (H-7), and (G-6). They combine into Bomb Coal. Alternatively defeat Explosures; stun or move away from Self-Destruct.

2 · Exoray Mold — Crawler's Nest

  1. From the entrance, pass the crawler/bee area, go up the hill, and drop into the first central “sack room.” Take the south-southwest route toward Map 3.
  2. Collect the first crumb on Map 1 near (I-10), then the second on Map 3 at (I-6).
  3. Return through the sack room and take the northwest tunnel. Continue through the green-lit passage, climb and drop into the next sack room, then take the west-southwest tunnel. At the ledge turn right without falling, then take the next left to the dead end at Map 2 (G-10).
  4. The three crumbs combine into Exoray Mold. Alternatively defeat Exorays. Keep them faced away from the party because their breath attacks are conal.

3 · Ancient Papyrus — Eldieme Necropolis

  1. Enter from Batallia Downs at approximately (I/J-10). Do not walk across the square graves; several are trapdoors to the lower floor.
  2. At the Hellhound fork, open the western route using the East Plate in the nearby grave room. A helper can stand on the plate, or a Magicked Astrolabe can operate it.
  3. Collect the three shreds on Map 1 at (H-9), (F-9), and (F-7), following the connected Lich/Brazier rooms westward and then north.
  4. The shreds combine into Ancient Papyrus. Alternatively defeat Liches in the same rooms.

Finish

  1. Return to Maat in Ru'Lude Gardens (H-5).
  2. Trade Ancient Papyrus, Exoray Mold, and Bomb Coal together.
  3. Confirm the completion scene and that your level cap is now 55.
Current Reality check: if Maat refuses the trade, confirm the quest was flagged on a level-50 job and that the three full items—not fragments—are in your main inventory.
