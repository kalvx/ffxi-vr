Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 86+ · cap 90 → 95

Dormant Powers Dislodged

StartNomad Moogle · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisite1 Kindred’s Crest · requested item · 10 Merit Points
RewardRaises the level cap to 95
  1. Speak with the Nomad Moogle and record the exact requested item. Possible requests include Sunsand, Magicked Skull, Damselfly Worm, Crab Apron, Bloody Robe, Dhalmel Saliva, Wild Rabbit Tail, Orcish Axe, Faded Crystal, Yagudo Caulk, Orcish Armor Plate, Quadav Backscale, Dangruf Stone, or Siren's Tear.
  2. Bring that exact item, one Kindred's Crest, and 10 Merit Points. Trade the item and crest while holding the merits.
  3. Speak again for the timing event. After “start,” count ten seconds. The displayed “...” marks the first four seconds; continue the same rhythm and choose Push! at ten.
  4. Retry if early or late. Success awards the Soul Gem key item and raises the cap to 95.
