Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 76+ · cap 80 → 85

Expanding Horizons

StartNomad Moogle · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisite5 Kindred’s Crests · 4 Merit Points
RewardRaises the level cap to 85
  1. Complete New Worlds Await and reach at least level 76.
  2. Accumulate 4 Merit Points and obtain 5 Kindred's Crests. Crests are different from Kindred's Seals.
  3. Trade the five crests to the Nomad Moogle while holding the merits.
  4. Confirm completion and the new level-85 cap before leaving.
