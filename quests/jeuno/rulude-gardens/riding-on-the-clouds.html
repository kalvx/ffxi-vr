Ru'Lude Gardens quest · Level 61+ · cap 65 → 70

Riding on the Clouds

StartMaat · (H-5)
Bring / prerequisite4 Kindred’s Seals
RewardRaises the level cap to 70
  1. Speak with Maat. Write down all four clues exactly.
  2. Bring four Kindred's Seals. Check Shami/Shemo in Port Jeuno and use /itemsearch "Kindred's seal". These are not Kindred's Crests.
  3. Resolve one clue in each starting nation plus one in Selbina or Mhaura. Trade one seal to each correct NPC to receive the Smiling, Scowling, Somber, or Spirited Stone.
  4. Return to Maat with all four key-item stones.
