Lower Jeuno quest walkthrough

Tenshodo Membership

Join the Tenshodo and obtain the key item needed to enter the Tenshodo headquarters freely.

Start NPCGhebi Damomohe
ZoneLower Jeuno — Neptune's Spire
CoordinatesI-7
Required fameJeuno Fame Level 3 to flag the normal quest route
RepeatableNo
Reward[Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card; Tenshodo Invite

Before you begin

For the normal quest route, raise Jeuno fame to level 3. Jeuno fame is influenced by your reputation in the three starting nations as well as Jeuno quests. If Ghebi will not offer the membership dialogue, check your Jeuno fame with Mendi in Lower Jeuno (H-8) and complete more fame-building quests.

Magicite players: this is the membership you need in order to enter the Tenshodo headquarters and continue the Aldo portion of Magicite.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Go to Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and speak with Ghebi Damomohe (I-7) at the counter.
  2. When the dialogue menu appears, move the cursor to the third, blank option and select it. Ghebi explains that membership requires either a Tenshodo Invite or a Tenshodo Application Form.
  3. For the application-form route, travel to Port Bastok and enter Warehouse #2, across from the Airship Agency.
  4. Speak with Jabbar in Warehouse #2 to obtain the Tenshodo Application Form.
  5. Return to Ghebi Damomohe at Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and complete the membership process.
  6. You receive the [Key Item] Tenshodo Member's Card and a Tenshodo Invite. You can now enter the Tenshodo back room/headquarters.

Invite route

If you already possess a Tenshodo Invite, trade it to Ghebi Damomohe as part of the membership process. Retail references disagree across eras/private-server implementations about whether this bypasses the fame flag entirely, so Current Reality should be tested before we label it as a guaranteed fame bypass.

Fast Jeuno fame

Community Service in Lower Jeuno is a repeatable Jeuno-fame quest. Speak with Zauko at (I-6) during the evening and light the twelve street lamps before the time limit. Jeuno reputation also benefits from reputation built in the starting nations. The Jeuno quest directory will list additional fame-building options as they are added.
