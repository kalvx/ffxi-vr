Windurst quest walkthrough

Star Struck

Koru-Moru wants you to find him a rough black stone, for some spaced-out reason.

Start NPCKoru-Moru
Starting zoneWindurst Walls
CoordinatesE-7
FameWindurst
Fame level1
NextBlast from the Past

Required items and key items

  • Torn Epistle
  • Meteorite

Rewards

Cmp. Eye Circlet

Complete walkthrough

  1. Kill Savanna Rarabs in West Sarutabaruta until they drop a Torn Epistle ().
  2. Speak to Koru-Moru while the Torn Epistle is in your inventory.
    • Optional: You can trade Koru-Moru the Torn Epistle at this point for 50 gil, but it isn't necessary.
  3. Head to West Sarutabaruta and kill crawlers there until they drop a Meteorite ().
  4. Trade the Meteorite to Koru-Moru for your reward.
