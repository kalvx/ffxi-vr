Windurst quest walkthrough

In a Pickle

The weighting stone used to make the Rarab Tail Hostelry's specialty dish, "Rarab Tail Pickles," was broken. Chamama wants you to find her a replacement stone that is just the right size, shape, and weight.

Start NPCChamama
Starting zoneWindurst Waters
CoordinatesF-10
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableYes

Required items and key items

  • Smooth Stone

Rewards

200 Gil
Bone Hairpin (First completion only)

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Chamama to begin this quest.
  2. Trade a Smooth Stone to Chamama.
    • Smooth Stones drop off Crawlers in East Sarutabaruta and West Sarutabaruta.
  3. Chamama will not always accept the stone you bring to her. If she doesn't, you must get another one and bring it to her. It's possible to have your stone rejected multiple times in a row.
