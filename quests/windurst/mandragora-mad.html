Windurst quest walkthrough

Mandragora-Mad

Yoran-Oran wants you to bring him unusual items that mandragoras drop, to help in his research on the derivations of the species.

Start NPCYoran-Oran
Starting zoneWindurst Walls
CoordinatesE-5
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableYes

Required items and key items

  • Cornette
  • Four-Leaf Mandragora Bud
  • Snobby Letter
  • Three-Leaf Mandragora Bud
  • Yuhtunga Sulfur

Rewards

120-5,500 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Yoran-Oran, who will request an item. See the below chart for details:
  2. Mandragora in West Sarutabaruta
  3. Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon
  4. Sylvestre in Buburimu Peninsula
  5. Mandragora in West Sarutabaruta
  6. Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon
  7. Records of Eminence NPCs for 50 sparks
  8. Harmodios in Bastok Markets (K-10) (when the nation is 1st or 2nd place in Conquest rankings)
  9. Mandragora in West Sarutabaruta
  10. Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon
  11. Sylvestre in Buburimu Peninsula
  12. Yuhtunga Mandragora in Yuhtunga Jungle
  13. Mourioche in The Boyahda Tree

Notes

1. Maxing fame (level 9) from the minimal level requires approximately 60x Cornettes. Other items may be weighted differently. 2. Reaching level 8 fame requires ~50x Cornettes.
