Windurst quest walkthrough

Toraimarai Turmoil

Become a certified Rhinostery adventurer in order to enter the Toraimarai Canal and cull the Starmites that are causing havoc there. Bring back the shells of three Starmites to receive your reward.

Start NPCOhbiru-Dohbiru
Starting zoneWindurst Waters South
CoordinatesJ-9
FameWindurst

RepeatableYes
PreviousBlue Ribbon Blues

4,500 Gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. If you had just completed the previous quest, you must zone first before you can get this quest.
  2. Speak to Ohbiru-Dohbiru for a cutscene. You will receive a [Key Item] Rhinostery certificate.
    • The [Key Item] Rhinostery certificate provides access to Toraimarai Canal through Windurst Walls (H-3).
  3. Trade Ohbiru-Dohbiru 3 Starmite Shells to complete this quest.
    • Starmite Shells drop off Starmites in Toraimarai Canal.
    • To find Starmites, enter Toraimarai Canal by speaking to Seven of Diamonds Windurst Walls (H-3).
    • Head east at the first intersection.
    • At (I-8) go down the stairs, then head east.
    • Turn right at the two intersections at (I-10).
    • Go up the stairs at (I-9).
    • Head west to (G-9) to find where the Starmites spawn.
    • Once you have 3, you can return to Ohbiru-Dohbiru and trade him the shells to complete the quest.
