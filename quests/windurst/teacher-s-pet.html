Windurst quest walkthrough

Teacher's Pet

Moreno-Toeno would like you to collect one bird feather and one two-leaf mandragora bud to be used as teaching materials in the next magic experiment at the School of Magic.

Start NPCMoreno-Toeno
Starting zoneWindurst Waters (L-6, Northern map)
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
NextMaking the Grade

Required items and key items

  • Bird Feather
  • Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud.

Rewards

250 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Moreno-Toeno in the east Aurastery (North Map) to begin the quest. You will be requested to collect and trade in a Bird Feather and Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud.
    • You may need to speak with them twice.
    • Once this is done, you will be rewarded 250 gil and the quest will be complete.
  2. A Bird Feather can be purchased from the Auction House, but the Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud is exclusive and must be obtained from defeating Mandragora monsters.
    • Low level monsters such as Carrion Crow for the feather and Tiny Mandragora and Mandragora can be found right outside Windurst in East and West Sarutabaruta.
