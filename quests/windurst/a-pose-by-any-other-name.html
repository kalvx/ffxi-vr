Windurst quest walkthrough

A Pose by Any Other Name

Angelica wants you to model for her next painting, but your taste in clothes does not match her current mood. Go Find the clothes she likes and come back wearing them... before she changes her mind.

Start NPCAngelica
Starting zoneWindurst Waters
CoordinatesF-10
FameWindurst
Fame level1
NextA Question of Taste · Picture Perfect
Title*Lower than the Lowest Tunnel Worm

Required items and key items

  • Bronze Harness
  • Leather Vest
  • Robe
  • Tunic
  • Kenpogi

Rewards

Ancient Blood

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Angelica (F-10)
  2. The items she asks you to bring (based upon your job) are:
    • Warrior / Paladin / Dark Knight / Dragoon / Corsair -- Bronze Harness
    • Monk / Bard / Blue Mage -- Robe
    • Thief / Beastmaster / Ranger / Dancer -- Leather Vest
    • White Mage / Red Mage / Black Mage / Summoner / Puppetmaster -- Tunic
    • Ninja / Samurai -- Kenpogi
  3. Talk to Angelica and stand still for 10 seconds.
  4. Talk to Angelica again. Now she tells you to wear your item.
  5. Talk to her the last time, and you've completed the quest.
    • You will receive Ancient Blood and [Key Item] Angelica's autograph.
  6. If you refuse to model and/or you take too long to get the right body armor, you get the title "Lower than the Lowest Tunnel Worm" instead of "Supermodel"

Notes

1. If you're not wearing a body piece you will be unable to start this quest. 2. This quest is timed. If you take too long to come back dressed in the requested item, she will tell you that you are "so yesterday's style" and the quest will end without success. - You can re-attempt the quest by zoning out/in. 3. +1 versions of items do not work.
