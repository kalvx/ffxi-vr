Windurst quest walkthrough

Know One's Onions

There are reports that the Cat Burglar has been loitering around the Ghosty House in Windurst Walls. This looks like a job for the Star Onion Brigade! Gather four wild onions so Kohlo-Lakolo and his gang can create the perfect cat trap!

Complete walkthrough

  1. Before you begin, you are required to zone if you just completed Truth, Justice, and the Onion Way!.
  2. Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo Port Windurst behind the warehouse at (G-5).
  3. Retrieve 4 Wild Onions and trade them to him.
    • In some cases, this will end the quest, showing you the cut-scene and giving you the reward. (Not sure what causes this - possibly having finished the main Windurst storyline)
  4. Head to House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-3) and try to enter to see a cut-scene.
  5. Return to Kohlo-Lakolo and speak with him to complete the quest and receive your reward.
