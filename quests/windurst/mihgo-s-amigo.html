Windurst quest walkthrough

Mihgo's Amigo

If you want to befriend the infamous Cat Burglar, then you'll have to give her something she likes.

Start NPCNanaa Mihgo
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
CoordinatesJ-3
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableNo
NextRock Racketeer
TitleCat Burglar Groupie

Required items and key items

  • 4x Yagudo Necklaces

Rewards

200 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Nanaa Mihgo multiple times till the Quest is flagged.
    • If done as a level 40+ Thief, it will potentially begin the Quests The Tenshodo Showdown (THF AF 1), As Thick as Thieves (THF AF 2) and/or Hitting the Marquisate (THF AF 3). You will need to finish these first to flag this Quest.
    • (Optional): Speak to Cha Lebagta.
  2. You will have to obtain 4 Yagudo Necklaces.
    • They can be purchased from the Auction House. ( ➞ Others ➞ Beast-made)
    • Alternatively, they can be obtained by killing Yagudos () in Tahrongi Canyon or Giddeus.
  3. Trade Nanaa Mihgo the Necklaces to complete the Quest.

Notes

1. Completing this Quest raises your fame in Norg. - It takes 53 completions of this Quest to raise your Norg fame to level 4, which is enough to start The Sahagin's Stash (Utsusemi: Ichi) and Trial by Water. 2. The completion of this Quest is one of the requirements to obtain Nanaa Mihgo's Trust.
