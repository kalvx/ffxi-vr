Windurst quest walkthrough

The Amazin' Scorpio

Soni-Muni wants something that will rekindle the passion for adventure in his mate Gottah. Something to do with the legend of the great grappler Scorpio might do the trick.

Start NPCSoni-Muni
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
FameWindurst
RepeatableNo
TitleGreat Grappler Scorpio?!

Required items and key items

  • Scorpion Stinger

Rewards

1500 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Soni-Muni in Windurst Woods (H-10) to begin the quest.
  2. Obtain a Scorpion Stinger:
    • Maze of Shakhrami: Labyrinth Scorpion or Maze Scorpion
    • Kuftal Tunnel: Diplopod
  3. Trade the Scorpion Stinger to Soni-Muni for your reward.
