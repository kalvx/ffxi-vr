Windurst quest walkthrough

Rock Racketeer

The Cat Burglar wants you to see if a sharp gray rock she "found" is of any value, by having it valued at the Goldsmiths' Guild in Bastok. If the rock is of any value, she wants you to sell it for her.

Start NPCNanaa Mihgo
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
CoordinatesJ-3
FameWindurst
PreviousMihgo's Amigo

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] Sharp gray stone
  • Sharp Stone

Rewards

2,100 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Nanaa Mihgo to receive a [Key Item] Sharp gray stone.
  2. Speak to Ardea, Bastok Markets (H-8)HP#4 and sell the Stone to her for 10 Gil.
  3. Return to Nanaa Mihgo.
  4. Speak to Varun, Windurst Woods (H-9).
  5. Obtain some Pickaxes, then head to the third floor of Palborough Mines.
  6. At (I-9) you will find two Mythril Seams. Mine the western seam for a Sharp Stone.
  7. Trade the Sharp Stone to Varun to complete the quest.
