Windurst quest walkthrough

Glyph Hanger

While transcribing an ancient manuscript, Hariga-Origa chanced upon a glyph that he cannot read. He wants you to track down Ipupu, a specialist in paleography, and hand her a note that contains the unknown letter.

Start NPCHariga-Origa
Starting zoneWindurst Waters
CoordinatesF-8
FameWindurst
Fame level1
NextEarly Bird Catches the Bookworm

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] Note from Hariga-Origa
  • [Key Item] Note from Ipupu

Rewards

Map of the Horutoto Ruins (if you do not already own it)
2,000 Experience Points

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Hariga-Origa in Windurst Waters at (F-8).
  2. You will be given the [Key Item] Note from Hariga-Origa, and asked to deliver it to Ipupu.
    • Ipupu is located in West Sarutabaruta (I-8/J-8), near the entrance to Windurst Waters.
  3. She will accept the item and in return give you the [Key Item] Note from Ipupu to deliver to Hariga-Origa.
  4. After delivering the note, you will complete the quest and receive your rewards.
