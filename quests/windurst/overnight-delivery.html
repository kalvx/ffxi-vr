Windurst quest walkthrough

Overnight Delivery

You couldn't quite make out what Kenapa-Keppa was trying to say, but you guess he wants you to go pick up something from Mhaura. This doesn't look like your ordinary delivery job...?

Start NPCKenapa-Keppa
Starting zoneWindurst Waters South
CoordinatesJ-9
FameWindurst
Fame level2
PreviousFood for Thought
NextWater Way to Go!

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Power Gi

Complete walkthrough

  1. You must zone after completing the previous quest, Food for Thought.
  2. Speak to Kenapa-Keppa after 7:00 Vana'diel time.
    • You need to speak to him four times to officially begin the quest.
  3. Head to Mhaura and speak to Kotan-Purutan at (I-9) - near the ferry ticket area - after 18:00 Vana'diel time to obtain a Small bag.
  4. Return to Kenapa-Keppa before 6:00 to complete the quest.
