Windurst quest walkthrough

A Greeting Cardian

The Spare Cardian you jokingly taught a silly salutation to has grown up...and is unable to forget that greeting. Now that he's a full- grown Cardian, you better find him and undo what you taught him... otherwise, he'll remain a walking joke.

Complete walkthrough

  1. You will have to zone after completing the previous quest.
  2. Talk to Kororo in the Manustery at (H-9) in Windurst Woods.
  3. Talk to the Cardian that Kororo mentions.
    • Choose any of the greetings, it doesn't matter what choice you make.
  4. Leave Windurst Woods, then return after one earth minute and talk to Kororo again.
  5. The Cardian you are looking for is the Five of Spades. He is at the outpost in Buburimu Peninsula.
  6. Go to Buburimu Peninsula and speak to Five of Spades.
  7. Return to Kororo to receive your reward.
  8. This quest cannot be flagged if you have Windurst Mission 9-2 open.
  9. This quest cannot be flagged if you have the quest The Kind Cardian open.
