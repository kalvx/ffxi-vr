Windurst quest walkthrough

Lure of the Wildcat (Windurst)

The mercenary company "Salaheem's Sentinels" has asked you to help recruit new members.

Start NPCIbwam
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
CoordinatesG-11
FameWindurst

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

[Key Item] Green invitation card

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak with Ibwam to recieve a [Key Item] Green Sentinel badge and begin the quest.
  2. Speak to all twenty (20) NPCs in the below chart.
  3. Return to Ibwam. He will exchange your badge for a [Key Item] Green invitation card.

Notes

1. After joining Salaheem's Sentinels, speak to Naja Salaheem with the invitation card, and she will reward you with Imperial currency. The more Lure of the Wildcat quests you have completed, the greater the reward. 2. After joining Salaheem's Sentinels, Alib-Mufalib will offer to teleport you to Aht Urhgan Whitegate. Trade him 300 gil to teleport.
