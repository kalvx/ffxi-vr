Windurst quest walkthrough

Making Amens!

Hakkuru-Rinkuru's switching of Ajido- Marujido's broken wand has been discovered. It seems that the original wand had some special significance to the minister. Hurry and retrieve the original wand from the furnace in Garlaige Citadel before it's too late!

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Kuroido-Moido to begin this quest. Rezone if you just completed the previous quest.
  2. Head to Garlaige Citadel.
  3. Once inside kill Fallen Evacuees in the small rooms near(ish) the entrance until you get a Garlaige Key.
  4. Head past the first two Banishing Gates and use the Garlaige Key to get into the Incinerator room at (I-7) on the third map.
    • Obtain a Pouch of weighted stones from the ??? on the ground on map 1 (G-8). This allows you to pass through the gates alone.
    • Proceed through the Banishing Gate #1 at (I-9) on the first map.
    • Then proceed through the Banishing Gate #2 at (H-10) on the second map.
  5. Speak to Mashira in the Incinerator Room at (I-7) who will hand you a Broken wand.
  6. Return to Kuroido-Moido for your reward.
  7. This quest cannot be completed if you are on Windurst Mission 8-1. If you are on the quest Orastery Woes, you may have to speak to Kuroido-Moido multiple times to complete the quest.
  8. It is recommended that you complete the quest Rubbish Day at the same time as this quest also sends you to the same Incinerator Room.
