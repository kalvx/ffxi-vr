Windurst quest walkthrough

The Tenshodo Showdown

The Tenshodo is gathering expert thieves to its fold. Carry out your orders by handing the Tenshodo envelopes to the appropriate people and having them sign the documents within.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Nanaa Mihgo as a Thief to begin this quest, and she will give you a [Key Item] Letter from the Tenshodo.
    • You must have the Tenshodo Membership to continue with this quest.
  2. Head to Lower Jeuno and talk to Harnek inside of the Tenshodo at (J-7) for a cutscene.
  3. You will be given the [Key Item] Tenshodo envelope.
  4. Travel to Selbina and talk to Elfriede (J-9) of the Shepherd's Muster Inn. You will receive a cutscene with Sneaking Tiger who will ask for a Quadav Stew.
  5. Quadav Stew can only be stolen from certain Quadavs in Beadeaux.
    • The stew can be stolen BEFORE you go to Selbina to save yourself some time.
  6. Head to Beadeaux and steal from Garnet Quadav, Silver Quadav, Zircon Quadav, and Bronze Quadav until you obtain the Quadav Stew.
  7. Once you get the Quadav Stew, go back to Selbina and trade the stew to Elfriede (J-9).
  8. You will receive the [Key Item] Signed envelope.
  9. Head back to Lower Jeuno and talk to Harnek (J-7) for a final cutscene and to receive your reward.

Notes

1. Having Windurst Mission 2-1 active will prevent you from starting this quest. 2. Nanaa Mihgo is involved in several quests/misions, you may have to talk to her multiple times to trigger the correct cutscene.
