Windurst quest walkthrough

Hat in Hand

Advertise Baren-Moren's latest creation by chatting with the local inhabitants of Windurst Waters. He'll pay you according to the number of townsfolk you speak to. But, you are not to leave Windurst Waters before completing the task.

Start NPCBaren-Moren
Starting zoneWindurst Waters
CoordinatesH-7
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
NextA Feather in One's Cap

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

50-200 gil and/or Windshear Hat .

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Baren-Moren (H-7) to begin the quest and get the New model hat.
  2. Mandatory NPCs you need to show the hat to:
    • Bondada inside the hat shop.
    • Honoi-Gomoi in the upper portion of Gingerberry Grove (E-7) (South Map).
    • Located in the residence to the right, however, you must go around the back of the house and up the stairs in order to meet him.
    • Kenapa-Keppa inside the most southern door of the Rhinostery (South Map).
    • Clais on the wooden bridge leading to the Rarab's Tail Hostelery / Timbre Timbers Tavern G-10 (North Map).
    • Kyume-Romeh in the Timbre Timbers Tavern (F-10) (North Map).
    • Tosuka-Porika in the east Optistery building (G-8) (North Map).
    • Pechiru-Mashiru in the east Aurastery building (L-6) (North Map).
    • Machitata near the Mog House (L-11) (North Map).
  3. The more NPCs you talk to, the greater your final reward, up to a maximum of 200 gil and a Windshear Hat.
    • <20 NPCs: You will not get credit or complete the quest
    • 20~29 NPCs: 50 gil, no hat.
    • 30~39 NPCs: 120 gil, no hat.
    • 40~79 NPCs: 150 gil and a Windshear Hat.
    • 80+ NPCS: 200 gil and a Windshear Hat.
  4. After you have shown the hat to the eight mandatory NPCs and 12 additional NPCs (i.e. 20 total), speak to Baren-Moren again to hang-up the hat which completes the quest.
  5. Zoning will reset any NPC spoken to before turning the hat in.
