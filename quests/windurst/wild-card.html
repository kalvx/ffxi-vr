Windurst quest walkthrough

Wild Card

You have been asked to take apart the Joker Cardian and steal its Mana Orb. But... Where, o where has the Joker gone? O where, o where can he be?

Start NPCHonoi-Gomoi
Starting zoneWindurst Waters South
CoordinatesE-7
FameWindurst
Fame level6
PreviousCrying Over Onions
NextThe Promise
TitleDream Dweller

Required items and key items

  • Toraimarai Coffer Key

Rewards

8,000 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Zone after finishing the previous quest.
  2. Speak to Honoi-Gomoi to begin the quest.
    • He is upstairs in his house, which is accessed through a back stairway.
  3. Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo, Port Windurst (G-5).
  4. Head to Windurst Walls (G-3) and check the door at the House of the Hero.
    • Note who you talk to in this cinematic story experience.
  5. Head to Toraimarai Canal (J-10) and open a Coffer there to obtain the Joker card.
    • Picking the coffer as a Thief will work.
    • Various mobs in this zone drops the Toraimarai Coffer Key.
  6. If you spoke to Joker during the previous cut-scene, return to to the House of the Hero for your reward.
  7. If you spoke to Apururu, return to the Manustery in Windurst Woods (use Home Point #3).
  8. Return to Honoi-Gomoi to complete the quest.
