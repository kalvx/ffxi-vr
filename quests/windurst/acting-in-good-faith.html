Windurst quest walkthrough

Acting in Good Faith

Gantineux wants you to help him in his study of white magic. Go to the Eldieme Necropolis and offer up "spirt incense" at the dimly flickering candle to try and appease the spirits of the dead.

Start NPCGantineux
Starting zoneWindurst Waters
CoordinatesE-10
FameWindurst
Fame level4
TitlePilgrim to Mea

Required items and key items

  • [Key Item] Spirit incense

Rewards

Scroll of Teleport-Mea

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gantineux on the second floor of the Rarab Hostelry (E-10). Closest to Home Point #4.
  2. Gantineux will give you the [Key Item] Spirit incense, and ask you to travel to Eldieme Necropolis.
  3. Head to Eldieme Necropolis and look for a ??? that spawns in one of four locations.
    • (I-7) in Eldieme Necropolis, Map 2: near Batallia Downs (G-7) entrance
    • (I-10) in Eldieme Necropolis, Map 2: near Batallia Downs (H-7/H-8) entrance
    • (M-6) in Eldieme Necropolis, Map 2: near Batallia Downs (I-6) entrance
    • (D-5) in Eldieme Necropolis, Map 2: from the Batallia Downs (F-5) entrance.
    • Accessible via Beaucedine Glacier, the Lycopodium Warps, or the Unity Warp to the zone.
  4. Click the ???.
    • The incense will always fail to light.
  5. Go back to Gantineux. He will provide a [Key Item] Gantineux's letter and tell you to deliver it to Eperdur.
  6. Go to Northern San d'Oria and talk to Eperdur (M-7) upstairs in the Cathedral to complete the quest.
    • Closest to Home Point #2.
