Windurst quest walkthrough

The Postman Always K.O.'s Twice

The auction house's postman was knocked out by a monster (again). Ambrosius, who runs the auction house, wants help in recovering all the mail that was lost.

Start NPCAmbrosius
Starting zoneWindurst Walls
CoordinatesJ-12
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
NextTo Bee or Not to Bee?

Required items and key items

  • Damp Envelope
  • Muddy Bar Tab
  • Odd Postcard
  • Torn Epistle

Rewards

Varies depending on quantity delivered in a single trade.
x1: 50 gil
x2: 150 gil
x3: 250 gil
x4: 500 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Ambrosius to begin this quest.
  2. Trade Ambrosius one or all of the following:
    • Muddy Bar Tab drops off of Savanna Rarab in East Sarutabaruta.
    • Torn Epistle drops off of Savanna Rarab in West Sarutabaruta.
    • Odd Postcard drop off Canyon Rarab in Tahrongi Canyon.
    • Damp Envelope drop off Canyon Rarab in Tahrongi Canyon.
  3. The more letters you turn in, the higher your reward will be.
