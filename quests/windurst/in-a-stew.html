Windurst quest walkthrough

In a Stew

Kuoh tried to feed the sick Chocobo some "secret cure-all medicament," but it wouldn't swallow it. She wants to try and hide the taste by mixing it in some of the bird's favorite food, if you can help obtain some...

Start NPCKuoh Rhel
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
CoordinatesK-12
FameWindurst
Fame level3
RepeatableNo
PreviousChocobilious

Required items and key items

  • 3x Woozyshrooms

Rewards

900 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Kuoh Rhel.
    • If you just completed Chocobilious, you will need to zone out/in.
  2. Speak to Matata (K-12).
  3. Speak to Ranpi-Monpi in Windurst Waters (D-9).
    • Trade him three Woozyshrooms to receive [Key Item] Ranpi-Monpi's special stew.
  4. Speak to Kuoh Rhel to complete the quest.

Notes

1. You must wait until the next conquest update if you wish to repeat this quest.
