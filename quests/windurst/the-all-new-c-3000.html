Windurst quest walkthrough

The All-New C-3000

Kopuro-Popuro has created a new type of Cardian that has been instructed to return from patrols with the spoils of battle. To teach the Cardian what it should bring back, you have been asked to find a beetle shell and a hecteyes eye.

Start NPCKopuro-Popuro
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
CoordinatesH-10
FameWindurst
Fame level4
RepeatableYes
PreviousLegendary Plan B
NextCan Cardians Cry?

Required items and key items

  • Beetle Shell
  • Hecteyes Eye

Rewards

600 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. You will have to zone after completing the previous quest.
  2. Speak to Kopuro-Popuro to begin this quest.
  3. Trade Kopuro-Popuro a Beetle Shell and a Hecteyes Eye to complete the quest.
