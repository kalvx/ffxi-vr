Windurst quest walkthrough

Curses, Foiled...Again!?

The dancing spell Shantotto cast on her bothersome Peeping Tom turned out to be a deadly curse. To remove the curse, she'll need a revival tree root, two bomb arms, and some hair from the victim. Hurry, before it's too late!

Complete walkthrough

  1. You must wait one game day and zone after completing the previous Quest before you can activate this Quest.
  2. Speak to Hiwon-Biwon (K-8).
  3. Speak to Shantotto (K-8).
  4. Zone out of the area and then back and speak to Shantotto (K-8).
  5. For her first question, choose either answer. For her second question, choose "No way! Not on your life!" (top option).
    • Your title will become "Tarutaru Murder Suspect."
  6. Shantotto asks you to bring her 2 Bomb Arms, 1 Revival Tree Root, and 1 Hiwon's Hair.
    • To obtain Hiwon's Hair, speak to Hiwon-Biwon again.
  7. Trade the items to Shantotto to complete the Quest.
