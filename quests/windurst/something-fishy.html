Windurst quest walkthrough

Something Fishy

Tokaka can tell the flow patterns and temperature of the ocean currents by looking at certain fish. Bring her any Bastore sardines that you catch so she can help keep the fishermen informed as to where to drop their lines.

Start NPCTokaka
Starting zonePort Windurst
CoordinatesC-8
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableNo

Required items and key items

  • Bastore Sardine

Rewards

70 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Tokaka to begin this quest. Home Point #1 in Port Windurst is extremely close to her.
  2. Trade Tokaka a Bastore Sardine to complete this quest.

Notes

1. You must zone every time you finish this quest before you can have it offered to you again. 2. You can get level 9 Windurst fame if you trade 20 Stacks of fish to Tokaka.
