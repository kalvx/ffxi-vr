Windurst quest walkthrough

Creepy Crawlies

The dhalmel farmer wants you to cull the crawler pests that eat up all the dhalmel food. Bring her three silk threads or three crawler calculi as proof of your efforts in order to gain a reward.

Start NPCIllu Bohjaa
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
CoordinatesI-6
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
TitleCrawler Culler

Required items and key items

  • 3x Silk Thread OR 3x Crawler Calculus

Rewards

600 gil

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Illu Bohjaa to begin this quest.
    • When prompted, you must reply in the affirmative (Yup!) before the quest will begin for the first time.
  2. Trade Illu Bohjaa either 3x Silk Threads or 3x Crawler Calculus.
    • Silk Threads may be purchased from Meriri (Clothcraft Apprentices or higher only), or Kuzah Hpirohpon at the nearby (Home Point #5) Clothcrafting Guild.
    • Both drop off Crawlers in East Sarutabaruta and West Sarutabaruta.
