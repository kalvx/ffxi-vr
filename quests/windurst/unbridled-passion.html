Windurst quest walkthrough

Unbridled Passion

Semih Lafihna has set out to find the truth. Track her down and help her in any way possible.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Talk to Perih Vashai in Windurst Woods (K-7) for a cutscene where she will tell you to go to Mhaura.
  2. In Mhaura speak twice with Koh Lenbalalako (F-9) for a cutscene. She will ask you for a Gold Earring. Trade it to her for a second cutscene and get the key item Koh's letter.
    • Gold Earring can be obtained for free by completing the RoE - Conflict: Xarcabard objective for the first time.
  3. Zone into Xarcabard for a cutscene hinting to look near Castle Zvahl Baileys.
    • Most convenient warp is Survival Guide - Castle Zvahl Baileys
  4. Find the cave at (E-7) in Xarcabard and examine the ??? where you will get another cutscene and then a Koenigstiger will pop. Defeat it.
    • If you are doing multiple, everyone must get their own cutscene and kill. It has a ~3 minute repop.
  5. Now head south to the cave at (E-8) and check the ??? here for another cutscene.
  6. After the cutscene is over, check the ??? again for 99 Ice Arrows.
    • This step may be optional.
  7. Head back to Windurst Woods and talk once more to Perih Vashai for the final cutscene and your reward.
