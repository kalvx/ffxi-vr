Windurst quest walkthrough

To Bee or Not to Bee?

Zayhi-Bauhi, the old man who constantly gives public speeches by the bridge to Heavens Tower, has come down with a sore throat. Follow Raamimi's advice on how to soothe this little pain in the neck.

Start NPCRaamimi
Starting zoneWindurst Walls
CoordinatesF-7
FameWindurst
Fame level2
PreviousThe Postman Always K.O.s Twice

Required items and key items

No advance item requirement is listed.

Rewards

Mulsum x3

Complete walkthrough

  1. If Zayhi-Bauhi speaks normally rather than coughing, try speaking to Kalupa-Tawalupa and Rutango-Botango in front of him to receive the quest.
  2. Speak to Raamimi to receive Honey.
  3. Trade Honey to Zayhi-Bauhi, then speak to Kalupa-Tawalupa and Rutango-Botango.
  4. Trade 4 more Honey, one at a time, to Zayhi-Bauhi.
  5. Return to Raamimi to receive 3 Mulsum.
