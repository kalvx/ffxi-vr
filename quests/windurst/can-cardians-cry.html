Windurst quest walkthrough

Can Cardians Cry?

Apururu beseeches that you find the Queen of Diamonds Cardian, who was sent on an expedition to faraway lands. Seeing that the Cardian could not make it back on her own, it's likely that she's literally gone to pieces somewhere.

Start NPCApururu

CoordinatesH-9

Required items and key items

  • Bruised Starfruit

Rewards

5,000 gil for first completion, then 1,000 gil.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Apururu to begin this quest. There is no need to zone after completing the previous quest.
  2. Kill Cursed Weapons in Xarcabard until you get a Bruised Starfruit.
    • This can also be purchased at the Auction House.
  3. Trade the Bruised Starfruit to Apururu for your reward.
  4. Trading a Bruised Starfruit to Apururu after completing this quest will reward you with 1,000 gil. It is unknown if this is infinitely repeatable.
