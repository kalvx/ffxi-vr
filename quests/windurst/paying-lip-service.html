Windurst quest walkthrough

Paying Lip Service

The apothecary has requested your assistance in gathering ingredients for her most popular preparation. She'll pay you for bringing her three beehive chips or two Remi shells.

Start NPCTapoh Lihzeh
Starting zoneWindurst Woods
CoordinatesI-8
FameWindurst
Fame level1
RepeatableYes
TitleKisser Make-Upper

Required items and key items

  • Beehive Chip ×3
  • Remi Shell ×2

Rewards

150 ~ 200 Gil, depending on what you bring to her.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Speak to Tapoh Lihzeh. Just a little bit North East of HP #1.
    • Will not start quest if Chocobilious is active.
  2. Trade her either 3 Beehive Chips or 2 Remi Shells to complete the quest.
    • Beehive Chips can be purchased for 40 gil from Odoba at the Bastok Mines Alchemy Guild - (K-6). HP #3 is the closest warp.
    • Both can be easily farmed from West Sarutabaruta, from Bees and River Crabs respectively.
  3. Your reward depends on which items you gave her to complete the quest.
    • Trading Beehive Chips reward you with 150 gil.
    • Trading Remi Shells will reward you with 200 gil.
