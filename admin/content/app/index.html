Traverse Vana’diel

Teleporting & Moving Players

12 entries

!zone

Teleport yourself to a zone by numeric ID.

!zone <zoneID>
Example!zone 126

› 126 is Qufim Island.

!goto

Move yourself to an online player.

!goto <player> (forceZone)
Example!goto Primrose

› forceZone defaults to 1.

!bring

Bring an online player to your position.

!bring <player> (forceZone)
Example!bring Primrose

!send

Send one player to a zone or another player.

!send <player> <zone|player>
Example!send Primrose 126

!gotoid

Teleport to a mob or NPC by entity ID.

!gotoid <mobID|npcID>
Example!gotoid 17137706

!gotoname

Find a mob or NPC by name in your current zone.

!gotoname <name> (index)
Example!gotoname Tunnel_Worm

!homepoint

Send yourself or a player to their home point.

!homepoint (player)
Example!homepoint Primrose

!gmhome

Return your GM to its configured home.

!gmhome
Example!gmhome

!return

Return from the current battlefield or instance flow.

!return (player)
Example!return Primrose

!pos

Display or set precise coordinates.

!pos (x) (y) (z) (rot) (zone)
Example!pos 0 0 0 0 126

!up

Move your GM vertically upward.

!up (distance)
Example!up 5

!down

Move your GM vertically downward.

!down (distance)
Example!down 5

Items, Gil, Effects & Key Items

11 entries

!additem

Add an item to your inventory.

!additem <itemID> (quantity) (augments)
Example!additem 4104 12

!giveitem

Give an item to a named player.

!giveitem <player> <itemID> (quantity)
Example!giveitem Primrose 4104 12

!addtempitem

Add a temporary item to yourself.

!addtempitem <itemID> (quantity)
Example!addtempitem 5390 1

!addtreasure

Place an item in the party treasure pool.

!addtreasure <itemID> (targetID)
Example!addtreasure 4104

!setgil

Set an exact gil balance.

!setgil <amount> (player)
Example!setgil 100000 Primrose

!givegil

Add gil without replacing the balance.

!givegil <amount> (player)
Example!givegil 50000 Primrose

!takegil

Remove gil from yourself or a player.

!takegil <amount> (player)
Example!takegil 1000 Primrose

!addeffect

Apply a status effect.

!addeffect (player) <effect> (power) (duration) (subID) (subPower)
Example!addeffect Primrose 253 0 10800

› 253 is Signet. A valid player name must be the first argument.

!deleffect

Remove a status effect.

!deleffect (player) <effect>
Example!deleffect Primrose 253

!addkeyitem

Grant a key item.

!addkeyitem (player) <keyItemID>
Example!addkeyitem Primrose 1

!delkeyitem

Remove a key item.

!delkeyitem (player) <keyItemID>
Example!delkeyitem Primrose 1

Server & Script Reload Commands

6 entries

!reloadglobal

Tier 4

Reload a global Lua without restarting xi_map.

!reloadglobal <globalLua> (I_am_sure)
Example!reloadglobal caskets

› Risky globals can retain stale state.

!reloadquest

Tier 5

Reload one quest container by filename.

!reloadquest <luaFileName>
Example!reloadquest A_Flash_in_the_Pan

!reloadinteraction

Tier 5

Reload the interaction framework.

!reloadinteraction (reloadData)
Example!reloadinteraction 1

!reloaddefaultactions

Tier 5

Reload default actions for this zone or all zones.

!reloaddefaultactions (allZones)
Example!reloaddefaultactions 0

!reloadbattlefield

Tier 5

Reload a battlefield Lua container.

!reloadbattlefield <battlefieldName>
Example!reloadbattlefield hills_are_alive

!reloadnavmesh

Tier 5

Reload the current zone’s navmesh object.

!reloadnavmesh
Example!reloadnavmesh
