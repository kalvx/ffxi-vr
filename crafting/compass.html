Read the two rings

The outer ring is the stable approach: face your crystal’s color for a five-point success bonus. HQ can still occur. The inner ring is the risky approach: its direction trades five success points for a 25% increase to HQ and eligible skillup chances.

A matching Vana’diel day adds two success points and multiplies those chances by 1.10. Matching single weather adds two points and ×1.10; double weather adds three points and ×1.15 instead. Other days and weather are neutral. These are custom Current Reality rules.

PreparationSuccess adjustmentHQ / eligible skillup chance
Safe direction+5 percentage pointsUnchanged
Risky direction−5 percentage points×1.25
Matching day+2 percentage points×1.10
Matching single weather+2 percentage points×1.10
Matching double weather+3 percentage points×1.15; replaces single

Example: a Dark Crystal, northeast risky facing, Darksday, and double Darkness weather combine to ×1.58125 HQ/eligible skillup chance, while the success adjustments cancel out. If your ordinary one-skill recipe odds were 95% success and 50% HQ on success, that alignment would give 95% success and approximately 79% HQ on success.

Success is capped at 99%. Alignment can raise HQ above the ordinary cap, up to an 80% hard limit. HQ is rolled after success; an HQ can also earn an eligible skillup. Recipes without an HQ variant retain their existing outputs. Skillups still require an eligible recipe and room below your rank cap; being far above a recipe helps HQ, but cannot make an ineligible recipe train your skill.

Facing, day, and weather are locked when each synthesis starts. Desynthesis keeps its existing rules. Personal Scholar storms and Mog House weather do not count. Moon phase is informational and adds no crafting bonus.

Craft in the city. Watch the sky.

Crafting works in every weather. Matching weather is an extra opportunity, so you can use the compass without waiting for a perfect forecast.

City regionNatural weather mix
San d’OriaRain and wind, with occasional northern winter snow.
BastokDust and heat fronts, with some rain and thunder.
Windurst and JeunoRain, wind, and thunderstorms.
Whitegate, Al Zahbi, NashmauCoastal rain and wind, with some heat and thunder.

Occasional Light/Dark fronts offer other elemental opportunities. Native weather checks occur every 3–30 minutes; a check can retain the current weather. Forecasts vary by region, and exact transitions can differ between districts. Look at the weather icon in game, then select that condition above.

Your repeated recipe has a compass.

After one normal synthesis captures your recipe, crcrafting shows the crystal’s safe and risky directions, your confirmed server facing, the Vana’diel day, and actual zone weather. Each repeat uses the alignment at the moment that synthesis starts.

The addon provides guidance while the server calculates the results. It never turns your character or changes your crafting stats. Its existing repeat controls and cooldown remain. Before server support is confirmed, it labels the compass as a preview.

Install the matching launcher update on PC or ROG to get the display. No client DAT replacement is required for the compass. Open the Vana’diel calendar →
