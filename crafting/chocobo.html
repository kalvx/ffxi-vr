Crafting & gathering

Chocobo System

Raising, breeding, care, abilities and a searchable Current Reality Chocobo Digging reference in one system without one enormous wall of text.

01

Start here

One chocobo, several connected systems.

Raising develops your personal chocobo. Breeding can pass useful characteristics to offspring. Walks and stories are how a raised bird acquires specialized abilities. Digging is Current Reality's gathering skill and uses your character's actual Digging rank together with the bird's Burrow/Bore traits when checking buried items.

02

Personal chocobo

Raising.

Obtain and register an egg

Obtain a Chocobo Egg and take it to the VCS trainer in the nation where you want to raise it: Hantileon in Southern San d'Oria, Zopago in Bastok Mines, or Pulonono in Windurst Woods. That nation matters because it changes which walk length reaches the NPC stories used for Burrow, Bore and Gallop.

Hatchling through adulthood

The egg stage lasts roughly four Earth days. Use Watch over the Chocobo to build affection. After hatching, name the chick, monitor its condition and choose care plans around the bird you want. Discernment matters for learning abilities: walking gets you the story; telling the story to the chocobo is what teaches the corresponding ability.

For a Current Reality digging bird

Your practical targets are Burrow and Bore. They are not buttons you press while digging. Once learned, they are passive traits that Current Reality checks when building the possible item pools for /dig.

03

Matchmaking

Breeding — from adult bird to a new egg.

1. Reach adulthood

A chocobo must be mature before it can be used for matchmaking. Speak with the VCS trainer responsible for that bird: Hantileon in Southern San d'Oria, Zopago in Bastok Mines, or Pulonono in Windurst Woods.

2. Obtain the parents' Chococards

Ask for a chococard from each mature parent. A male bird produces a Chococard (M) and a female produces a Chococard (F). You need one of each. The two birds do not both have to be yours; the cards are the records Finbarr uses for matchmaking.

3. Go to Finbarr — Upper Jeuno (G-7)

Bring the male and female cards to Finbarr in Upper Jeuno at (G-7). Choose and obtain the appropriate VCS Honeymoon Ticket. The honeymoon plan influences the offspring rather than guaranteeing an exact result, so use breeding as a way to improve the odds of carrying desirable parent traits forward.

4. Start matchmaking

Trade Chococard (M) + Chococard (F) + VCS Honeymoon Ticket together to Finbarr. This starts the matchmaking event. Parent abilities can be inherited, which makes a Burrow/Bore parent especially useful when building a line of digging chocobos.

5. Return for the egg

Return after the matchmaking wait and speak to Finbarr for the resulting Chocobo Egg. Take the egg to the VCS trainer in the nation where you want the new bird raised. Remember that the raising nation determines the easiest walk lengths for the ability stories below.

Digging-bird strategy: inheritance can save part of the story-learning process, but it is not guaranteed. If the offspring does not inherit the desired digging ability, you can still acquire its story through walks and teach it normally.

04

Walks, stories and passive traits

How chocobo abilities are actually learned.

The important distinction is walk → encounter → story → learned ability. A walk does not instantly award Burrow or Bore. Repeatedly encounter the appropriate NPC while taking the correct length of walk; after the required story event, you receive that NPC's story. Then use the raising interaction to tell that story to your chocobo. Discernment affects how readily the bird understands it.

Burrow

Story of a Worrisome Chocobo, obtained through encounters with Zopago. For a bird raised in Bastok use Short Walks; in San d'Oria use Regular Walks; in Windurst use Long Walks. After the story is obtained, tell it to the bird until Burrow is learned.

Bore

Story of a Youthful Chocobo, obtained through encounters with Pulonono. For a bird raised in Windurst use Short Walks; in San d'Oria use Regular Walks; in Bastok use Long Walks. Bore is generally the harder story ability to teach, so stronger Discernment is useful.

Gallop

Story of an Impatient Chocobo, from Hantileon. San d'Oria uses Short Walks; Bastok and Windurst use Regular Walks. Gallop is a learned passive chocobo trait associated with speed. You do not activate Gallop as a command, and Current Reality's Digging roll does not use Gallop to choose loot.

Canter

Story of a Curious Chocobo, from Brutus. Brutus is encountered on Long Walks in all three nations. Canter is the other core passive trait, associated with endurance/riding duration. Like Gallop, there is no Canter button to press and it is not a Burrow/Bore digging-pool gate.

Current Reality distinction: all four abilities can still exist on the raised chocobo, but for our Chocobo Digging implementation the two traits that change the item-selection path are Burrow and Bore. Gallop and Canter remain passive learned chocobo abilities; they are not manually invoked during /dig.

05

Current Reality gathering

Chocobo Digging.

Mount a chocobo, carry Gysahl Greens, and use /dig. Each attempt consumes a green. Current Reality stores Digging as its own real skill and advances it through successful skill-up rolls. Higher Digging rank shortens the digging/zone cooldowns and unlocks rank-gated candidates in the zone tables.

The server's active digging list currently contains 26 configured zones. The finder below is generated from the Current Reality chocobo_digging.lua tables rather than a generic retail list.

08

Raised chocobo layers

Burrow & Bore — Current Reality implementation.

They expand the roll; they do not replace Digging skill

Regular is the normal zone pool. A chocobo with Burrow makes that zone's BURROW entries available, and a chocobo with Bore makes its BORE entries available. Current Reality then still checks the character's Digging rank before a candidate can be awarded. The ability answers which pool may be considered; Digging rank answers whether that particular item is eligible.

Why the walk choice matters

The two digging abilities deliberately send raisers through different walk patterns. A Bastok-raised digger gets the Zopago/Burrow route on Short walks but must use Long walks for Pulonono/Bore. Windurst reverses that relationship: Short for Pulonono/Bore and Long for Zopago/Burrow. San d'Oria uses Regular walks for both. This is why the Compendium identifies the raising nation instead of simply saying “take walks.”

Gallop and Canter are not digging actions

Gallop and Canter are the other two core walk/story abilities commonly encountered while raising a bird. They remain passive learned traits. You do not type a Gallop or Canter command before digging, and Current Reality does not use either one as a key for the BURROW or BORE loot tables. For a purpose-built digger, Burrow and Bore are the important loot-expansion traits.

The old handkerchief/whistle step

Retail FFXI has a separate Chocobo Whistle mini-quest in which Hantileon asks the player to prove the bond with the raised bird. During walks the player searches while the chocobo is distracted for a Handkerchief; an exceptionally strong bond can produce the Dirty Handkerchief version. Returning it to Hantileon leads to the Chocobo Whistle.

Current Reality: that old Handkerchief/Dirty Handkerchief whistle gate is intentionally discarded from the progression described here. It is not the Burrow or Bore unlock and should not be confused with the walk/story system. The Current Reality path keeps the useful raised-chocobo abilities and connects Burrow/Bore directly to the server's expanded digging pools.

Current Reality digging flow: active zone → available Regular/Burrow/Bore layer → Burrow/Bore trait if that layer requires it → character Digging-rank gate → weighted item roll → award.

09

Attempt limits

Fatigue & timers.

Digging uses a persistent dig-count/fatigue system plus cooldowns. Fatigue does not erase permanent Digging skill or rank. Current Reality also checks how recently you entered the zone and how recently you dug; higher Digging rank reduces those waits within the server's configured minimums.

Yield flow: available zone/layer → Burrow/Bore access if required → Digging-rank requirement → weighted roll → awarded item.
