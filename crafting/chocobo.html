Crafting & gathering

Chocobo System

Raising, breeding, care, abilities and a searchable Current Reality Chocobo Digging reference in one system without one enormous wall of text.

01

Start here

One chocobo, several connected systems.

Raising develops your personal chocobo. Breeding can pass useful characteristics to offspring. Abilities such as Burrow and Bore expand the digging pools available to a raised bird. Digging is Current Reality's gathering skill and uses your actual Digging rank when checking buried items.

02

Personal chocobo

Raising.

Obtain and register an egg

Obtain a Chocobo Egg, take it to a Vana'diel Chocobo Society trainer in one of the three starting nations, and begin the raising process. The three primary VCS trainers are Hantileon in the Southern San d'Oria stables, Zopago in the Bastok Mines stables, and Pulonono in the Windurst Woods stables. The nation where you register the egg becomes that bird's raising home.

Hatchling through adulthood

The egg stage lasts roughly four Earth days. Visit and use Watch over the Chocobo to build affection. After hatching, name the chick and continue checking its condition. Care plans influence physical and mental development, while walks and story events can help a bird learn specialized abilities.

Digging-focused bird

If the goal is gathering, plan around learning Burrow and/or Bore. In the original raising system, Burrow is associated with the Story of a Worrisome Chocobo and Bore with the Story of a Youthful Chocobo. Current Reality keeps the useful digging distinction: these abilities open additional item layers, but they do not replace your character's Digging rank requirement.

03

Matchmaking

Breeding — from adult bird to a new egg.

1. Reach adulthood

A chocobo must be mature before it can be used for matchmaking. Once the bird reaches adulthood, speak with the VCS trainer responsible for that bird: Hantileon in Southern San d'Oria, Zopago in Bastok Mines, or Pulonono in Windurst Woods.

2. Obtain the parents' Chococards

Ask the VCS trainer for a chococard. A male bird produces a Chococard (M); a female produces a Chococard (F). You need one of each. The two cards do not have to come from two birds you personally raised—Chococards can represent another player's mature chocobo as well. A Chococard is the breeding record for the bird and is different from the VCS Registration Card used for a retired chocobo.

3. Go to Finbarr — Upper Jeuno (G-7)

Take both parent cards to Finbarr in Upper Jeuno at (G-7). Finbarr handles VCS matchmaking and sells the VCS Honeymoon Ticket. Choose the honeymoon plan according to the kind of offspring you are trying to encourage.

Gourmet: favors Strength and a male chick. Sports: favors Endurance and inheritance from the male parent. Hiking: favors Discernment and a female chick. Jeuno Tour: favors Receptivity and inheritance from the female parent. These are influences, not guarantees.

4. Start matchmaking

Trade Chococard (M) + Chococard (F) + the chosen VCS Honeymoon Ticket together to Finbarr. This begins the matchmaking event. The offspring can inherit characteristics such as parent traits, abilities, statistics or coloration, but breeding is deliberately probabilistic.

5. Return for the egg

After the matchmaking wait, speak to Finbarr again to collect the resulting Chocobo Egg if one was laid. Retail references use the next Japanese midnight as the return point. Once you receive the egg, take it to the VCS trainer in whichever starting nation you want to use as its raising home and begin the raising cycle again.

Digging-bird strategy: if a parent already carries Burrow or Bore, breeding can be used as part of a long-term attempt to produce another specialized digging bird. The honeymoon plan can influence inheritance, but it does not guarantee the desired ability.

04

Specialized birds

Abilities & care.

A raised bird can be developed for specialized digging. In Current Reality's digging data, Regular is the normal zone pool, Burrow and Bore are additional raised-chocobo layers, and Treasure is a special high-end layer. The finder below identifies the exact layer for every configured item.

Important: having Burrow or Bore makes its layer available. The candidate item still has to pass your character's Digging-rank requirement and its weighted roll.

05

Current Reality gathering

Chocobo Digging.

Mount a chocobo, carry Gysahl Greens, and use /dig. Each attempt consumes a green. Current Reality stores Digging as its own real skill and advances it through successful skill-up rolls. Higher Digging rank shortens the digging/zone cooldowns and unlocks rank-gated candidates in the zone tables.

The server's active digging list currently contains 26 configured zones. The finder below is generated from the Current Reality chocobo_digging.lua tables rather than a generic retail list.

08

Raised chocobo layers

Burrow & Bore — Current Reality implementation.

What the two abilities actually do

Burrow and Bore do not simply increase the odds of the normal digging table. They open additional item pools attached to a zone. A normal bird can roll the Regular layer. A bird with Burrow can additionally reach that zone's Burrow entries, while a bird with Bore can reach its Bore entries. Where configured, Treasure remains a separate special high-end layer.

This is why the finder color-codes the layers instead of combining everything into one loot list: the distinction is part of Current Reality's actual digging design.

How Current Reality combines abilities with Digging skill

Opening a Burrow or Bore layer does not bypass progression. Each configured item still carries its own required Digging rank and relative server weight. In practical terms, the server first determines which zone/layers are available to the bird, then your Digging rank determines which candidates you are eligible to receive, and the configured weighted roll determines the result. A newly trained Burrow bird therefore does not instantly gain every Burrow item in the game.

This lets the raised chocobo matter without replacing the character's permanent Digging progression: bird ability controls access; Digging rank controls eligibility.

Where Burrow and Bore came from

In the original raising system, the abilities were learned through chocobo stories and could also be inherited through breeding. Burrow is associated with the Story of a Worrisome Chocobo, traditionally encountered through walks connected with Zopago. Bore is associated with the Story of a Youthful Chocobo, traditionally connected with Pulonono. Retail digging likewise used these abilities to expose items unavailable to ordinary rented chocobos.

Current Reality removes the old handkerchief gate

Current Reality deliberately does not make the player complete the old handkerchief hunt just to make the raised-chocobo system useful. In retail, this was a small unlogged mini-quest commonly documented as the Chocobo Whistle Quest and sometimes called the White Handkerchief Quest. The Dirty Handkerchief was not actually the quest's name—it was the alternate handkerchief key item received when the bond with the chocobo was exceptionally strong.

The retail flow sent the player to Hantileon in Southern San d'Oria, then back to walks with the raised chocobo to search the correct walk area for a Handkerchief or Dirty Handkerchief before returning to Hantileon for the Chocobo Whistle. Current Reality discards that handkerchief/whistle gate from this implementation so the important gameplay loop is the bird you raised, its Burrow/Bore specialization, and your Digging skill—not repeating an awkward legacy mini-quest.

Current Reality design: Regular = baseline digging pool · Burrow = Burrow ability pool · Bore = Bore ability pool · Treasure = special high-end pool. Then Digging rank and item weight decide what the character can actually obtain.

09

Attempt limits

Fatigue & timers.

Digging uses a persistent dig-count/fatigue system plus cooldowns. Fatigue does not erase permanent Digging skill or rank. Current Reality also checks how recently you entered the zone and how recently you dug; higher Digging rank reduces those waits within the server's configured minimums.

Yield flow: available zone/layer → Burrow/Bore access if required → Digging-rank requirement → weighted roll → awarded item.
