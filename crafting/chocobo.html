Crafting & gathering · Chocobo systems

Chocobo Raising, Breeding & Digging

Raise a personal chocobo, breed useful traits and abilities, then develop Chocobo Digging as a gathering skill. Current Reality tracks Digging progression directly and uses your rank when determining which buried items you are qualified to find.

01

Start here

Three systems that work together.

Raising develops a personal chocobo through daily care and care plans. Breeding lets two mature chocobos produce an egg with a chance to inherit useful traits or abilities. Digging consumes Gysahl Greens while mounted and can uncover zone-specific items.

Current Reality: Chocobo Digging has real skill progression. Yellow skill-up messages report gains such as “Chocobo Digging skill rises 0.1 points.” Your Digging rank is also checked against buried-item rank requirements.

02

Personal chocobo

Raising a chocobo.

1. Obtain an egg

Chocobo Eggs can come from chocobo-related quests, certain battlefields, the Ru'Lude Gardens egg seller, or breeding two mature chocobos. Take the egg to a Vana'diel Chocobo Society trainer in one of the three starting nations and begin raising it.

2. Egg and hatchling care

The egg stage lasts about four Earth days. Visit the trainer and use Watch over the Chocobo to build affection. After hatching, name the chick and continue checking its condition. High affection is valuable throughout raising.

3. Choose care plans

Care plans influence physical and mental development. Feed and care for the bird according to the role you want. A digging-focused chocobo is especially valuable if you intend to learn the digging abilities Burrow and Bore.

4. Adult and retirement

A chocobo reaches maturity during the raising cycle and can later be registered/retired. Keep its registration information safe. A personal bird can be used for riding and, when properly developed, specialized digging.

03

Matchmaking

Breeding chocobos.

Once a chocobo is mature, ask its VCS trainer for a Chococard. Male and female birds use separate Chococard items. Breeding uses the cards rather than the retirement registration card.

  1. Obtain a Chococard (M) from the male chocobo.
  2. Obtain a Chococard (F) from the female chocobo.
  3. Visit Finbarr in Upper Jeuno and choose/purchase a VCS Honeymoon plan.
  4. Trade Finbarr the male card, female card, and the appropriate VCS Honeymoon Ticket.
  5. Return after the matchmaking wait to receive the resulting Chocobo Egg, then raise the new chick normally.

Offspring can inherit characteristics from their parents, including a chance at useful abilities. Inheritance is not guaranteed, so breeding a dedicated digging bird may take more than one generation.

Digger goal: breeding and raising are useful ways to build toward a chocobo with Burrow and/or Bore. Those abilities matter because they can expose additional buried-item pools while digging.

04

Gathering skill

Chocobo Digging.

Getting started

You need access to chocobo riding, a mounted chocobo, and Gysahl Greens. Each dig attempt consumes a green. Use /dig while mounted. Early ranks have longer delays, so a “You must wait longer” response usually means the digging timer has not finished.

Current Reality progression

Digging starts at Amateur and gains skill through digging. Current Reality reports those gains directly in the chat log. As skill crosses each 10-point boundary, the Digging rank advances just like the familiar crafting rank progression.

How a yielded item is chosen

Current Reality's digging loop first determines the item pools/layers available for the zone and your chocobo's digging abilities. A candidate item then has its required Digging rank checked against your actual Digging rank. If you do not meet the requirement, that item cannot be awarded. If you do, it can proceed through the normal weighted roll and, on success, become your yielded item.

Practical example: at Amateur/rank 0, an item requiring rank 1 is blocked. When you reach Recruit/rank 1, that item becomes eligible to pass its normal roll. Rank does not guarantee the item—it unlocks eligibility.

Burrow and Bore

Burrow and Bore are specialized chocobo digging abilities. When present on the chocobo, they can open additional digging layers/item pools that a normal rental bird cannot access. Your Digging rank still matters after those pools are available: the item must also satisfy its required-rank check.

That makes a specialized bird valuable, but it also means more possible results can enter the digging pool. Build the bird around the kinds of materials you actually want to hunt.

Fatigue and daily digging

Digging uses a fatigue/dig-count system so a character cannot gather indefinitely in one cycle. Once the limit is reached, stop and return after the reset rather than treating failed attempts as a broken skill. Current Reality keeps this fatigue system separate from your permanent Digging skill and rank.

05

Recommended route

Build a dedicated digger.

  1. Unlock chocobo riding and keep a supply of Gysahl Greens.
  2. Begin Digging immediately and let the skill progress naturally.
  3. Raise a personal chocobo while you continue skilling Digging.
  4. Use breeding if you want to pursue inherited traits/abilities.
  5. Develop a bird with Burrow and/or Bore for access to specialized buried-item pools.
  6. Keep raising Digging rank: higher required-rank items become eligible as your rank advances.
  7. At each new rank, revisit zones where the debugged/current server data shows higher-rank items—you may now qualify for drops that were previously blocked.

Remember: chocobo abilities determine which extra pools can be reached; Digging rank determines whether rank-gated candidates inside the available pools are eligible; the weighted roll still determines whether the eligible item is actually found.

06

Current Reality notes

What is server-specific.

The raising and breeding flow follows FFXI's established VCS systems. Current Reality's Digging implementation adds visible skill-up progression and uses the character's real Digging skill/rank directly in its yield logic. Because private-server tuning can differ from retail guides, use the Current Reality behavior documented here for skill progression, rank gating, fatigue, and Burrow/Bore yield integration.
