Chocobo System

Raising, breeding, care, abilities and a searchable Current Reality Chocobo Digging reference in one system without one enormous wall of text.

01

Start here

One chocobo, several connected systems.

Raising develops your personal chocobo. Breeding can pass useful characteristics to offspring. Abilities such as Burrow and Bore expand the digging pools available to a raised bird. Digging is Current Reality's gathering skill and uses your actual Digging rank when checking buried items.

02

Personal chocobo

Raising.

Obtain and register an egg

Obtain a Chocobo Egg, take it to a Vana'diel Chocobo Society trainer in one of the three starting nations, and begin the raising process. The egg stage lasts roughly four Earth days. Visit and use Watch over the Chocobo to build affection.

Hatchling through adulthood

Name the chick after hatching and keep checking its condition. Care plans influence physical and mental development. Feed and care for the bird around the role you want rather than treating every bird identically.

Digging-focused bird

If the goal is gathering, plan around learning Burrow and/or Bore. Those abilities unlock additional digging layers; they do not replace your character's Digging rank requirement.

03

Matchmaking

Breeding.

  1. Use mature chocobos and obtain a Chococard (M) and Chococard (F) from their trainers.
  2. Visit Finbarr in Upper Jeuno and choose a VCS Honeymoon plan.
  3. Trade the two Chococards with the appropriate VCS Honeymoon Ticket.
  4. Return after the matchmaking wait to receive the resulting Chocobo Egg.
  5. Raise the offspring normally. Useful inherited characteristics are possible but not guaranteed.
04

Specialized birds

Abilities & care.

A raised bird can be developed for specialized digging. In Current Reality's digging data, Regular is the normal zone pool, Burrow and Bore are additional raised-chocobo layers, and Treasure is a special high-end layer. The finder below identifies the exact layer for every configured item.

Important: having Burrow or Bore makes its layer available. The candidate item still has to pass your character's Digging-rank requirement and its weighted roll.

05

Current Reality gathering

Chocobo Digging.

Mount a chocobo, carry Gysahl Greens, and use /dig. Each attempt consumes a green. Current Reality stores Digging as its own real skill and advances it through successful skill-up rolls. Higher Digging rank shortens the digging/zone cooldowns and unlocks rank-gated candidates in the zone tables.

The server's active digging list currently contains 26 configured zones. The finder below is generated from the Current Reality chocobo_digging.lua tables rather than a generic retail list.

08

Raised chocobo layers

Burrow & Bore.

Regular items need no special raised-chocobo digging ability. Burrow rows require Burrow. Bore rows require Bore. Treasure is a separate special layer and contains rare high-rank results in several active zones.

Use the finder with Layer = Burrow or Bore to answer the practical question: what does this ability actually add, and in which zone?

09

Attempt limits

Fatigue & timers.

Digging uses a persistent dig-count/fatigue system plus cooldowns. Fatigue does not erase permanent Digging skill or rank. Current Reality also checks how recently you entered the zone and how recently you dug; higher Digging rank reduces those waits within the server's configured minimums.

Yield flow: available zone/layer → Burrow/Bore access if required → Digging-rank requirement → weighted roll → awarded item.
