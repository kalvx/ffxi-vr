Reading this market

Select an item for Price History. Singles and full stacks have separate stock counts and sales. A stock count is the number of listings; a stack contains the item’s stated stack size. Sales counts cover the last 24 hours of retained completed transactions.

◆ marks an item selected by the current automated seller rotation. Previous rotations can leave stock available. Eligible items outside that catalog may still be listed by players. Red ⊘ means the item has NOAUCTION or no auction category. An unverified category is shown separately.

Completed sales can include automated buyers. Existing seeded market references are labeled “Reference” and never counted as completed sales. Asking prices remain hidden, as in FFXI. This page displays the market; buying and selling take place in game.

The market publishes every two minutes while Current Reality Auction runs. Delivery can add several minutes. Check the inventory timestamp; after six minutes without a fresh snapshot, counts are marked stale.
