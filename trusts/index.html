Current Reality party lab · Phase III

Trust Team Generator

Build from the player outward: choose job → level → weapon type → weapon skill, then tune the strategy and preferred chain. Weapon choices follow the selected job, and weapon skills are unlocked by the selected level before the team generator evaluates Trust AI, roles, skillchain partners, and burst opportunities.

Phase III: Job, level, weapon type, and weapon skill are now treated as the core build. Level gates the weapon-skill list; strategy and preferred chain refine the result rather than replacing those required player choices. The AI-aware scoring continues to penalize Trusts that are likely to interfere with a player-controlled chain.

Summon and dismiss

Summon by magic/ma "Kupipi" <me>

Replace Kupipi with the exact Trust spell name. Trusts can also be selected from Magic → Trust.

Dismiss the targeted Trust/refa <t>

Target the Trust first. The leader must not have a weapon drawn.

Dismiss every Trust/refa all

Long forms also work: /returnfaith and /returntrust.

Enable synchronized emotes/emotetrust on

Use /emotetrust off to disable Trust reactions to supported emotes.

Party limits and rules

Starting capacityUp to three Trusts at once.
Maximum capacityUp to five Trusts after the applicable Rhapsodies of Vana’diel key items, filling a six-member party with the player.
Who summonsOnly the party leader can summon Trusts.
CombatNormally cannot summon after the leader has generated enmity. Summon before pulling.
DuplicatesDifferent versions of the same NPC normally cannot be active together, with limited exceptions.
ScalingTrust level follows the summoner. Before level 99, dismiss and resummon after leveling so their stats refresh.
AlliancesA player/Trust party cannot form an alliance.

Unlocking and learning Trusts

  1. Reach level 5 and complete the Records of Eminence objective Call Forth an Alter Ego when available.
  2. Begin one of the three starter-nation Trust initiation quests.
  3. Use mission, quest, campaign, event, or Unity requirements to obtain additional alter egos. Many arrive as a Cipher item.
  4. Trade a Cipher to a Trust initiation NPC in a starter nation.
  5. Summon outside combat with /ma "Trust Name" <me>.
