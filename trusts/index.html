Trusts

Purchase companions from Trusty-Musky, check every seal price, and build your party. Legacy acquisition notes remain available as optional references.

Buy Trusts from Trusty-Musky

Open the Trusty-Musky purchase and menu guide →

Mhaura · near Home Point #1, at X −10.990, Y −15.813, Z 88.810. Speak to him to browse 119 purchasable Trusts. Monberaux is unavailable; Alchemist is a playable job on Current Reality.

  1. Open All shadows, Basic shadows, or Other shadows.
  2. Select a Trust to see its role and Kindred’s Seal price, then select Choose this shadow.
  3. Within three minutes, trade the exact requested number of Kindred’s Seals and no other items. Beastmen’s Seals and gil do not count. Speak to him again to cancel or choose another Trust.
  4. The purchase permanently teaches that summon. Purchased entries remain in the catalogue as [Learned] and cannot be bought again.

Trading seals before choosing a Trust opens a free catalogue preview; those seals are not consumed. The introduction appears once and offers Skip to catalogue. Later visits open the menu directly. How does this work?, Your automaton?, and Leave remain available.

No Shadow Lord clear or nation Trust tutorial is required to buy or summon a purchased Trust. Existing Trust rosters reset once on each character’s first login under this system. Subsequent purchases are saved.

Caution: Legacy Trust quests have not been verified on Current Reality. Proceed with caution when following the retained quest guides. These are optional references; purchase your Trusts from Trusty-Musky.

Legacy Trust quests retain their other progression and rewards. A matching quest meeting or redeemed Cipher records a one-time one-seal discount for that Trust, with a minimum purchase price of one seal. Repeating the same memory does not increase the discount. Cipher redemption still uses the original nation NPC and permit requirements.

Trust Team Generator

Build from the player outward: choose job → level → weapon type → weapon skill, then tune the strategy and preferred chain. The optimizer now recognizes pet jobs that can participate in skillchains, so a Summoner, Beastmaster, or Alchemist does not automatically need a TP-using Trust to make the chain. That can make healers, buffers, tanks, bursters, and low-TP-interference Trusts the better fit.

Pet-aware planning: SMN uses verified Current Reality avatar Blood Pact properties; BST is treated as pet-skillchain capable through damaging Ready moves without inventing a jug-specific route; ALC uses verified automaton weapon-skill properties but marks frame/automaton-skill-dependent routes as conditional. DRG wyvern breaths are not counted as normal weapon-skill skillchain steps. Each calculation produces multiple close team plans with an estimated success percentage. If skillchains or bursts are not your goal, try Support / Buffs to favor utility instead of TP timing.

Summon and dismiss

Summon by magic/ma "Kupipi" <me>

Replace Kupipi with the exact Trust spell name. Trusts can also be selected from Magic → Trust.

Dismiss the targeted Trust/refa <t>

Target the Trust first. The leader must not have a weapon drawn.

Dismiss every Trust/refa all

Long forms also work: /returnfaith and /returntrust.

Enable synchronized emotes/emotetrust on

Use /emotetrust off to disable Trust reactions to supported emotes.

Trust summoning macros

Trusts are available across jobs. Find their summoning commands here, separately from each job’s magic and macro lists. Search by Trust name or role and copy one macro line; every command targets <me>.

Use the CR Paste helper to put a copied command into a macro line. Summon before pulling, subject to the party rules below.

Party limits and rules

Buy five Trusts and summon all five: purchase guide →

Starting capacityUp to five purchased, compatible Trusts, within the six-member party limit.
Maximum capacityFive Trusts plus your character fill the party. Rhapsody in White and Rhapsody in Crimson are no longer required for the fourth and fifth slots; their other mission rewards remain.
Who summonsOnly the party leader can summon Trusts.
CombatNormally cannot summon after the leader has generated enmity. Summon before pulling.
DuplicatesDifferent versions of the same NPC normally cannot be active together, with limited exceptions.
ScalingTrust level follows the summoner. Before level 99, dismiss and resummon after leveling so their stats refresh.
AlliancesA player/Trust party cannot form an alliance.
