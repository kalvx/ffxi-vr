City weather and seasonal dress

Current Reality’s home nations, Jeuno and Whitegate-region cities now have natural elemental forecasts. Matching actual zone weather improves crafting; the weekday does not determine the weather. See the regional weather guide.

Auto follows the real-world calendar in the server’s Pacific time zone. Halloween, Christmas and New Year follow the dates below. The active season changes on the next server tick after real-world midnight; Vana’diel time does not change the holiday.

  • Halloween: October 20–November 1. Existing NPC costumes and four shuffled town songs.
  • Christmas: November 2–December 31. Existing Starlight decorations and five shuffled town songs, with fireworks and occasional cosmetic lightning.
  • New Year: January 1–7. Fireworks and occasional cosmetic lightning continue; Christmas decorations and music end.
  • January 8–October 19: normal town appearances and music.

Fireworks run in the present-day districts of San d’Oria, Bastok, Windurst and Jeuno, plus Aht Urhgan Whitegate, Selbina and Mhaura. Displays are decorative, exclude Mog Houses and do not change actual weather, elemental crafting effects, damage, NPC routes, quests or rewards.

The administrator’s existing holiday controls retain Auto / On / Off. Turning one holiday On switches the other Off; Christmas On includes the display. New Year follows both controls in Auto. Both Off restores normal towns. Settings survive Map restarts.

The shared server applies this cycle to every player. To receive all nine original holiday songs and previous client fixes, close the game and use Update in the Current Reality launcher for cumulative version 1.0.27. Fireworks and lightning use existing native client effects.
