Blue Crystal Locations

Find the six added blue crystals, touch each one to collect its destination, and use your travel menu to return.

Look for the blue crystal

Holla, Dem, and Mea are beside the Shattered Telepoints. Their blue crystals are at the broken telepoint circles. The ordinary intact telepoints are separate landmarks elsewhere around those crags.

Touch the added blue crystal itself to unlock its destination. Collecting the original gate-crystal key item does not register this custom travel point, and that key item is not required to register it.

  1. Travel to the area and find the location below.
  2. Target the blue object named Holla Crystal, Dem Crystal, Mea Crystal, Altep Crystal, Yhoat Crystal, or Vahzl Crystal, then interact with it.
  3. After collection, the destination becomes available under Gate crystals in the home-point travel menu.
  4. Travel to an unlocked added crystal costs 500 gil. Each destination must be collected separately.

The original telepoints, gate-crystal key items, and White Mage teleport spells retain their existing functions.

Holla · La Theine Plateau

Find the Shattered Telepoint at X 340, Z −60. Holla Crystal is immediately beside it, at X 343.0816, Y 19.2040, Z −64.5199.

The intact Holla Telepoint is at X 420, Z 20, about 115 yalms away from the blue crystal. If you are beside that intact telepoint, head toward the shattered circle at X 340, Z −60.

Dem · Konschtat Highlands

Find the Shattered Telepoint at X 140, Z 220. Dem Crystal is immediately beside it, at X 143.0816, Y 19.2040, Z 215.4801.

The intact Dem Telepoint is a separate location at X 220, Z 300. Look around the broken telepoint circle for the blue crystal.

Mea · Tahrongi Canyon

Find the Shattered Telepoint at X 180, Z 260. Mea Crystal is immediately beside it, at X 183.0816, Y 35.2500, Z 255.4801.

The intact Mea Telepoint is a separate location at X 100, Z 340. Use the shattered circle as your landmark.

Altep · Eastern Altepa Desert

Find the intact Telepoint at X −61.942, Z 224.900. Altep Crystal stands roughly 13 yalms from it, at X −70.9420, Y 3.9380, Z 215.9000.

Yhoat · Yhoator Jungle

Find the intact Telepoint at X −280.942, Z −144.156. Yhoat Crystal stands roughly 13 yalms from it, at X −293.3848, Y 0.4015, Z −142.3697.

Vahzl · Xarcabard

Find the intact Telepoint at X 150.258, Z −37.256. Vahzl Crystal stands roughly 13 yalms from it, at X 159.2580, Y −21.0000, Z −46.2560.

Gobbald-telefix in Upper Jeuno offers separate outpost journeys for 75 gil.

If you cannot find or use a crystal

  • Holla, Dem, or Mea: check that you are at the shattered circle rather than the intact telepoint.
  • Destination missing from Gate crystals: interact with that destination’s added blue crystal in person first.
  • You already have the original gate-crystal key item: the added travel point still needs its own first interaction.
  • Travel is refused: check your gil, combat status, and distance from the crystal.
