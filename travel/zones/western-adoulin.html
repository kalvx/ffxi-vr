Getting to Western Adoulin

Reach Adoulin legitimately from Jeuno, unlock its transportation network, and know which exits lead into the Ulbuka frontier.

First-time walkthrough

  1. Go to Lower Jeuno and begin the Adoulin access chain. Complete The Geomagnetron. This grants the Adoulinian charter permit and access to the waypoint system used to reach Adoulin.
  2. Use the Lower Jeuno Waypoint. Waypoints consume Kinetic Units. On first-time exploration, activate each useful Waypoint you encounter so future travel can bypass the long walk.
  3. Enter the Adoulin region. If you arrive in Ceizak Battlegrounds, Western Adoulin connects at Ceizak (K-7) ↔ Western Adoulin (C-9). Once inside the city, activate the Home Points and Waypoints you pass.
  4. For the Waterfront, stay in Western Adoulin. It is not a separately unlocked city zone. Head toward the northwest/waterfront side. The Rala Waterways entrance at Western Adoulin (F-5) connects to Rala Waterways (B-5).
  5. For future frontier travel, use the city-to-Ceizak exit. Western Adoulin (C-9) leads to Ceizak Battlegrounds (K-7). Ceizak then connects onward to Sih Gates and Yahse Hunting Grounds.

Sneak & Invisible

Inside Western Adoulin: neither effect is normally necessary. It is a city.

Ceizak and later frontier/dungeon routes: carry Silent Oils and Prism Powders if you cannot cast Sneak and Invisible. Do not assume both effects make every route safe: some enemies use other detection types, and Colonization Reives can physically block paths regardless of invisibility.

When following a Current Reality route page, use its specific protection note instead of blindly applying both effects. Sneak corresponds to sound detection and Invisible to sight detection; the route may require one, both, or neither.

Activate these before leaving

  • Western Adoulin Home Point #1: around (E-9).
  • Western Adoulin Home Point #2: around (H-12).
  • Western Adoulin Waypoints: touch destinations as you discover them; destinations generally must be attuned before they become convenient return points.
  • Ceizak Waypoint / Frontier travel points: activate them during exploration to shorten later GEO, mission, and coalition travel.
