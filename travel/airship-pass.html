Permanent key item

Airship Pass

Unlocks the regular airship routes connecting Port Jeuno with San d’Oria, Bastok, and Windurst.

At a glance

Key itemAirship pass
Normal routeComplete your nation’s Rank 4 final mission and begin Rank 5-1; the Rank 5 story awards the pass.
Purchase routeDerrick, Lower Jeuno (H-7), 500,000 gil.
Routes openedPort Jeuno ↔ Port San d’Oria, Port Bastok, and Port Windurst.

Mission route

  1. Complete your home nation missions through Rank 4. Keep enough Rank Points to accept the next mission from a gate guard.
  2. Accept your nation’s Rank 4 finale: Magicite. This route sends you into the three Jeuno-area beastman strongholds.
  3. Complete the mission objectives in Davoi, Beadeaux, and Castle Oztroja, then return as directed by the mission walkthrough.
  4. Finish the Jeuno cutscenes and report to your nation. Promotion to Rank 5 grants access to the airship story progression and the permanent pass.
  5. Verify under Main Menu → Key Items that Airship pass appears before walking to a departure counter.

Use the nation-specific Magicite page for every NPC, coordinate, key item, and stronghold route; this page only covers the pass itself.

500,000-gil route

  1. Travel to Lower Jeuno.
  2. Find Derrick at (H-7), near the air travel agency.
  3. Speak to Derrick and choose the purchase option.
  4. Confirm payment of 500,000 gil. The purchase is permanent; do not buy it if you intend to finish Rank 5 soon.
  5. Check Key Items for the pass, then proceed to Port Jeuno.

Using the pass

San d’Oria terminalPort San d’Oria (H-5)
Bastok terminalPort Bastok (H-8)
Windurst terminalPort Windurst air travel agency
Jeuno terminalsPort Jeuno; each destination has its own counter and gate.
FareRegular routes normally charge 200 gil per trip.
  1. Speak with the departure counter NPC for the desired route.
  2. Pay the fare and pass through the gate into the departure lounge.
  3. Board when the airship arrives. If it has just departed, wait for the next scheduled flight.
  4. After arrival, leave through the destination terminal; the pass remains in Key Items.

Troubleshooting

Counter refuses entry

Confirm you have the standard Airship pass, not only the Airship pass for Kazham.

Mission did not award it

Finish the final report/cutscene and check your Rank. A mission objective alone may not complete the promotion.

Wrong terminal

Kazham uses a separate Port Jeuno counter and separate pass.
