Jeuno ↔ Kazham

Airship Pass for Kazham

Earn the permanent pass with three beastman chest keys, or buy it outright.

Start and requirements

Start NPCGuddal, Port Jeuno (I-7), Kazham airship counter.
Key routeGhelsba Chest Key + Giddeus Chest Key + Palborough Chest Key.
Purchase route148,000 gil paid to Guddal.
RewardAirship pass for Kazham (permanent key item).
  1. Speak to Guddal before farming. Review both the key and gil choices.
  2. Free at least three inventory slots. These are items, not temporary key items.
  3. Choose the key route below or pay 148,000 gil and skip to the finish.

Collect all three chest keys

Ghelsba Chest Key

Travel from West Ronfaure into Ghelsba Outpost and continue through the Orc-controlled Ghelsba/Fort Ghelsba/Yughott areas. Defeat key-dropping Orcs appropriate to the dungeon tier until the key enters inventory.

Giddeus Chest Key

Travel from West Sarutabaruta into Giddeus. Defeat the deeper Yagudo types that carry the chest key; check Treasure Pool and inventory before leaving.

Palborough Chest Key

Travel from North Gustaberg into Palborough Mines. Fight Quadav in the deeper mine sections until the key drops. Use the mine elevators and map connections to move between floors.

A chest key drop can remain in the Treasure Pool. Lot or pass deliberately—do not zone before it resolves.

Turn in and board

  1. Return to Port Jeuno (I-7) with all three keys in inventory.
  2. Trade the Ghelsba Chest Key, Giddeus Chest Key, and Palborough Chest Key to Guddal together.
  3. Complete the dialogue and verify Airship pass for Kazham under Key Items.
  4. Use the nearby Kazham counter and departure gate. This terminal is separate from the three-nation airships.
  5. On arrival in Kazham, activate the nearby Home Point before continuing into Yuhtunga Jungle.

Troubleshooting

Guddal does not accept the trade

Confirm the exact three key names and trade all three at the same time.

No key drops

You may be fighting the weakest beastmen near the entrance. Move deeper and confirm the mob family’s key drop table.

Cannot use normal airships

This pass only opens Kazham. The standard Airship pass is separate.
