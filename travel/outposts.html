Conquest transportation

Outpost Supply Runs & Warps

Deliver your nation’s supplies during the correct conquest week to permanently register each regional outpost.

Gobbald-telefix · Upper Jeuno

Gobbald-telefix offers outbound outpost travel from Upper Jeuno for a flat 75 gil per journey. Find him at X −3.5176, Y −0.0001, Z 25.3962, beside the stone wall and steps shown below. Renik and the street lamp are visible nearby in this reference photo.

Use his menu

  1. Speak to Gobbald-telefix. His menu lists the outpost regions currently available to your character, each marked 75g.
  2. Select the region, then select Travel – 75 gil to confirm. Back returns to the destination list; Leave closes the menu.
  3. Hold still while he prepares the teleport. Once casting succeeds, 75 gil is deducted and the teleport takes you to that region’s outpost destination.

He uses your existing outpost registrations and level eligibility. A supply run is still needed where the normal outpost system requires one. This service does not register an outpost or bypass an area’s progression requirements. Other national teleport NPCs keep their own fares.

Nothing offered? Check your completed supply runs and character level. Teleport canceled? Stand close, remain still during the cast, and keep 75 gil available. No fare is taken for a canceled cast; if the teleport cannot start after payment, the fare is returned.

Looking for the blue Holla, Dem, Mea, Altep, Yhoat, or Vahzl crystal? Those are a separate travel service.

Before accepting

AllegianceYou must accept supplies from your current nation’s guards.
ControlYour nation must control the target region during the current conquest tally.
SignetObtain Signet while speaking to an appropriate conquest guard.
TimingRegional control can change at the weekly conquest tally; accept and deliver before the next tally.

Complete a supply run

  1. Check Region Info and find a region currently controlled by your nation that you have not registered.
  2. Speak to a conquest guard in your home nation and choose Supplies or the supply-mission option.
  3. Select the desired region. The guard gives you the region’s supplies as a temporary/key-item objective.
  4. Travel to that region’s outpost without changing nations or allowing the conquest tally to invalidate the assignment.
  5. Speak to the outpost guard and deliver the supplies. Finish all dialogue until the guard confirms delivery.
  6. Return to your home-nation outpost-warp NPC when convenient. The region should now appear in the destination list.

Use an unlocked outpost

  1. Speak with your nation’s outpost teleport NPC.
  2. Select a registered region. Availability and gil cost depend on level, allegiance, and conquest conditions.
  3. Confirm the destination and fee to warp from the city to the regional outpost.
  4. For the return trip, speak with the regional outpost guard and select the teleport option when available.

Changing allegiance does not erase completed supply-run registrations in modern behavior, but Current Reality-specific travel overrides should be verified in game before documenting a custom exception.

Troubleshooting

Region is not offered

Your nation may not control it this week, or you may already be carrying supplies for another region.

Destination missing after delivery

Finish the outpost guard dialogue, then reopen the warp NPC menu. Verify you used the correct region’s outpost.

Warp refused

Check minimum level, gil, nation allegiance, and whether the NPC offers inbound or outbound travel.
