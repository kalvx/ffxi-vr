How Do I Get There?

Pick a destination and follow the first-time route. These guides emphasize legitimate travel: required quests or key items, zone-by-zone directions, coordinates, dangerous stretches, Sneak/Invisible advice, and the Home Points, Survival Guides, and Waypoints worth activating on the way.

Travel kit: Carry Silent Oils for Sneak and Prism Powders for Invisible when your job cannot cast the effects. Reapply before they expire, but remember that some monsters detect by methods other than sight or sound. A route guide will call out the protection appropriate to that stretch.
