How Do I Get There?

Pick an expansion, region and destination. Opening a mapped Route A/B/C turns the walkthrough into a visual travel strip: each box is a leg of the trip and the arrows show exactly what comes next.

Travel kit: Silent Oil = Sneak and Prism Powder = Invisible when you cannot cast them yourself. Reapply before they expire. Some enemies detect by methods other than normal sight or sound, so mapped routes call out special danger where known.

World directory

Visual route maps: Open any ROUTE MAPPED destination, then expand Route A/B/C. The animated arrow diagram gives the quick path; the numbered walkthrough directly underneath keeps the full instructions.
