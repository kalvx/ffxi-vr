Zone Field Guides & Route Maps

Pick an expansion, region and area. Every card is built as a zone field guide: a mini-map schematic shows where the area connects, exit coordinates sit near their approximate map position, and reciprocal exits are used to show the destination-side arrival point when available.

Travel kit: Silent Oil = Sneak and Prism Powder = Invisible when you cannot cast them yourself. Zone guides can also identify sight, sound, scent, true sight and true sound threats so you know which protection actually works.

World directory

How to read the mini-map: the current zone stays in the center. Exit boxes are placed roughly by their FFXI map coordinate and arrows point outward to connected areas. Multi-map exits keep their Map 1/2/3 labels. Detailed Route A/B/C walkthroughs appear beneath the connection map when a longer controlled path is needed.

Each zone guide can include

ConnectionsNeighboring zones, current-map exit coordinate, reciprocal arrival coordinate, map/floor labels and special transport links.
Mobs & detectionNotable monster families plus sight, sound, scent, true sight, true sound or special aggro behavior.
NPCsImportant quest, mission, travel, vendor, map, chocobo and system NPCs with coordinates.
Chocobo digging / gatheringDigging finds plus logging, harvesting, mining or excavation when the zone supports them.
ItemsUseful zone-specific drops, caskets, keys and other obtainable items.
Travel walkthroughExactly how to reach the next zone, including doors, keys, Reives, one-way drops and Sneak/Invisible advice.
