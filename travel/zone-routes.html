How Do I Get There?

Browse by expansion and region, then open the exact zone you want. Route cards are designed for legitimate first-time travel and can hold multiple Route A/B/C entries, map/floor changes, exits, coordinates, quest or key-item gates, Sneak/Invisible advice, and shortcuts worth activating.

Travel kit: Silent Oil = Sneak and Prism Powder = Invisible when you cannot cast them yourself. Reapply before they expire. Some enemies detect by methods other than normal sight or sound, so route notes call out special danger where known.
