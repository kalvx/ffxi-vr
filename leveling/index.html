Level 1–99 Field Guide

A practical route for solo players using Trusts, small groups, and traditional parties. Each band gives a dependable camp, alternatives, milestones, and the point at which you should move on.

Current Reality rule: enemy placement, experience rates, expansion access, and individual scripts can differ from retail references. Treat the level bands as safe starting ranges: use Check, move when enemies become Too Weak or kills slow down, and report a server-specific mismatch so this route can be refined.
Prepare1–99 routeLimit breaksTrust partiesSkills & gearAfter 99

Before the first pull

Set your safety netTouch Home Points and Survival Guides, carry a scroll or source of Reraise when practical, and set a nearby Home Point before entering a dungeon.
Keep experience bonuses activeUse an experience ring if available, keep Signet or the appropriate regional status active, and complete repeatable Records of Eminence objectives alongside normal kills.
Refresh your TrustsTrust levels are set when summoned. After gaining several levels, dismiss and summon them again so their stats catch up.

Starter-nation routes

San d’OriaWest/East Ronfaure → La Theine Plateau → Valkurm DunesBegin close to the city gate. Work outward only when nearby Orcs, worms, rabbits, and sheep become easy.
BastokSouth/North Gustaberg → Konschtat Highlands → Valkurm DunesAvoid higher-level Quadav and goblin camps until Check shows a manageable target.
WindurstWest/East Sarutabaruta → Tahrongi Canyon → Buburimu PeninsulaUse the wide starter fields first; Yagudo and linking groups can punish a new solo player.
Party ruleFight enemies the group can defeat continuouslyFast, safe chains beat long rests or repeated deaths. A camp is wrong if the healer empties MP every pull.

Trust and party templates

Safe solo core

Tank + healer + support or damage

Start with survival. Add damage only after the tank holds enemies reliably and the healer finishes fights with MP remaining.

Player is the tank

Healer + support + damage

Use a support Trust that improves accuracy, attack, haste, or MP efficiency. Avoid another tank fighting you for positioning and enmity.

Player is the healer

Tank + two damage

Keep one emergency support or backup healer until you know the camp’s area attacks and status ailments.

Skillchain route

Tank/healer foundation + compatible closer

Use the Trust Team Generator and Skillchain planner. More weapon skills are not automatically better if Trusts interrupt the planned chain.

Do not outlevel your character

  • Equip the weapon you intend to use before a long session.
  • Keep healing, enhancing, enfeebling, elemental, and defensive magic moving with the job.
  • Buy essential spells and tools before cosmetic upgrades.
  • Favor accuracy when attacks miss repeatedly; missed swings produce no TP.
  • Replace weapons more often than minor armor pieces while leveling.
  • Carry food appropriate to the problem: accuracy, attack, defense, or MP.
  • Upgrade the main armor set at meaningful milestones instead of every level.
  • Save job-specific and Artifact quests for natural travel breaks.

Warning sign: if Even Match enemies take a long time because attacks miss or spells resist, changing camps will not fix the underlying skill or equipment problem.

First priorities at level 99

  1. Finish useful story progression. Missions unlock travel, zones, Trust capacity, and endgame systems.
  2. Obtain an item-level weapon and armor foundation. Level 99 equipment varies enormously; character level alone is not a useful measure of readiness.
  3. Cap the weapon and magic skills your job actually uses. Repair any gap created by accelerated leveling.
  4. Build job points or the Current Reality equivalent only after basic accuracy and survival are stable.
  5. Create role-specific equipment sets. TP, weapon skill, casting, healing, idle, and defensive sets serve different purposes.
  6. Choose one job to finish first. A well-equipped main job makes future unlocks and alternate jobs much easier.
