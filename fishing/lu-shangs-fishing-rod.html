Port San d’Oria fishing quest

Lu Shang’s Fishing Rod

Choose one of two competing brothers, support only his side, and deliver a cumulative 10,000 carp to earn one of Vana’diel’s most dependable fishing rods.

NPCsJoulet or Gallijaux
LocationPort San d’Oria (H-8), beside the water
Quest namesJoulet: The Competition
Gallijaux: The Rivalry
Required total10,000 Moat Carp and/or Forest Carp
RewardLu Shang’s Fishing Rod
Level restrictionNone for accepting, trading carp, or completing the quest

The two brothers

Joulet — The Competition

Joulet is the younger brother. Speak with him and accept The Competition if you want to help him defeat his elder brother’s fishing record.

Gallijaux — The Rivalry

Gallijaux is the elder brother. Speak with him and accept The Rivalry if you want to defend his position against Joulet.

Pick one brother and stay with him. The brothers maintain separate quest progress. Do not alternate turn-ins or divide your carp between them; deliver every carp to the NPC whose quest you accepted.

Complete walkthrough

  1. Travel to Port San d’Oria (H-8).
    Find Joulet and Gallijaux together beside the water.
  2. Choose your brother.
    Speak to Joulet for The Competition or Gallijaux for The Rivalry. Both paths award the same rod.
  3. Obtain carp.
    Catch, purchase, or receive Moat Carp and Forest Carp. Either species contributes to the same 10,000-fish requirement for your chosen brother.
  4. Trade them to your chosen brother.
    You may submit individual fish or full stacks over as many visits as needed. You do not need to carry all 10,000 at once.
  5. Continue until his personal total reaches 10,000.
    The quest remembers accepted turn-ins. Speak to your brother between deliveries for progress-related dialogue; some server builds change dialogue without displaying a precise number.
  6. Collect the reward.
    When the 10,000th accepted carp is delivered, finish the dialogue and receive Lu Shang’s Fishing Rod.

Can a low-level character do it?

Yes. The quest itself has no job-level or combat requirement. A low-level character can enter Port San d’Oria, accept either brother’s quest, purchase or receive carp, make turn-ins, and earn the rod.

Fishing is the slower part—not quest access. Current Reality can apply fishing fatigue, daily catch limits, bite requirements, or anti-bot rules independently of the quest. Those rules affect how quickly you personally catch 10,000 fish; they do not prevent you from trading carp obtained through other legitimate sources.

Practical completion plan

  • Keep a written running total after each trip because the NPC may not give an exact number.
  • Use full stacks to reduce the number of trade windows.
  • Combine methods: fish your normal daily amount, purchase reasonably priced stacks, and accept carp from family or other players.
  • Verify the recipient before every trade—the brother’s name matters more than where the two NPCs are standing.
  • Do not discard the reward: Lu Shang’s is a long-term rod and can later be repaired through woodworking when damaged.

If the carp are not counting

  • Confirm that you accepted a quest from one brother before trading.
  • Return to the same brother who gave you the active quest.
  • Verify the fish is exactly Moat Carp or Forest Carp.
  • Do not trade Tricolored Carp, Gold Carp, or another fish with “carp” in its name.
  • If you previously supported the opposite brother, check both NPCs’ dialogue to identify which quest is active before submitting more fish.
  • Re-zone and speak to the selected brother if the completion dialogue does not appear after the final trade.
