Alchemist Party Helper

Automatic healing, cures, MP recovery and mixture buffs for you, party members and trusts.

Start and stop

Client update 1.0.19 includes cralcbot as a manually loaded helper. Run /addon load cralcbot after entering the game using your load macro or chat. It no longer loads at startup, and it always loads stopped. Use the Start button in CR Alchemist Helper or /cralcbot start when playing main-job Alchemist. Stop with its button or /cralcbot stop.

/addon reload cralcbot
/cralcbot start
/cralcbot stop

/cralcbot show and /cralcbot hide control its window. Hiding the window does not stop an active helper.

Drag the Move handle to reposition the transparent panel. Its controls now use 80×64 finger-sized buttons with extra spacing. Apart from the handle, the panel displays only Running or Stopped, with Start/Stop, Hide and X. Start changes to Stop while active. Both Hide and X close the panel while preserving the helper’s running state; use Stop to stop it, and /cralcbot show to reopen the panel. Explicitly loading or reloading also opens a previously closed panel; hidden settings no longer leave it invisible after a reload. /cralcbot status prints details only when requested: addon version, Running/Stopped, last action, received/requested party reports, rejected reports, last-reply age and Guard readiness. Start once to begin a rotation; restarting the helper begins a new rotation.

Party care

Start enables reactive support for you, other party members and summoned trusts. The helper reads their current effects and MP, then chooses a learned, ready mixture. As your main-job Alchemist level increases, higher-level support becomes available automatically.

Priority is critical healing (35% HP or below), waking sleepers, specific ailment cures, ordinary healing, MP recovery, sleep prevention and missing buffs. Both Max Potion versions can wake a sleeping ally even at full HP. Regular Max Potion is preferred; the mixed version is the fallback.

Targets must be alive, in your six-person party, in your zone and within range. Routine buffs rotate through ready mixture types and party slots, skipping allies who already have the relevant effect. Each buff remembers its last served slot so short effects do not keep pulling it back to the same person. Critical healing and cures interrupt this rotation. All actions use their normal independent cooldowns, level requirements and carried medicines; the existing Resourcefulness waiver applies. Medicines are carried rather than consumed. Cooldowns limit how quickly it can cover a full party.

Sleep prevention uses the engaged enemy's actual move or spell list and spell-level requirements. Sheep and mandragora are included. Recently observed sleep also triggers protection for that same ongoing fight. Special scripted sleep can still surprise it before the first occurrence.

Final Elixir, offensive actions and Resourcefulness remain manual. This is party support: it does not navigate, pull enemies or replace every combat decision.

Mana Shield upkeep

In update 1.0.18, cralcbot 1.6 also casts Mana Shield on you when it is missing and ready. This unlocks at Alchemist level 40 and requires the learned spell, positive MP, normal carried medicines or Resourcefulness, and an available recast. The server checks the actual effect rather than guessing from its icon. Healing, waking, cures and needed MP recovery take priority.

Mana Shield lasts 60 seconds with a three-minute recast, so the helper cannot maintain continuous coverage. It uses the existing mixture preparation and normal spell cast. The helper remains manually loaded and starts stopped.

This update also replaces the internal mixture prepare/cancel chat-command handoff that produced “A command error occurred” in the supplied PC screenshot. Internal control messages no longer execute as typed chat commands. Padded server acknowledgements and confirmed native spell completion prevent false preparation timeouts. Automated callback checks passed; live PC/ROG casting remains to be confirmed after updating.

During play

The helper pauses while you type, cast or use an item. It stops after zoning, job changes or death; start it again when ready. Stop also cancels a queued helper action that has not executed.

Update 1.0.17 fixes party-report reception using the actual 512-byte Ashita buffer captured from the receiving PC. Only the bounded message before padding is parsed. The earlier 1.0.16 limit handled the shorter native packet but still rejected the larger client buffer. The user confirmed that cralcbot 1.5 mixes again on the PC. All 15 support mixtures, 30 unique routine buff/party-slot pairs, wraparound, existing-effect/range/recast skips and emergency priorities passed automated Lua checks. PC mixing is user-confirmed; the receiving ROG client remains a beta check. Close the game and update through the launcher, then load the helper and press Start. No Map restart is needed.
