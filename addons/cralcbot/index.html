Alchemist Party Helper

Optional Max Potion and Guard Drink support for you, party members and trusts.

Start and stop

Client update 1.0.11 includes cralcbot. It loads stopped. Use the Start button in CR Alchemist Helper or /cralcbot start when playing main-job Alchemist. Stop with its button or /cralcbot stop.

/addon reload cralcbot
/cralcbot start
/cralcbot stop

/cralcbot show and /cralcbot hide control its window. Hiding the window does not stop an active helper.

What it uses

ActionPriority and condition
Max PotionHeal the lowest-HP eligible party member at 75% HP or below when available.
Mix: Max PotionFallback when regular Max Potion is cooling down and the mixed version is ready. Requires Alchemist level 30.
Mix: Guard DrinkAfter healing checks, fill missing Protect or Shell when ready. Requires level 20.

It targets you, the five other party slots and summoned trusts in those slots. Targets must be alive, in your zone and within range. It uses ordinary job-ability commands with the existing independent recasts, learning requirements, carried medicines and Resourcefulness waiver.

During play

The helper pauses while you type, cast or use an item. It stops after zoning, job changes or death; start it again when ready. Stop also cancels a queued helper action that has not executed.

The decision logic, party/trust filtering, both healing timers, missing buffs and stop behavior passed automated Lua checks. A live party session on the receiving client is still a beta check.
