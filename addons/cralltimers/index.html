CR All Timers

Spell and job-ability cooldown bars for every job.

Watch cooldowns

Client update 1.0.11 includes cralltimers and loads it at startup. Spell bars are blue; ability bars are gold. Each bar counts down and disappears when that cooldown is ready. The window appears when a timer is active and can be dragged to a comfortable position.

All jobs are supported, including Alchemist’s separate mixture timers. Abilities sharing a native recast group share one bar. Charged abilities show available charges while the remaining charges recover; an action can be usable before that recharge bar disappears.

Commands

CommandUse
/addon load cralltimersLoad if needed.
/addon reload cralltimersApply an installed code update.
/cralltimers showShow active timers.
/cralltimers hideHide the window.
/cralltimers lockKeep its position fixed.
/cralltimers unlockAllow dragging.
/cralltimers debugPrint current timer status.

When no bar appears

Cast a spell after loading the addon to start tracking its cooldown. Reloading clears spell timers already in progress. Chainspell and other effects that remove recast produce no spell bar; this also explains testing under GM God Mode’s permanent Chainspell.

These are cooldown bars. Casting progress and weapon-skill TP are separate displays; weapon skills do not have individual spell-style recasts.

The older launcher timer options now load this display through compatibility entries. The timer display avoids those addons’ native recast scans and unchecked signature reads. Ability and spell bars were confirmed in the H client; crash-free use on every receiving client remains a beta check.
