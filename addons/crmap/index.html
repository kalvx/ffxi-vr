CR Minimap Controls

A square, draggable map with controller zoom for the ROG Ally.

Start with your selected map

Client update 1.0.19 includes the controls. Keep Minimap selected in the launcher. The helper follows that selection and does not turn on another map when Boussole or Mappy is selected.

On the first Minimap launch, the helper backs up your map settings, selects the existing square theme, sets the frame to 4% opacity, uses a 248-pixel map with a wider 0.35 zoom, and places it inset from the lower-right corner. Later changes are retained. It briefly reloads only the Minimap plugin to apply this first preset.

Zoom and move

Hold X + A together, then move the left stick up to zoom in or down to zoom out. Keep holding to repeat; center the stick to stop. The combo consumes those buttons and left-stick movement while active. Ordinary X/A taps and holds are retained, with a brief 90 ms window to recognize the combo.

Drag inside the square map with touch or the mouse to move it. Placement is free rather than snapped to a border. Repeated zoom and dragging track the latest position immediately, including before native settings are saved. The native mouse wheel and /minimap settings remain available.

CommandUse
/crmap in / /crmap outOne zoom step.
/crmap cornerReturn the map to its inset lower-right position.
/crmap off / /crmap onDisable or enable the added controls for this session.
/minimapOpen the existing native map settings, including shape and frame opacity.

The controls use the game's XInput events. No additional Ally button assignment or background controller poller is required. Live Ally zoom and touch dragging remain receiving-device checks after updating.
