Client Addons

This information now lives on its own page—use the left navigation to move between subjects without crossing the full Compendium.

Dark Potion II and helper

Client update 1.0.23 adds the level-65 dark mixture, shortened mixture names and Chains support for Darkness closers and dark magic bursts. Finisher and burst instructions

CR Alchemist Helper 1.7 uses Dark Potion I on your engaged enemy after its existing support priorities are covered. Dark Potion II remains manual for timing.

Crafting follows the elements

Client update 1.0.22 adds safe/NQ and risky/HQ direction hints to crcrafting for your captured recipe, with server-confirmed facing, day and actual weather. Open the interactive compass and rules →

Close the game, apply launcher Update on PC or ROG, then reopen.

Square map, Ally zoom and Mana Shield

Client update 1.0.21 adds CR Minimap Controls: square shape, a barely visible border, free dragging and X+A plus left-stick zoom. Alchemist Helper gains missing Mana Shield upkeep, and mixture prepare/cancel messages use a corrected handoff.

Alchemist party care

Client update 1.0.17 fixes party-status reception using the actual Ashita buffer captured on PC and adds fair rotation across ready buffs and party slots. Alchemist Helper supports 15 friendly mixtures: healing, specific cures, MP recovery, missing buffs and sleep prevention for party members and trusts. Higher-level support becomes available as you level. Load it after login and press Start.

Chat and macro paste

CR Paste inserts a copied line into chat or a macro editor with Ctrl+V or a compact transparent pad. Client update 1.0.14 enlarges the buttons for finger taps, adds Move handles and makes CR Paste and Alchemist Helper manually loaded after login. Explicit load/reload reopens either panel. Enter and saving remain yours.

Current Reality timers and party helper

CR All Timers displays spell and ability cooldown bars for every job. Alchemist Party Helper offers optional healing and Guard Drink for your party and trusts. Both are included in client update 1.0.11.

CRCrafting, CRHotSwap & CRMM addon patch

October 9, 2026 · Adds the custom addons missing from earlier client builds: CRCrafting 0.2.0, CRHotSwap 1.2.0 and CRMM 1.2.1, including their helper files and macro scripts.

Download addon patch installer ↓Portable ZIPChecksums

Close the game and launcher, run the installer, and select your existing Current Reality folder containing Runtime. Existing addon files are backed up; personal settings and startup scripts are preserved. For the ZIP, back up existing addon files and extract into that same folder, merging Runtime.

Then load each addon in game:

/addon load crcrafting
/addon load crhotswap
/addon load crmm

CRMM is the Summoner combo helper and needs the matching Current Reality server support. This download installs client files only. Packaging is verified; an in-game check on the receiving PC is still needed.
